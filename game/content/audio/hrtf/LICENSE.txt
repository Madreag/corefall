M12B HRTF subset — attribution + licensing
==========================================

The production HRIR table at `mit_kemar_subset.bin` is derived from the
MIT KEMAR dataset (Bill Gardner, 1994).

    KEMAR HRTF measurements at 1.4 m
    Bill Gardner and Keith Martin
    MIT Media Lab Machine Listening Group, 1994

The MIT KEMAR dataset is in the **public domain** — see the original
release notes:

    https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html

The 32-azimuth × 8-elevation × 2-ear × 128-sample subset shipped here
is a downsampled, fixed-layout binary suitable for runtime O(1) lookup.
Layout is documented in `cf-audio/src/spatial.rs`.

Current state (2026-05-17): the binary in this directory is a M12B
placeholder generated by `tools/m12b_content/generate_hrtf_placeholder.py`
(single-tap impulse per bucket). The production MIT KEMAR subset
replaces it at M48B per spec § Out of scope: "Per-actor HRTF
personalization (head circumference / ear shape) — uses generic MIT
KEMAR. Per-player calibration deferred to M48B audio polish.".
