# M14J — Actor Advanced Mobility

## Status

`done`

## Intent

**M14J is the actor advanced-mobility milestone.** M14A shipped limb-driven walking + jetpack + mass aggregation; M16 shipped water tiles + the swim affliction; nothing has ever owned **how an actor scrambles up a wall, vaults a chest-high obstacle, climbs a ladder, ropes down a cliff, throws a grappling hook, swings across a chasm, slides down a zip line, mounts a horse / pet / critter, or actually moves through water with anything more than a placeholder `swim` flag.** M14J closes that gap and turns the actor's locomotion vocabulary from "walk + jet" into "walk + jet + parkour + climb + rope + swing + zip + ride + swim."

After M14J, the player presses jump near a chest-high crate and the actor vaults it without breaking stride; presses jump near a wall in mid-air and the actor wall-jumps off it, gaining a perpendicular kick; aims a grappling-hook gun at a ledge and fires, the hook embeds, the player presses interact and the actor rope-climbs hand-over-hand; swings the rope by pushing left/right at the top of the arc and releases at maximum amplitude to launch over a gap; climbs a ladder rung-by-rung with the M14A limb path system; clips onto a zip line at the high end and slides to the low end at gravity-driven speed with optional braking; mounts a horse / a domesticated lattice-pet / any saddled critter and rides at the critter's top speed while still able to fire a one-handed weapon; submerges into water and **actually swims** with limb-driven breast-stroke / freestyle / dive paths, with the M16 swim-stamina + drowning timer now reflecting real M14A mass aggregation.

M14J's player-facing promise: **"the actor goes where you point, not where the floor lets it. Walls climb. Ropes swing. Ladders rung. Zips slide. Critters ride. Water swims. The body knows nine ways to move, and you fluently chain them in two seconds."**

## Player-facing behavior

- Vault chest-high obstacles (≤1.2 m) at full run without speed loss: the M14A walking loop detects an obstacle in the swept path, plays a 200 ms `Vault` stance whose limb path lifts the body over the obstacle, and re-enters `Walking` on the far side; failed vault (obstacle too tall) = bonk + small stagger.
- Wall-jump off any vertical surface within 250 ms of contact while airborne: presses `jump` while sliding/falling along a wall trigger a perpendicular impulse equal to 70% jump-impulse + 100% reflected horizontal velocity; consumable up to 3 chained wall-jumps before the actor must touch ground (prevents infinite scaling).
- Fire a **grappling-hook gun** (T2 equipment, 20 steel + 5 motor + 10 rope; built at T2 fabricator) at any surface within 30 m; the hook embeds (1 tick), the rope deploys, the actor can either rope-climb hand-over-hand toward the anchor (0.8 m/s) or rappel away from the anchor (1.5 m/s) using `act.player.rope_input { climb: -1..1 }`.
- **Rope swing** when the actor is suspended below an embedded grappling hook: pressing left/right adds tangential force at the bob; releasing the rope at the apex of a swing launches the actor at the rope's instantaneous velocity (pendulum exit math, not teleport).
- Climb ladders, cables, vines, hanging ropes, and chain-link fences using a shared `Climbing` stance: M14A's limb path system swaps its `feet` group for `hands+feet` and the actor advances at 1 m/s vertical with no jetpack required; falling off is a button-press OR a stamina-exhaustion event.
- Clip onto a **zip line** (deployed by player attaching two grappling-hook anchors with a `zip_kit`, T2 equipment) at the high end; the actor slides to the low end at gravity-driven speed clamped to 12 m/s; pressing `interact` engages the brake (-3 m/s²); pressing `interact` while braked + at end = release.
- Mount a tamed M27 critter (horse / lattice-pet / saddled drone-quad / domesticated bovine-equivalent) via `act.player.mount { critter_id }`; the actor's stance becomes `Mounted`, the critter's M14A chassis carries both masses, the rider can fire one-handed weapons while moving; dismount via `act.player.dismount` is instantaneous when the critter is stationary, 1-tick stagger if mid-gallop.
- Refined swim — supersedes M16's `swim` placeholder: when the actor enters a water tile, the M14A walking loop swaps its limb paths for `breast_stroke` (surface horizontal) / `freestyle` (surface burst) / `dive` (vertical-down) / `tread` (idle keep-head-above); stroke rate consumes M16 swim-stamina at race-aware rates (Aqueous origin = -50% drain, Robotic origin can't swim = sinks); helmet seal + M19C dive-suit shell removes the `drowning` affliction the swim-stamina rule would otherwise produce.
- Chain all of the above fluidly: `Walking → Vault → Wall-Jump → mid-air Grapple-Fire → Rope-Climb → swing-Release → Zip-Line → Brake → Dismount-onto-mounted-horse` is one continuous player input string with no stance lockouts beyond the 200 ms transition each step takes.
- Quick-action wheel (M14A) gets four new pie slices: `[Vault] [Grapple] [Mount/Dismount] [Zip-Brake]` — context-sensitive (only visible when the situation supports them).

## Notes for implementer

- The vault-detect helper in `cf-actor::parkour` is just one swept-volume query per tick at chest height in the actor's facing direction; cache the result against a small `parkour_signal` struct so the limb path can dispatch off it without re-querying.
- Wall-jump-detect is the same swept query but at the actor's bounding box mid-line, perpendicular to current motion; the 250 ms grace window after wall-contact is a stance-timer field, not a polled raycast.
- Verlet rope (8 segments default) is a standard 2-pass solver: integrate positions, then 4 iterations of distance-constraint relaxation between adjacent segment endpoints. Each end is either anchored (embedded hook) or attached to an actor's hand-bone point. Don't over-engineer — 8 segments handles 30 m rope with no visible jitter at 60 Hz.
- Mounted-pairing combined mass: rider.mass + critter.mass → critter chassis aggregates both for M14A speed curves and jetpack-vs-mass math. The critter loses ~20% top speed when carrying a human-mass rider; tune in content RON.
- Mount control multiplexing: rider provides `ride_direction` (dx, dy as input to critter's locomotion goal); critter AI handles gait selection (walk / trot / canter / gallop) via its own M14A limb paths. Rider weapon inputs go to rider's own arm sim — independent of critter motion.
- Swim limb paths should reuse M14A's existing `LimbPath` data type and waypoint format — no new path schema needed. Each stroke kind (breast / freestyle / dive / tread) is one path with the same calibration constants as walk paths.
- The race-aware swim rates (Aqueous, Robotic, Human, Crystalline) should pull from M17's per-origin reaction table; M14J only adds a `swim_stamina_rate_multiplier` column to it.
- Auto-vault MUST be conservative — false-positive vaults (e.g., the actor accidentally vaults over a low ally instead of stepping around) ruin the feel. Tune the swept-volume forward distance to 0.4-0.5 m so the actor commits only when collision is inevitable.
- Wall-jump impulse magnitudes should be tuned against M14A's `JUMP_IMPULSE` constant, not re-derived. Anchor everything to existing M14A calibration.
- Replay event surface: 11 new event schemas, all minimal `{ actor_id, tick, ... }` shape per M5 conventions. Don't bake game-state into them.
- Quick-action wheel additions (M14A QAB) — register the four new context-sensitive slices via the M14A `qab.register` API; don't fork the QAB renderer.
- Storyteller hooks: `actor_drowned`, `actor_dismounted` (mid-gallop), and `grapple_embedded` (long-distance shot ≥ 25 m) register as M25 story-grade events for narrative bullets.
