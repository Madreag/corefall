# M12C — In-Engine Cinematic Cutscenes + Opening / Between-Mission / Ending Cinematics + Camera Cinematography

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Closes the gap M12 left open between **static painted slideshow** (8 painted CCCP-style intro slides) and **dramatic in-engine moments**: M12C ships a **scripted in-engine cinematic playback system** that takes control of the live camera + actors + lighting + audio mixer to play **mission-opening cinematics (30-60s)**, **between-mission cinematics (15-30s)**, and a **campaign-ending cinematic (2-5min)** using the actual game world — distinct from comic panels (M12), painted slideshows (M12), and dialog popups (M26B). Cassandra opens with dread, Phoebe with quirk, Randy with chaos, Ironman with a challenge, Sandbox skips entirely; the camera dolly-zooms on the dropship door as the squad disembarks; the voice-over and subtitles arrive on M4 chapter markers; the player can skip, pause, and replay any cutscene from the codex.

M12C promise: **"every mission opens with a cinematic the player remembers — actors move, the camera moves, the voice-over hits — and every campaign ends with a 5-minute set piece that earns its place."**

## Player-facing behavior

### Mission-opening cinematic (30-60s)

Plays automatically when a mission scenario boots (after M9A asset bake, before player input):

- **Camera takes over** — the gameplay camera dolly-pans across the mission's named landmarks: dropship doorway → squad silhouettes → mission objective POI → distant boss / hazard / faction emblem. Player input is blocked except `act.player.skip_cinematic` and `act.player.pause_cinematic`.
- **Live actors play scripted poses** — squad members enter their pre-mission stance (chassis idle + weapon at low-ready + storyteller-specific body language); the camera frames them in rule-of-thirds with the M12 vivid palette + faction-coherent rim lighting.
- **Storyteller voice-over (M12A/M37A) plays over the scene** — Cassandra murmurs dread, Phoebe offers a quirky aside, Randy laughs, Ironman issues a challenge. Subtitle ribbon overlays via `cf-ui::caption_ribbon`.
- **Mission-briefing card** — at T+15s, a 6-line briefing fades in over the lower third (objective + reward + risk + storyteller stinger); auto-dismisses at cinematic end.
- **Per-mission script** — 30+ launch missions each have a `<mission_id>.cinematic.ron` script under `content/cinematics/opening/`; the per-storyteller stinger picks a variant from `content/cinematics/opening_stingers/<storyteller_id>.ron`.
- **Skippable** — pressing `[Esc]` or `[Space]` at any moment fires `cinematic.skipped { id, skipped_at_ms }` and snaps to gameplay; first-time-seen cinematics get a 3-second skip-confirm window per DR-018 (tutorial safety).

### Between-mission cinematic (15-30s)

Plays in the base scene between two mission departures:

- **Camera dolly across the base** — sweeps the command core, then settles on the storyteller (or the AI commander rival per M25) delivering a 10-15s monologue.
- **Storyteller-bound** — Cassandra delivers a dread monologue ("the next contract is heavier than the last"), Phoebe a quirky one ("there's a tea-stain shaped like Phobos on my chart"), Randy chaotic ("HA — coin says south"), Ironman a challenge ("you survived. now earn the next one"), Sandbox NONE (skipped; instant transition).
- **AI-commander rival interjection (M25)** — if the player's persistent rival is alive, 40% chance the rival's faction-channel taunt plays over a static portrait card with comm-static SFX, costing zero gameplay time (M4 chapter marker `cinematic.rival_taunt`).
- **Auto-plays once** per campaign-day; second visit to base before mission select skips directly to UI.

### Campaign-ending cinematic (2-5min)

Plays exactly once when M49's launch BP12 ends (final mission win or campaign-arc resolution; this milestone wires the **playback system** — the per-arc content is baked at M49):

- **3-act structure** — Act 1 (denouement, 60-90s, in-engine base/world sweep) → Act 2 (montage of pivotal player decisions replayed from save journal, 60-120s) → Act 3 (resolution painted slideshow, 30-60s, returning to CCCP-style for the bookend per M12).
- **5 storyteller-specific finales** — Cassandra's reads as elegy; Phoebe's as quiet hope; Randy's as a cackling shrug; Ironman's as a salute; Sandbox skips Acts 1-2 and runs only Act 3 painted slides.
- **Per-faction ending variants** — final faction relationships (M26) gate 8 alternate Act 1 sequences; allied factions wave from their bases, hostile factions burn theirs.
- **Skippable; replayable from codex** — `Codex → Cinematics → Campaign Ending` after the player has earned it.

### Per-storyteller cinematic style

Modeled on Rimworld's storyteller pacing, shipped as **per-storyteller cinematic profiles** that bias camera + audio + color grade + pacing without authoring 5× the content:

| Storyteller | Camera bias | Audio bias | Color grade bias | Pacing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cassandra Classic** | Slow dolly + low-angle dread shots | Deep cello bed + sparse impacts | Cool desaturated tint (-15% sat, -8% value) | Long beats, 15-25s per shot |
| **Phoebe Chillax** | Steady medium shots + soft handheld | Warm strings + light piano | Warm saturated tint (+10% sat, +5% value) | Even beats, 8-15s per shot |
| **Randy Random** | Whip-pans + dutch tilt + cut-on-action | Percussive synth + horn stabs | Punchy contrast (+20% contrast) | Snappy 3-8s per shot |
| **Ironman** | Locked-off heroic angles + slow zoom | Marching drum + brass swells | Stark high-key + hard shadows | Deliberate 10-18s per shot |
| **Sandbox** | Plain establishing wide; no acting | Ambient bed only; no music score | Default neutral grade | Cinematic suppressed entirely (drop to UI) |

Profiles in `content/cinematics/storyteller_profiles/<id>.ron`. The cinematic player reads the active storyteller from M25 director state and applies its profile globally.

### Cinematic camera moves

Five named camera primitives, composable into a per-shot script:

- **Pan** — straight 2D translation across the scene at constant velocity; eased per `EaseInOutCubic` by default.
- **Dolly** — perpendicular zoom-and-translate ("push in" / "pull out"); preserves the screen-space anchor on a named target.
- **Zoom** — pure focal-length / orthographic-half-height change without translation.
- **Orbit** — circular arc around a named target at fixed radius (2D side-view: parallax-shift across foreground/midground/background layers).
- **Shake** — perlin-noise additive offset, parameterized by amplitude_px + frequency_hz + decay_s; reuses M12's `cf-render-2d::juice::camera_shake_amplitude` curve.

Each move declares `{ kind, duration_ms, target, easing, params }` in the script. The player composes per-shot move-stacks (e.g., dolly + shake on a dropship landing). Replay-deterministic: pure functions of script + tick, no `thread_rng()`.

### Skip / pause / replay UX

- **Skip** — `[Esc]` or `[Space]` (configurable). Fires `cinematic.skipped` + jumps directly to next gameplay tick. Skip is disabled in the **first 3 seconds** of any never-before-seen cinematic (anti-fat-finger; tracked per-cinematic in `save.cinematic_seen_set`).
- **Pause** — `[P]`. Pauses the cinematic clock; camera frozen; voice-over paused at boundary; subtitle frozen. Resume with `[P]` again.
- **Replay** — any cinematic the player has watched is unlocked in `Codex → Cinematics`. Replay runs the script identically (replay-deterministic) without affecting save state. Voice-over + captions identical to first viewing.
- **Captions-on contract** — the M12A `caption_visible` predicate gates the subtitle ribbon; never silent without captions when `captions_enabled=true`.

### ElevenLabs narration sync

- **Per-cinematic narration track** — each cinematic ships one ElevenLabs `eleven_v3`-baked WAV (per-storyteller voice from M37A: cassandra_narrator_balanced_female, phoebe_chillax_warm_female, randy_random_chaotic_male, ironman_stoic_male, sandbox_neutral_narrator).
- **Word-level timestamps** — the bake emits `cinematic.narration_track.json` alongside the WAV with per-word `start_ms` + `end_ms`; the cinematic player uses these to drive caption ribbon highlighting + chapter markers.
- **Lip-sync (out of scope; M37A)** — M12C wires the timestamp track + caption ribbon. Visible mouth-sync of speaking-actor portraits ships at M37A.
- **Loudness contract** — narration mixed at -14 LUFS; ambient music ducked to -22 LUFS during narration windows; SFX held at -16 LUFS (per M12A audio mix).

### Chapter markers via M4 events

Each cinematic emits cf-replay events at named beats so M4 replay can scrub + the death-recap (M12 view-as-graphic-novel) can stitch cinematic stills into the recap timeline:

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| `cinematic.started { id, source: opening\|between\|ending }` | First tick of playback |
| `cinematic.chapter_marker { id, chapter_id, ms }` | Each named chapter beat (e.g., "dropship_door_opens", "boss_silhouette_reveal", "rival_taunt") |
| `cinematic.skipped { id, skipped_at_ms }` | Player skip |
| `cinematic.paused / cinematic.resumed { id, ms }` | Pause toggle |
| `cinematic.ended { id, duration_ms, was_skipped: bool }` | Last tick |
| `cinematic.narration_word { id, word_index, text }` | Per-word caption beat (emitted only when captions on) |

Chapter markers are author-defined in the script (`chapters: [{ id, at_ms }]`); the player + cinematic kernel auto-emits them when the playhead crosses each marker.

### Codex replay surface

- New codex tab `Cinematics` under `Codex → Cinematics`, three subgroups: **Mission Openings** (30+) / **Between-Mission Beats** (5 per storyteller × variations) / **Campaign Endings** (5 per storyteller).
- Locked entries show silhouette + "watch the mission to unlock"; unlocked entries are clickable for full replay.
- Per-cinematic metadata: `duration`, `storyteller`, `first_seen_at`, `mission_id?`.

## Out of scope

- Lip-sync of speaking-actor portraits (M37A — voice-driven mouth animation)
- Tier 2 cinematic audio production polish (M48A — audio polish pass)
- Per-cinematic motion-captured pose data (never — actors use M9A animation frames + scripted pose IDs)
- Real-time ray-traced lighting overrides (M48 visual polish if ever)
- Per-language narration bakes (M40B localization)
- Real 3D camera moves (this is a 2D side-view game; orbit is parallax-layer-shift)
- Modder-authored cinematics via in-game editor (modders edit RON files; in-game editor is a future polish item)
- Inline branching cinematics (cinematic plays one path per session; dialog branching is M26B)

## Notes for the implementer

- The cinematic camera REPLACES the gameplay camera at the render layer (`cf-render-2d::camera_takeover`); do NOT teleport the gameplay-owned camera transform — the camera stack is a separate optional resource that the renderer reads first when present. This keeps mid-cinematic save-state restorable.
- Shake noise MUST seed off the engine's deterministic RNG, not `thread_rng()` (per AGENTS.md sim-crate rule). The shake primitive accepts a `seed: u64` parameter; the kernel passes the M4 replay seed + per-shot index.
- Skip-confirm window is 3000 ms; the value is a constant in `cf-cinematic::skip_pause_replay` and NOT a hardcoded `60` tick assumption — it converts via the active tick rate.
- The narration track JSON schema is `{ word: string, start_ms: u32, end_ms: u32 }[]`. Empty array = no caption highlighting; cinematic still plays.
- Between-mission cinematics SHOULD use already-loaded base-scene actors; do NOT spawn new entities for cinematic-only purposes (perf budget: Steam Deck floor, 60 fps).
- Mission-opening cinematic boot path: `cf-shell::cinematic_hooks::on_mission_load` reads `<mission_id>.cinematic.ron` if it exists; if missing, skips silently — never block mission boot on a missing cinematic file.
- Codex unlock state lives in `save.cinematic_seen_set: HashSet<CinematicId>`; persisted via M41 save format.
- The "Sandbox suppresses cinematics" rule is implemented by the storyteller profile, not by the cinematic kernel; this keeps the suppress logic data-driven and lets mods author their own suppress-cinematics storytellers.
