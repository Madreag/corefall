# M14E — Per-Pixel Structural Integrity Field + Tunnel Collapse

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Per-chunk structural-integrity field that detects unsupported overhead spans + triggers cave-in physics when a tunnel ceiling exceeds the unsupported-span threshold — closes the M3.md:349 carry-over and ships Cortex Command's defining "hand-dug tunnels collapse if unsupported" mechanic.

## Player-facing behavior

- Dig a horizontal tunnel into a hillside; ceiling holds for ~16 unsupported pixels.
- Extend the tunnel further without placing support_beam → ceiling chunks visibly crack (L1/L2/L3 decals) → randomized collapse roll fires → terrain.cave_in_triggered + falling-debris damage to anyone underneath.
- Place a support_beam (T1 craftable: 2 iron + 1 wood) under the unsupported span → integrity field locks the local area against collapse.
- Mining tools that dig faster (M30B plasma cutter / mining laser) make collapses more likely if no reinforcement placed (vibration accumulates).
- Reactor breach (M9) that damages a load-bearing wall → cascading cave-in across multiple chunks (Stationeers parity).
- Underground bunkers (M28 base build) require support beams in their structural template; demolishing a beam mid-base = visible cave-in warning.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Tunnel under 16 px span holds without support
  Given an actor digs a horizontal tunnel 14 pixels wide into a hillside
  When 300 ticks elapse with no reinforcement
  Then no cave-in triggers
  And integrity_field shows the ceiling at integrity ≥ 200 (locked)

Scenario: Tunnel over 16 px span starts cracking + warns
  Given an actor digs a horizontal tunnel 24 pixels wide
  When 30 ticks elapse
  Then terrain.structural_integrity_low fires for the affected chunk
  And L1 crack decal renders on the ceiling
  And audio plays tunnel_creak cue (one-shot)
  And HUD banner "STRUCTURAL WARNING — ceiling unstable" displays

Scenario: Unsupported tunnel collapses after escalating warnings
  Given a 32-pixel-wide tunnel with no support_beam
  When 600 ticks elapse (L1 → L2 → L3 crack decals progress)
  Then terrain.cave_in_triggered fires with chunk_id + bbox + falling_debris_count
  And falling-debris pixels spawn from ceiling to floor
  And any actor underneath receives fall-impulse damage per M14 fall_impulse_chain
  And the tunnel ceiling is permanently lowered (terrain mutated)
  And audio plays cave_in_thunder cue

Scenario: Support beam prevents collapse
  Given a 32-pixel-wide tunnel
  And an actor places a support_beam at the center span (cost 2 iron + 1 wood)
  When 600 ticks elapse
  Then NO cave-in triggers
  And integrity_field locks the ±8 pixels around the beam to integrity 500
  And terrain.support_beam_placed event fires at placement

Scenario: Demolishing a load-bearing beam mid-base cascades cave-in
  Given a base with a 48-pixel-wide tunnel supported by 2 beams
  When the player demolishes one beam (cf-equipment demolish tool)
  Then terrain.support_beam_destroyed fires
  And integrity_field re-computes
  And terrain.structural_integrity_low fires within 5 ticks
  And cave-in cascade may follow within 60 ticks

Scenario: Plasma cutter speeds up dig + raises collapse risk
  Given an actor dual-wields a plasma cutter (M30B; 4× dig speed) on an unsupported tunnel
  When the same 32-pixel span is dug 4× faster
  Then vibration_accumulator increases by 2× normal
  And cave-in roll happens at 200 ticks instead of 600 ticks
  And player is warned "VIBRATION ACCUMULATING" via HUD

Scenario: Per-tick collapse-check perf budget
  Given 500 actively-dug chunks in a scene
  When the per-tick (every 15 ticks) integrity pass runs
  Then total compute time ≤ 0.4 ms (p99)
  And cave-in roll determinism is byte-identical across seeded runs

Scenario: Cascade collapse across neighbor chunks
  Given a 48-pixel tunnel spanning 3 chunks
  When the middle chunk cave-in triggers
  Then terrain.cave_in_triggered fires for the center
  And within 15 ticks the neighbor chunks recompute integrity
  And if their integrity drops, secondary cave-ins fire (Stationeers cascade)
```

## Out of scope

- Lateral wall collapse (only ceiling cave-in is in M14E; sidewall collapse is M14F+).
- Per-pixel rebar / mesh embedded in concrete that increases unsupported-span budget — M48+ realism.
- Earthquake-triggered base-wide cave-ins — M16 hazard category, owned by M31 weather + M19 atmospherics if relevant.
- AI doctrine that respects structural integrity — M22 / M24 picks this up post-M14E (only basic miner AI "place beam before deep dig" included via AI-MINE-A-09).

## Notes for the implementer

- Integrity field update is **expensive** — `compute_integrity_pass` must run on a deferred per-N-tick cadence (default N=15) and only on chunks that received `terrain.terrain_dirty_region_batch` events in the last N ticks.
- Cave-in roll uses seeded RNG (engine's deterministic RNG, NOT thread_rng). Roll formula: `cave_in_chance_per_tick = (unsupported_span_px - 16) × 0.0001 × vibration_modifier`.
- Falling-debris pixel count = roughly `min(span_px × ceiling_thickness, 200)` to bound cosmetic load.
- AI-MINE-A-09 (per Digging audit) extends the AI miner doctrine to place a beam if `unsupported_span > 12 px` during deep-dig — M14E ships the support_beam tool + integrity field; M22/AI-MINE-A wires the AI consumer.
- The collapse-cascade-to-neighbor-chunks pattern is identical to terrain.terrain_cascade (M18 sabotage scenario) — reuse that event family for visual + audio continuity.
