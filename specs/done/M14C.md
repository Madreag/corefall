# M14C — Tank-Grade Rounds (HEAT + APFSDS Producers + Content)

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Promote M14's HEAT + APFSDS forward-compat helpers to full producers + ship at least one HEAT-capable launcher + one APFSDS-capable chassis weapon so M41's polished kill cam has live death-type variants instead of forward-compat stubs.

## Player-facing behavior

- HEAT (High-Explosive Anti-Tank) round: shaped-charge plasma jet penetrates module armor in a narrow cone; bypasses spaced armor; can pre-detonate against ERA (explosive reactive armor) blocks.
- APFSDS (Armor-Piercing Fin-Stabilized Discarding-Sabot) round: long-rod kinetic penetrator; depleted-uranium baseline; behaves as a hypervelocity dart that punches through chassis modules sequentially per M14's `traverse_ray`.
- Kill cam (M41) shows HEAT plasma jet trail piercing the chassis with spalling debris cone; shows APFSDS as a glowing rod path with module-by-module energy decay.
- Heavy Trooper (M14A) is HARD to kill with rifles but a single APFSDS round through the torso = instant penetration + crew KO.
- Mech-on-mech combat (M44/M41) uses APFSDS as the standard kinetic round + HEAT for soft-skin module breaching.

## Out of scope

- Reactive armor that self-replenishes — M34 Material Lab mod content.
- Smart submunitions — M48+ endgame.
- Per-round price + production chain — handled by M32 crafting + M30 mining.

## Notes for the implementer

- HEAT damage = shaped-charge jet velocity × charge mass; NOT raw KE. Magic numbers: 10 MJ jet @ ~3 km/s. The penetration cone is narrow (5° half-angle); off-axis hit = glance not penetration.
- APFSDS sabot discards in flight; the long-rod hits alone. Magic numbers: 7 kg rod @ 1600 m/s = 9.0 MJ KE; rod aspect ratio ~30:1 (Stationeers/War Thunder reference); per-module energy decay = `KE_in × (1 - module.absorption_ratio)`.
- ERA pre-detonation reduces HEAT effective penetration by `era_charge_kg × 0.7`. ERA itself is M14C content; one-shot module.
- Existing M14 forward-compat helpers (`heat_jet_modules_penetrated`, `era_pre_detonates_heat`, `apfsds_energy_through_module`) are mathematically complete — M14C only needs producer wiring + content authoring.
