# M16C — Mental Health Conditions + PTSD + Addiction + Trauma + Therapy + Pain Affliction

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Promote M19H's single stress accumulator + breakdown event into a **full mental-health system**: a discrete **Pain affliction**, **8 mental-health conditions** (PTSD, Anxiety Disorder, Depression, Addiction, Withdrawal, Insomnia, Panic Disorder, Acute Stress Reaction), per-condition triggers (combat trauma, witness-death, sleep_dep ladder, drug-use), per-condition treatment (therapy NPC at M26, medication, environment), and trait persistence across veterans.

## Canonical ownership

Canonical owner of mental health, Pain, addiction lifecycle, and trauma persistence. M19H stress accumulator feeds the **trigger** layer. M14G wound severity feeds Pain. M16B mental_illness disease entry shares lifecycle FSM with M16C conditions.

## Player-facing behavior

- **Pain affliction (new in M16's 22-affliction roster; M16C promotes to 23 + 7 chronic = 30).** Pain stacks per active wound × severity × `pain_contribution_per_severity` (M14G). Stack value drives:
  - Aim wobble multiplier.
  - Move speed × (1 − pain_pct × 0.3).
  - Morale −1 / tick when Pain > severe.
  - Auto-triage trigger when Pain > 80%.
- **8 launch mental-health conditions.**

| Condition | Triggers | Symptoms | Treatment | Persistence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PTSD** | Witness 3+ deaths in 60s OR survive Critical wound OR Concussion at KO | Random panic-attack spikes; reduced morale ceiling; insomnia comorbid; flashback aim-freeze | Therapy NPC sessions (M26) + medication (SSRI-equivalent) | After cure: 30% chronic / 70% in-remission with relapse risk |
| **Anxiety Disorder** | Sustained stress > 60 for 7 days | Sustained −10% accuracy + caffeine-craving thirst boost | Therapy + benzo-equivalent + meditation room (M19H decor) | High remission rate |
| **Depression** | Sustained stress > 50 for 30 days OR loss-of-squadmate-with-relationship | Move speed × 0.85; refuses non-essential orders; suicide risk if untreated | Therapy + SSRI + light therapy room | Long-tail; can recur |
| **Addiction** | 7+ doses of any combat stim within 30 days | Withdrawal on absence; pursue dose; bypass-cost reduces accuracy | Medication-assisted withdrawal + therapy + abstinence period | Permanent vulnerability trait |
| **Withdrawal** | Addiction trait + drug absent > N hours | Aim-shake (2× wobble); HP drain on tick; rage outbursts | Tapering schedule + symptom medication | Resolves over 2 weeks |
| **Insomnia** | Sustained stress + Pain > moderate + combat anxiety | Cannot fall asleep even when bedded; sleep_dep escalates without rest | Sleep medication + reduced stress + dark room | Resolves with cause removal |
| **Panic Disorder** | Single panic-attack threshold cross OR Anxiety chronic | Acute spikes: brief paralysis + freeze for 3–8s | Therapy + benzo + breathing exercise (M26 NPC interaction) | Treatable |
| **Acute Stress Reaction** | Imminent combat-trauma trigger before 30-day window for PTSD | Short-window (24h–7d) symptoms similar to PTSD | Rest + debrief NPC | Resolves if not converted to PTSD |

- **Lifecycle FSM:** `Triggered → Acute → (Subacute | Chronic) → (Remission | Refractory)`. Shared with M16B mental_illness disease entry.
- **Therapy NPC (M26 integration):** dedicated therapy session takes 30 in-game minutes; per-session efficacy roll; multi-session course needed.
- **Pharmacological treatment registry:**
  - SSRI-equivalent (depression / anxiety / PTSD adjunct)
  - Benzo-equivalent (anxiety / panic / sleep)
  - SNRI-equivalent (depression-resistant)
  - Opioid-equivalent (severe pain; addiction risk)
  - Stimulant-equivalent (combat boost; addiction risk)
  - Anti-psychotic (severe PTSD with psychotic features)
  - Sleep aid (insomnia)
  - Withdrawal-assist (methadone-equivalent for opioid; clonidine-equivalent for stim)

- **Comorbidity matrix.** PTSD + Depression common; Addiction + Withdrawal mandatory pair; Insomnia comorbid with most. M16C tracks comorbid pairs for treatment-plan adjustment.

- **Persistence + traits (M14I consumer).** Recovered → trait `recovered_from_<condition>`; chronic → trait `chronic_<condition>`; refractory → trait `refractory_<condition>`. Traits modify M14I scar-record + M41 veteran dossier.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Pain stack drives aim wobble
  Given an actor with 3 wounds: LacerationModerate(0.5), Burn2nd(0.6), FractureSimple(0.4)
  When the per-tick pain pass runs
  Then pain.stack_changed fires with new_stack=18
  And the M16 affliction.pain shows severity=0.6
  And aim wobble multiplier rises to 1.6×

Scenario: PTSD triggers from squad wipe
  Given an actor with 3 squadmates
  When 3 squadmate deaths fire within 60s in the actor's witness radius
  Then psych.condition_triggered fires with condition=PTSD
  And the lifecycle enters Acute stage

Scenario: Addiction develops from sustained stim use
  Given an actor takes combat_stim_t1 once per day
  When 7 doses elapse within 30 days
  Then psych.addiction_developed fires
  When dose absent for > 12h:
    Then psych.withdrawal_started fires
    And aim wobble × 2.0

Scenario: Therapy session contributes toward remission
  Given an actor with Acute PTSD + Therapy NPC available
  When 10 sessions complete + 14d on SSRI:
    Then psych.remission_achieved fires
    And the actor gains trait="recovered_from_ptsd"

Scenario: Panic attack freezes actor for 3-8s
  Given an actor with Panic Disorder
  When the per-tick panic-roll triggers
  Then psych.panic_attack fires
  And actor.stance = Stance::PanickedFreeze for duration ∈ [3s, 8s]

Scenario: Insomnia prevents sleep
  Given an actor with Insomnia + Pain > moderate
  When the actor enters a bed
  Then act.player.sleep is rejected with reason="insomnia"

Scenario: Mental health persists into veteran dossier
  Given an actor with chronic_depression trait
  When the mission ends + the actor enters M41 veteran roster
  Then the M48C pilot dossier shows the trait

Scenario: Per-origin mental-health filter
  Given a robot actor
  When PTSD-eligible event fires for the robot
  Then psych.condition_triggered does NOT fire
  And the robot's mental_health state remains empty

Scenario: Determinism — same seed reproduces panic-attack timing
  Given identical seed + identical scenario
  When 1 in-game day elapses
  Then psych.panic_attack events fire at identical ticks
```

- Comorbidity matrix lives in `content/psych_conditions/_comorbidity.ron`.
- Therapy efficacy roll uses seeded RNG; same seed reproduces session outcomes.
