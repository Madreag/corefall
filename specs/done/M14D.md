# M14D — Projectile-Projectile CCD

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Selective projectile-vs-projectile continuous collision detection — fulfills DR-033's commitment "Projectile <-> projectile collide selectively" with kinetic deflection / fragmentation for energy rounds and fuze-trigger / detonation for explosive rounds.

## Player-facing behavior

- Two rifle rounds crossing in flight visibly deflect off each other (rare but possible at high actor density).
- A frag grenade hit by a tracer round can be triggered mid-flight (rare but rewarding when it happens).
- Anti-projectile defense lasers (M48+ Active Protection Systems) work by intercepting incoming HEAT/APFSDS via this kernel.
- C-RAM (Counter-Rocket Artillery Mortar) installations defend bases by shooting down incoming projectiles per this contract.
- Energy round vs energy round at correct intercept angle = mutual cancellation (visible spark + dual disappear).

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Kinetic round hits explosive round → fuze trigger
  Given a frag grenade in flight at [100, 50] with velocity [+5, +2]
  And a rifle bullet in flight at [105, 55] with velocity [-5, -2]
  When the swept paths intersect at TOI 0.3
  Then collision.projectile_pair_contact fires with outcome="fuze_triggered"
  And the grenade detonates at the intercept point
  And the rifle bullet fragments (consumed in the energy transfer)

Scenario: Two kinetic rounds cross → deflect + energy loss
  Given two rifle rounds in flight with intersecting paths at angle ≥ 30°
  When the swept paths intersect
  Then both rounds deflect away from their original vectors
  And each round loses 40% of remaining kinetic energy
  And visible sparks render at the intercept point
  And neither round reaches its original target

Scenario: Glancing kinetic intercept does NOT collide
  Given two rifle rounds with paths converging at angle < 10°
  When the swept paths intersect
  Then collision.projectile_pair_contact does NOT fire
  And both rounds continue undisturbed (selective filter rejects shallow angles)

Scenario: Anti-projectile laser intercepts incoming HEAT round
  Given a base with C-RAM module + incoming RPG (HEAT) at 80 m
  When the C-RAM fires a tracking laser pulse
  Then collision.projectile_pair_contact fires with outcome="aps_intercept"
  And the HEAT round detonates harmlessly at intercept point
  And the C-RAM cooldown begins

Scenario: Performance — 50 projectiles in flight, < 0.3ms broadphase
  Given 50 active projectiles in a 1024×1024 chunked scene
  When the per-tick projectile-pair pass runs
  Then broadphase completes in < 0.3 ms (p99)
  And only ≤ 12 candidate pairs reach narrowphase (spatial-hash culling)
  And total pair-CCD pass fits within 0.5 ms of the M14 collision budget

Scenario: Determinism across same seed
  Given two engines with identical 30-projectile salvo input
  When 600 ticks of swept collision + projectile-pair pass run
  Then identical collision.projectile_pair_contact event stream
  And identical SaveBlob.checksum at tick 600
```

## Out of scope

- Beam-vs-beam intercept (energy weapons vs energy weapons) — M48+ exotic combat.
- Projectile-vs-debris CCD (M14 already handles debris via terrain-pixel collision).
- Anti-projectile reactive armor surfaces beyond ERA (M14C owns ERA).
- Smart-fuzed rounds that detect and avoid intercepts — M48+ AI projectiles.

## Dependencies

- M14 swept collision (done): `prioritize_swept_collisions` is the base
- M14C HEAT/APFSDS (active): some intercept scenarios use these round kinds
- M48 endgame: Active Protection Systems consume this kernel

## Notes for the implementer

- Selective filter: only project-pair-test when `(projectile_a.kind, projectile_b.kind)` is in `INTERESTING_PAIRS` allowlist. Pure ballistic-vs-ballistic at shallow angles is filtered out (perf + game-feel).
- Spatial hash bucket size = 32 px (matches average projectile sweep length per tick at 60Hz).
- Per-pair TOI computation is the same minkowski-difference swept algorithm M14 uses for projectile-vs-actor; just symmetric on both sides.
- Sparks are cosmetic — `cosmetic: true` flag on the event payload; renderer can drop under backpressure.
- The kill cam should NOT replay projectile-intercept moments by default (cosmetic noise); player can opt-in via settings.
