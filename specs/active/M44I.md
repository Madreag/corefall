# M44I — Helicopter vs Fighter Cross-Arm Air Combat + Mixed-Altitude Engagement Rules

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M44I is the cross-arm air-combat milestone** — the missing cross-spec pass that lets a helicopter (M44G) and a fixed-wing aircraft (M44H) actually fight each other. M44G's `out of scope` explicitly defers "Helicopter-versus-fighter-jet dogfighting (cross-spec pass after M44H)"; M44H never picks it back up. M44I closes that loop.

After M44I, players can stage a high-stakes asymmetric engagement: a low-altitude attack helicopter nap-of-earth crawl vs a high-altitude fighter sweep; the fighter's radar paints the helicopter (M44H AESA pulse-doppler) but the helicopter's terrain-mask + low IR signature complicates the lock; the helicopter pops up to fire a Stinger-class AGM-defense missile while the fighter dives for a guns pass; the fighter's gun-camera kill-cam (M41 polished) shows the cross-arm kill from the fighter's perspective; or — the helicopter wins the merge because the fighter overshot and the helicopter pivoted in place.

M44I promise: **"helicopters and fighters fight differently — and they fight each other. The fighter is faster + higher + has missiles. The helicopter is slower + lower + can hide behind trees and pivot. Cross-arm engagements have authored rules: altitude bands, radar masking, missile envelopes, evasion patterns, and a kill-cam that reads correctly from both perspectives."**

## Canonical ownership

This spec is the **canonical owner of cross-arm air-combat rules** between rotary-wing (M44G) and fixed-wing (M44H) platforms. Per-platform flight physics + per-weapon ballistics remain owned by their respective specs. M44I owns the engagement geometry: altitude band rules, radar-masking by terrain + ground clutter, missile-engagement envelopes when target/shooter are different platform classes, asymmetric kill-cam framing, and authored cross-arm scenarios.

## Player-facing behavior

### Altitude band registry

Authored altitude bands gate which engagements are even possible:

| Band | Altitude AGL | Typical occupant | Cross-arm visibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| `nap_of_earth` | 0-50 m | Attack/Scout helicopter | Hidden from fighters above 1000 m unless line-of-sight clear |
| `low_level` | 50-300 m | Utility helicopter / VTOL hover | Fighter radar sees with -20 dB clutter penalty |
| `tactical` | 300-3000 m | Attack helicopter cruise / fighter low-CAP | Mutually visible; missile envelopes open |
| `medium` | 3000-9000 m | Fighter cruise / bomber ingress | Fighters dominant; helicopters absent |
| `high` | 9000-15000 m | Fighter / strategic bomber | Fighter-only; helicopters cannot reach |
| `stratospheric` | 15000-30000 m | Strategic bomber / spy plane | Fighter-only; thin air degrades weapons |

### Radar masking + ground clutter

- Fighter radar (AESA pulse-doppler per M44H) detects targets via doppler shift. **Helicopters in `nap_of_earth` band produce near-zero closure rate against ground clutter — radar reads them as ground returns**; signal-to-clutter ratio drops below detection threshold.
- Look-down/shoot-down mode (M44H AESA secondary mode) cuts clutter; reveals helicopters but only within `tactical` band and above.
- Terrain mask: any terrain feature ≥ 30 m higher than the helicopter, between helicopter and fighter, breaks the radar lock for as long as the mask holds.
- IR signature: helicopters at `nap_of_earth` band emit less detectable IR plume than fighters at altitude (rotor wash diffuses heat). IR-guided AAM (AIM-9 per M44H) requires `tactical` band or higher for reliable lock against helicopters.

### Cross-arm missile envelopes

Per launching-platform vs target-platform pair, missiles have authored envelopes:

| Shooter | Weapon | Target | Min range | Max range | Min closure | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fighter (M44H) | AIM-9 IR | Helicopter | 800 m | 12 km | 50 m/s | IR-lock requires target above `low_level` band OR sun-rejection disabled |
| Fighter (M44H) | AIM-120 AESA | Helicopter | 1500 m | 80 km | 100 m/s | Look-down/shoot-down required; clutter degrades pK |
| Fighter (M44H) | M61 20mm guns | Helicopter | 50 m | 1500 m | none | Gun-pass tactic; high closure = single firing window |
| Helicopter (M44G) | Stinger-class IR AGM-defense | Fighter | 200 m | 4 km | none | Helicopter-mounted self-defense missile (M44I-introduced loadout option); IR lock from ground-up looking up at hot exhaust |
| Helicopter (M44G) | Hellfire-A laser AGM | Fighter | 500 m | 6 km | none | Asymmetric edge case: pre-lased + LOAL; requires fighter to fly slow + steady (unlikely) |

### Engagement-pattern doctrine for AI

- **Fighter-vs-helicopter:** AI fighter prefers gun-pass over missile if `range ≤ 2 km`; AAM if `range ≥ 4 km`. Disengages to `medium` band if helicopter mounts AGM-defense missile.
- **Helicopter-vs-fighter:** AI helicopter prefers terrain-mask retreat over engagement; pops up only when AGM-defense missile is loaded + fighter is `tactical` band or lower; otherwise drops to `nap_of_earth` and hides.
- **Mixed-arm flight:** if both players field one of each class, doctrine routes them into **separate altitude bands** until one breaks coverage. The interaction is asymmetric — fighter rules the high game, helicopter rules the low game.

### Asymmetric kill-cam framing

- Cross-arm kill triggers M41's polished kill cam with **two camera roles**: shooter POV + victim POV; cinematic chains both for ~3 s each.
- Cross-arm killcam slow-mo factor = 0.4× (slower than same-class kill 0.6×); the asymmetry is the show.
- Per-victim ejection (per M44H ACES II) plays into the kill-cam regardless of who shot.

### M44I-introduced loadout option

- **AGM-defense missile (Stinger-class)** — 1 hardpoint on M44G Attack + Scout helicopter; ground-launched IR-homer; the helicopter's answer to a fighter pass.

## Out of scope

- Air-to-ground engagements between helicopter/fighter and ground vehicles (owned by M44D/G/H).
- Anti-air SAM systems (deferred; would be M44J if shipped).
- Helicopter-to-helicopter combat (owned by M44G; same-class rules apply).
- Fighter-to-fighter combat (owned by M44H; same-class rules apply).
- AWACS / AEW radar overlap (deferred to post-launch).

## Dependencies

- M44G (helicopter rotor physics + chassis weapons) — must close.
- M44H (fighter / VTOL / radar + AAM/AGM + ejection) — must close.
- M14C (HEAT/APFSDS round producers) — for missile warhead physics.
- M14D (projectile-vs-projectile CCD) — for AAM intercept of incoming SAM/AAM in cross-arm scenarios.

- M7B (squad command grammar) — for mixed-arm squad tasking.

## Notes for the implementer

- The 6 altitude bands are a content/data axis — no engine changes; just an authored RON table that the engagement-doctrine code reads.
- Radar-masking calculation reuses M44H's existing `RadarBeam` raycast — M44I adds terrain-mask + clutter-modifier on top, not a separate raycast.
- The Stinger-class AGM-defense missile is a content addition to M44G's `chassis_weapons/helicopter/` subtree (per the unified chassis_weapons subtree convention from M44H).
- Asymmetric kill-cam framing should be implemented as a `KillCamProfile::CrossArm` variant on M41's polished kill cam, not a separate camera kernel.
- The authored doctrine table should be tunable by storyteller per M25C — `randy_random` may bias fighters to prefer suicidal low passes (high entertainment), `cassandra_classic` keeps it military-realistic.
- Replay determinism requires the doctrine decision to be a pure function of `(tick, snapshot, doctrine_table)`; no wall-clock or RNG outside the seeded sim PRNG.
