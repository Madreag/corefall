# M11.7 — PvE Endgame Bosses + World Events

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M11.7 is the PvE endgame milestone** — the 5 named bosses + 12 dynamic world events that give 100+ hour PvE Survival runs their narrative spine. After M11.7, every PvE world has 1-2 boss encounters, every session has dynamic storyteller events, and endgame progression unlocks unique recipes + artifacts + lore.

M11.7 splits this out of M11.5 because boss content + storyteller events are their own coherent scope. M11.5 ships the survival mechanics + procgen; M11.6 ships transport; M11.7 ships endgame challenge.

M11.7 promise: **"100 hours in, you face the Hollow King in Earth's volcanic core, the Frozen Heart in Europa's caverns, the Last Star on Vulcan — each kill rewriting your civilization."**

## Player-facing behavior

### 5 PvE endgame bosses (uses canonical BossDef schema)

**Schema:** All 5 bosses are authored against the canonical `BossDef` schema defined in `specs/active/M7.md` § Mini-boss + boss patterns. M11.7 ships DATA rows; the boss engine (`cf-boss` crate) lives in M7.

| Boss | World / Biome | Required tier | Phase summary | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Hollow King** | Earth volcanic biome | T3 plasma weapon | 3 phases; phase 1 ranged flame; phase 2 summons lava minions; phase 3 enraged pyroclastic burst | Unique recipe (Pyroclastic Armor) + artifact + lore unlock |
| **The Frozen Heart** | Europa cave system | T2 weapons + heat-resistance gear | 3 phases; phase 1 cryogenic blast; phase 2 ice mech adds; phase 3 supercooled core meltdown | Cryo-Plasma Rifle recipe + ice-walker artifact + lore |
| **The Crimson Tide** | Mars dust storm boss | T2 mech + ammo stockpile | 4 phases; phase 1 sand-walker swarm; phase 2 storm intensifies; phase 3 split into 3 mini-bosses; phase 4 reform | Dust-rated chassis recipe + sand artifact + lore |
| **The Eclipse Walker** | Mimas microgravity | T2 weapons + jet pack required | 3 phases; phase 1 agile cyborg; phase 2 gravity inversion; phase 3 sustained combat | Micro-gravity boots + cyborg artifact + lore |
| **The Last Star** | Vulcan endgame | T3-T4 endgame; campaign-long unlock | 5 phases; Sol-zone superboss; each phase corresponds to a Vulcan biome challenge | Stellar Forge recipe + Last Star artifact + 5 lore unlocks |

**Boss arena rules** (per M7 schema EscapeRule):
- Hollow King: OneWayCutsceneEntry (player commits; no retreat)
- Frozen Heart: TwoWayDoor (player can retreat to refit)
- Crimson Tide: TimedRetreat (arena seals after 5 minutes)
- Eclipse Walker: TwoWayDoor (microgravity allows retreat)
- Last Star: OneWayCutsceneEntry (final commitment)

### 12 dynamic world events

**Registration API:** All 12 events register via M7's `cf-storyteller::registry.register()` API at scenario load. M11.7 ships the event DATA + trigger logic + effects; M7 owns the registry + scheduling. See `specs/active/M7.md` § Storyteller event registration API for the canonical contract.

Per M7 storyteller integration — events registered via M7 storyteller registration API. Each event has typed severity + trigger + effect:

**1. Solar flare (severity: critical):**
- Triggers from astrography astronomical model
- Duration: 1 in-game hour
- Effect: all electronics offline; mass blackout cascade per M7.6
- Player must fall back to manual hand-crank generator OR battery reserves
- Replay event: `event.solar_flare_started/ended`

**2. Pirate raid (severity: high):**
- Faction forces attack player base
- Wave count: 2-5 waves; difficulty scales with player tier
- Reward: pirate gear + loot drops
- Mission-style event with objective markers

**3. Trader arrival (severity: low):**
- NPC trader ship docks at player base
- Offers gear / recipes / materials at fluctuating prices
- Player can trade or refuse
- Lasts 30 in-game minutes

**4. Anomaly storm (severity: medium):**
- Existing anomaly cluster expands temporarily
- New anomalies appear within 50m radius
- Artifact farming opportunity (rare drops)
- Lasts 15-30 in-game minutes

**5. Volcanic eruption (severity: high; volcanic biomes only):**
- Lava cascade affects surface
- New ore deposits revealed at lava cooling
- Player must evacuate zone
- Lasts 1 in-game hour; lava cools naturally

**6. Asteroid impact (severity: medium):**
- Random ore-rich asteroid lands on player's planet
- Massive ore deposit revealed at impact site
- Crater hazard zone (radiation + hot terrain)
- Mining opportunity

**7. Mysterious signal (severity: low):**
- Codex artifact + lore unlock; quest line begins
- Player must investigate signal source
- Reward: rare recipe + faction reputation

**8. Famine (severity: medium):**
- Low caloric resources globally
- Forces player to focus on M7 plant growing + cooking + scavenging
- Lasts 24 in-game hours

**9. Plague outbreak (severity: high; faction-specific):**
- Affliction risk; medical recipes needed
- Player can develop antidote (T3 research)
- Lasts 12 in-game hours

**10. Faction war declaration (severity: critical):**
- 2 factions declared war
- Mass attacks spawn on player territory if relevant
- Player can ally; reward depends on side

**11. Eclipse (severity: medium; bodies in alignment per DR-039):**
- Solar reduced to 0% on planet being eclipsed
- Battery management becomes critical
- AI uses darkness for stealth

**12. Mineral rush (severity: low):**
- Rare ore deposit reveals on world surface
- Player rush to claim; faction conflict possible
- Lasts 1 in-game hour
