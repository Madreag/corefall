# M11.5 — PvE Survival Mode + Inter-Planet Transport

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M11.5 is the PvE Survival game mode + inter-planet transport milestone** — the Terraria / Stationeers / Minecraft / Cortex-Command hybrid PvE mode that unlocks the **full sandbox potential** of every Corefall system at scale. After M11.5, players can:

1. Spawn on a **large procedurally-generated planet** (per DR-039 catalog + new procedural generation)
2. Survive + build + craft + base through **all 5 crafting tiers** (M7.8)
3. Use the **full power-engineering ladder** (M7.6) to defend / sustain / expand
4. **Travel between worlds** via dropship / rocket / teleporter (3 launch methods)
5. Build space stations + asteroid mining colonies + planet-side bases
6. **Solo or 2-8 player coop** via M11 online co-op infrastructure
7. Face dynamic AI raiders / faction wars / world events / endgame bosses (per M7 storytellers)
8. **Persist** across sessions via M11 server persistence

M11.5 promise: **"if you survive 100 hours, you've built a multi-world civilization with your own bunker, your own avatar, and your own AI commanders to fight."**

### Procedural world generation (M5.9 / M5.10 / M7.7 integration)

Per launch world (12 worlds per DR-039), M11.5 generates:

- **Surface terrain**: per-world surface_template + biome variations (3-5 biomes per world per M7.7)
- **Ore deposits**: per-world ore_distribution (M8.6); deeper = rarer
- **Hazard zones**: per-world environmental hazards (acid rain on Venus; dust storms on Mars; cryogenic on Europa; etc.)
- **AI raider spawns**: per-faction settlement zones + raid frequency
- **Anomaly placements**: STALKER-style anomaly hazards (per M5.7) with artifact loot
- **Resource scarcity tiers**: surface = abundant T0/T1; mid-depth = T1/T2; deep crust = T2/T3; core = T3/T4
- **Pre-existing structures**: ruined outposts (with codex lore); pirate bases (loot + bosses); ancient artifacts

Generation seed deterministic; same seed = byte-identical world per replay determinism contract.

### Three launch worlds to "build your civilization" on (M11.5 launches with 3 + M11.6 extension to 12)

To prevent feature creep, **M11.5 ships 3 procedurally-generated worlds at launch**:

1. **Earth** — temperate; baseline atmosphere; all 12 ores accessible; balanced for new players
2. **Mars** — low gravity + thin atmosphere + dust storms; iron + gold rich; suit required
3. **Mimas** — microgravity vacuum; ice deposits; tactical training ground for spaceships

Remaining 9 worlds available at M11.5 close as "post-launch unlock" via campaign progression OR Material Lab unlock.

### Inter-planet transport — 3 launch methods

Per DR-039 + spec/celestial-bodies-and-worlds-model orbital math + comms latency:

**1. Dropship (T1 — Industrial-era)**

- Player builds a dropship at base (T2 fabricator + 50 steel + 20 motor + power 10 kW + 30 min)
- Flight time = `distance_m / dropship_velocity` (1000 m/s baseline)
- Earth → Mars: 2-4 in-game hours (depends on orbital position at launch time)
- Player can pilot OR set autopilot (M6.5 LLM mind or scripted)
- Risk: anti-air weapons can shoot down; crash chance at landing (per DR-027 craft state); fuel consumption
- Cargo capacity: 5 actors + 10 weapons + 50 material units
- Multiple dropships can be queued / scheduled

**2. Rocket (T2 — Advanced-era)**

- Player builds a multi-stage rocket at base (T3 fabricator + nuclear engine + 500 steel + 200 titanium + 100 motor + power 100 kW + 4 hr + assembly line)
- Flight time = `flight_time = distance_km / 100 m/s` (acceleration profile from low Earth orbit; faster than dropship for inter-planetary)
- Earth → Vulcan: ~12-24 in-game hours
- Higher cargo capacity: 20 actors + 50 weapons + 500 material units
- Endgame option for true inter-planetary expansion

**3. Teleporter (T3 — Endgame; requires both endpoints + paired)**

- Player builds two teleporter pads (T3 fabricator + 100 titanium + 50 superconductor + 30 plasma_cell + 20 capacitor + power 50 kW + 8 hr + plasma forge)
- Pads must be **paired** (player marks one as "anchor" and other as "destination")
- Instant transport (1-tick) between paired pads
- Power cost per transport: 50 kWh per actor (large drain)
- Cannot transport items > 100 kg (player + small inventory only; not cargo)
- Pad cooldown: 30s between transports
- Endgame requirement: paired pads must be built on the destination world FIRST (so dropship is still required for initial expansion)

**Comms latency during transport:**
- Per DR-039: Earth ↔ Mars comms latency 6-44 minutes round-trip
- During dropship/rocket flight: player loses real-time base awareness (HUD shows "BASE COMMS DELAY 12 min")
- Teleporter is instant; comms instant on arrival

### Space stations + orbital bases

Per DR-039 + DR-035:

- **Orbital station** (per celestial spec representative class) can be built as a small base
- Built via T3 fabricator + special "orbital launch" recipe (300 steel + 100 nuclear_cell + power 1 MW + 24 hr)
- Once built, becomes a persistent base in orbit around a planet
- Player can dock dropships at the station
- Station provides:
  - Zero-g manufacturing (efficiency +20% for some recipes)
  - Comm relay (reduces comms delay to surface bases)
  - Mining drone bay (auto-mines belt asteroids in same orbital zone)
  - Defense turrets covering nearby asteroids
- Risks:
  - Asteroid impact (storm equivalent for stations)
  - Pirate raids
  - Atmospheric loss if breached (sealed life support)
  - Solar storm radiation

### Asteroid mining colonies

- Belt asteroids (per DR-039 representative class) can be claimed as small bases
- Player builds drilling rig + ore extractor + cargo bay + thrusters
- Each asteroid has unique ore composition (1-3 ore types; rarer at outer belt)
- Asteroids slowly drift in orbital path (M5.9 + DR-038 gravity model)
- Auto-extraction via drone bots (per M5 drone allies)
- Player picks up cargo via dropship periodic visits

### Endgame bosses (per M7 boss patterns + M11.5 PvE-specific)

5 PvE-survival-specific endgame bosses (per world):

1. **The Hollow King** (Earth volcanic biome) — flame-spitting world boss; T3 plasma weapon required
2. **The Frozen Heart** (Europa cave system) — cryogenic mech; T2 weapons + heat resistance gear
3. **The Crimson Tide** (Mars dust storm boss) — sand-walker swarm; T2 mech + ammo stockpile
4. **The Eclipse Walker** (Mimas microgravity) — agile cyborg; T2 weapons + jet pack required
5. **The Last Star** (post-launch; Vulcan) — Sol-zone superboss; T3-T4 endgame required; campaign-long unlock

Boss kills drop unique recipes + artifacts + lore unlocks.

### World events (per M7 storyteller + M11.5 extends)

Dynamic events on persistent worlds:

- **Solar flare** (per DR-039 astrography): all electronics disabled for 1 hour; mass blackout cascade per M7.6
- **Pirate raid**: faction forces attack player base; mass-defense scenario
- **Trader arrival**: NPC trader docks at player base; offers gear / recipes / materials
- **Anomaly storm**: anomaly hazards expand temporarily; artifact farming opportunity
- **Volcanic eruption** (Vulcan / Earth volcanic): lava cascade affects surface; new ore deposits revealed
- **Asteroid impact**: random ore-rich asteroid lands; mining opportunity
- **Mysterious signal**: codex artifact + lore unlock; quest line
- **Famine** (low caloric resources): forces player to focus on M7 plant growing + cooking
- **Plague outbreak** (faction-specific): affliction risk; medical recipes needed

Per Rimworld storyteller pacing: Cassandra Classic / Phoebe Chillax / Randy Random / Ironman / Sandbox.

### Persistent multi-session base + world

Per M11 + DR-029 save model:

- Player's base persists across sessions
- World state persists (terrain damage / built structures / ore extraction / faction territory)
- Veterans persist with scars + relationships (per M11)
- Inventory + gold + research progression persists

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Player survives first night on Earth
  Given fresh PvE survival scenario + Cassandra storyteller
  When player spawns at noon:
    Then T0 recipes available; player can gather wood + stone
  When dusk falls:
    Then storyteller event "Approaching Night" fires; visibility drops
  When night begins:
    Then AI raiders may spawn; player must defend
  When dawn:
    Then player survives night; +1 day stat; +research point

Scenario: Dropship travel Earth → Mars
  Given dropship built + 50 fuel + Mars accessible
  When player initiates travel:
    Then dropship.depart fires
    Flight time = orbital_distance / dropship_velocity (e.g. 4 in-game hours at peak distance)
    Banner "Transit to Mars: 4hr 12min — comms delay 18min"
  When arrival:
    Then dropship.land fires at Mars LZ; player + cargo deployed
    And astrography.distance_changed updates

Scenario: Teleporter requires paired pads on both ends
  Given player has 1 teleporter pad on Earth + 0 on Mars
  When player attempts transport:
    Then rejected with reason="no_destination_pad"
  When player builds 2nd pad on Mars (after dropship trip first):
    Then teleporter.activated fires
  When player uses:
    Then instant transport; 50 kWh drained from local grid

Scenario: Orbital station persistent across sessions
  Given player builds orbital station above Earth
  When session ends + player logs out
  When player returns next session:
    Then station persists; manufacturing in progress continues
  And mining drones continued asteroid extraction

Scenario: Dynamic faction war
  Given Faction A relationship with Faction B = -55
  When trigger event fires:
    Then faction.war_declared fires for the entire server
    And mass attacks spawn over next 24 hours
    And player can choose side (alliance + reward)

Scenario: Endgame boss kill
  Given player on Earth volcanic biome with T3 plasma weapon
  When player engages Hollow King boss:
    Then boss multi-phase fight per M7 boss patterns
    When boss HP = 0:
      Then boss.killed fires
      And unique recipe drops + artifact + lore unlock
      And cross-shard event notifies other players (if MMO mode)

Scenario: Storyteller event escalation
  Given Cassandra Classic + 5 in-game days elapsed

    Then director.escalation fires
    And weather changes to storm OR raid event triggers

Scenario: Resource scarcity by depth

Scenario: World event "solar flare"

  Given scenario config: permadeath=true
  When player dies:
    Then save cannot rollback; new save required
    And player must restart from scratch

Scenario: Multi-player coop persistence
  Given 4 players on same PvE shard
  When player 1 builds turret + leaves session
  When player 2 returns: turret persists; player 2 can continue building

Scenario: Determinism replay

  Then PvE survival events byte-identical across replay
```
