# M28E — Base Filter Maintenance + Refrigerant Cycle + Fire Suppression

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Stationeers-deep filter-lifecycle + refrigerant chemistry + sprinkler/fire-suppression system: every powered base device with airflow has a filter that clogs, refrigerant gas types have their own chemistry (R-22, ammonia, CO2 supercritical) with leak detection + refill, and fire-response systems (sprinklers + halon dump + CO2 flood + airlock isolation) trigger on detected fire.

## Player-facing behavior

- **Filter lifecycle**: every scrubber / heater intake / AC unit / breach_repair_tool / suit (M19C) has a filter slot. Filter clogs over hours of operation → flow rate drops → replace at base.
- **Filter ladder** (mirrors M19C suit filters): T1 paper (cheap; clogs fast) → T2 chemical (mid; clogs in pollutant atmosphere) → T3 HEPA (premium; rare-earth elements; longest life) → T4 universal multi-stage.
- **Refrigerant cycle**: AC and cooler modules use refrigerant (R-22, ammonia, CO2 supercritical) per Stationeers chemistry. Refrigerant leaks degrade cooling; replacement at base refrigerant station.
- **Refrigerant leak detection**: leak sensor module per-room detects refrigerant in atmosphere; alarms if R-22 or ammonia present.
- **Fire suppression**:
  - Sprinklers (water-based) — flood detected fire rooms; risk to electronics.
  - Halon dump — fire-out gas (chemical); safe for electronics but dangerous to actors.
  - CO2 flood — fast extinguish; suffocates fire AND nearby actors.
  - Manual fire extinguisher (M6 inventory item) — handheld.
- **Smoke detector + auto-response**: smoke detector in each room; on fire detection → auto-trigger sprinkler OR halon OR CO2 (per setting).
- **Fire alarm panel**: per-base panel showing all room fire states + system armed/disarmed + manual triggers.

## The filter table (locked)

| Filter | Compatible | Throughput | Clog rate | Cost |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| T1 paper | dust / particulate | 80% | 4 hours per filter | 1 paper |
| T2 chemical | pollutant / chlorine | 90% | 2 hours in toxic atm | 2 steel + 1 activated_carbon |
| T3 HEPA | all particulate + chemical | 95% | 8 hours per filter | 3 steel + 4 activated_carbon + 1 rare_earth |
| T4 universal | EVERYTHING (multi-stage) | 100% | 12 hours per filter | 5 steel + 8 activated_carbon + 2 rare_earth + 1 valve |

## The refrigerant table (locked)

| Refrigerant | Boiling point | Toxicity | Cost per kg | Use case |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| R-22 (HCFC) | -40.8°C | Mild (asphyxiant) | low | Standard AC |
| Ammonia (NH3) | -33.3°C | High (poisonous) | medium | Industrial cooling; cheaper but dangerous |
| CO2 supercritical | -78.5°C (sublimation) | Asphyxiant only at high concentration | medium | Premium; vacuum-friendly; doesn't deplete ozone |
| Water-glycol | -40°C to +100°C | None | low | Coolant loop (M28B); not for vapor-compression cycle |

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Filter clogs after extended use
  Given a base scrubber with T1 paper filter operating in moderate atmosphere
  When 4 in-game hours elapse
  Then filter_clogged fires
  And scrubber throughput drops to 50%
  And HUD displays "SCRUBBER FILTER CLOGGED — replace"
  And player must run to scrubber + hold [E] to replace (3s)

Scenario: Filter replacement restores throughput
  Given a clogged scrubber filter
  When the player replaces with a fresh T1 paper filter
  Then base_filter_replaced fires
  And scrubber throughput returns to 100%
  And used filter material can be recycled at base for 50% material recovery

Scenario: Refrigerant leak detected by sensor
  Given a base AC with R-22 refrigerant + a refrigerant_leak_sensor in the same room
  When the AC develops a leak (5% per hour rate)
  Then refrigerant_leak_detected fires
  And HUD alarm "R-22 LEAK — ROOM <id>"
  And R-22 partial pressure in room rises (M19 atmospheric integration)
  And actor exposure builds toward asphyxiant affliction (M16A)

Scenario: Refrigerant refill at station
  Given an AC with refrigerant level at 30%
  When the player connects to the refrigerant_station + selects R-22 refill
  Then refrigerant_refilled fires
  And AC refrigerant returns to 100%
  And AC cooling capacity returns to full

Scenario: Smoke detector triggers sprinkler auto-response
  Given a fire breaks out in the kitchen room (M15 fire material spreads)
  And a smoke_detector is in the same room
  When the detector identifies smoke at threshold
  Then smoke_detected fires
  And sprinkler_triggered fires (auto-cascade per setting)
  And water spreads across the room extinguishing the fire
  And M19 water material spreads + actors take wetness affliction (minor)

Scenario: Halon dump extinguishes fire safely for electronics
  Given a fire in the server room (sensitive electronics)
  When the player triggers halon_dump_triggered manually
  Then halon gas floods the room

  And actors in room without breathing apparatus take asphyxiation affliction (M16A)

  Then per-room status shows: armed/disarmed + smoke detected / clear / suppressing

  And history log shows past 24h of fire events

Scenario: Multi-system cascading fire response
  Given a kitchen fire + nearby electrical fire (cascading)
  When detected by two adjacent smoke detectors
  Then sprinkler triggers in kitchen + halon dump triggers in electrical room
  And both fires extinguish via appropriate method
  And per-room damage minimized

Scenario: Determinism across filter + fire scenarios
  Given two engines running m28e_kitchen_fire_response with same seed
```

## Notes for the implementer

- Per-filter clog state is a `f32` capacity (0.0 = fresh; 1.0 = clogged); accumulates per tick per device airflow rate.
- Refrigerant gas registered in M19 as a tracked atmospheric component; leak detection reads partial pressure threshold.
- Sprinkler water spreads across room ceiling tiles (M19 + M15 fluid simulation); damages electronics in same room (per cf-render-2d damage state).
- Halon is a single-room dump (atmosphere replacement); CO2 flood is multi-room (cascading via M28D HVAC).
- IC10 chip slot per smoke_detector for custom triggers (e.g., "ignore steam but trigger on flammable_gas").
- Refrigerant_station T2 base module with 4 refrigerant types in storage; player selects which to refill via UI.
