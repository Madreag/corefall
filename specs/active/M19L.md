# M19L — Gas + Liquid Registry Full (24 Gases, 33 Liquids, Stationeers + ONI Parity)

## Status

`active`

## Intent

M19 enumerates 10 launch gases and 6 liquid mixtures; M15C lists 25+ liquids by name without full thermodynamic schemas. M19B + M19I + M28B consume gas + liquid data assuming a complete registry. **M19L ships the canonical, single-source gas + liquid registry** — 24 launch gases + 33 launch liquids — each declared with full thermodynamic fields: molar mass, density, viscosity, surface tension, boil/freeze temps, specific heat, thermal conductivity, flammability, toxicity, corrosiveness, electrical conductivity.

Promise: **"helium balloons float, mercury sinks through every floor, LOX + hydrocarbon = bomb — every gas and liquid behaves like real chemistry."**

## Canonical ownership

Canonical owner of the **gas registry** and **liquid registry**. M15C still owns solids/powders/plasmas/energy fields, but gases + liquids move here because they carry richer thermodynamic state (PV=nRT, latent heat, viscous flow) that the broader material registry does not capture.

| Topic | Owner |
|---|---|
| Solids / powders / plasmas / energy fields | M15C |
| **Gas registry (this spec)** | **M19L** |
| **Liquid registry (this spec)** | **M19L** |
| Reaction matrix | M15D |
| Atmospherics PV=nRT | M19 |
| Fuel chain | M19B |
| Per-tile element model | M3B |

## Player-facing behavior

- 24 gases each with real molar mass — He (4 g/mol) rises, radon (222 g/mol) pools in basements; layered atmospheres form automatically.
- 33 liquids each with real density + viscosity — mercury (13534 kg/m³) sinks through wood/concrete; molten gold (17310 kg/m³) sinks through mercury.
- F8 tile inspect shows id + mass + temperature + density + viscosity.
- HUD breath-mix flags toxic gases (Cl2, SO2, H2S, phosgene, CO); M16 afflictions trigger.
- Cryogenic liquids (LH2/LOX/liquid methane) self-boil without insulation; rocket-tank gameplay hinges on cryocooler uptime.
- Mod authors load custom gases/liquids via `content/gases/*.ron` + `content/liquids/*.ron`; cf-mod schema-validates.
- cfctl `query.material.gas_registry` + `query.material.liquid_registry` return the full list.
- Liquids of different density layer in containers (oil floats on water; mercury sinks below both).
- Surface tension governs bead size — water beads up on hydrophobic surfaces; ethanol spreads thin.
- Boil + freeze temps drive M3B phase transitions; registry is sole source of truth.

## Out of scope

- New REACTIONS between these substances (M15D owns; M19L only declares them).
- Plant respiration / O2 production (M19I).
- Per-suit breathing routing (M19).
- Visual fog / volumetric rendering (M40).
- Item-scale storage canisters (M19B).

## Dependencies

- M15C reserves gas + liquid IDs referenced here.
- M19 PV=nRT consumes registry-driven molar mass + density.
- M3B per-tile model consumes registry-driven boil/freeze temps.
- M15B GPU kernel reads registry at startup into constant buffers.

## Notes for implementer

- **Audit first.** M19 ships 10 gases + 6 liquid mixtures today. Mark them `PASS (already in)` and file `.ron` entries only for the missing 14 gases + 27 liquids.
- Replace hardcoded gas constants in `cf-atmos::*` with registry lookups; mark each replacement `IMPLEMENTED`.
- `gas.smoke` and `gas.pollutant_x` are mixtures, not pure compounds — keep approximate molar masses as locked constants (note in comments).
- Cross-reference NIST + ONI wiki + Stationeers wiki when in doubt on viscosity/surface tension.
- Mercury (13534 kg/m³) must NOT float above molten_gold (17310) — verify with golden tests at 273 K.
- LOX + LH2 do NOT autoignite at room temperature without a separate M15D reaction; the registry alone does not ignite.
- `liq.tar` viscosity (1000 Pa·s) is intentionally pitch-like; do not lower for "fun" — gameplay comes from real chem.
- Schema validation runs at **load time**, not per-tick.
- Mod authors append; never mutate existing IDs (replay compatibility).
