# M37C — Stealth Visibility + Light/Shadow + Sneak Attack + Distraction Items + Camouflage

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Promote M6's stealth meter from a single distance-and-occlusion scalar into a **full light-and-shadow stealth system**: per-tile light level read from M19H light propagation feeds per-actor visibility class (Lit / Partial / Shadow / Concealed), sneak-attack damage multipliers fire when an attacker is concealed AND positioned behind the target's facing cone, 5 throwable / deployable distraction items create false signals in the M22C sound + M6 sight kernels, and 4 environment-keyed camouflage equipment pieces multiply visibility per biome (ghillie suit in forest, urban in concrete, arctic in snow, desert in sand) — turning a scoped Sniper into a 5-tile-radius statue and giving Engineers a reason to throw a noisemaker at a guard's blind spot.

M37C promise: **"stealth is the light you stand in, the angle you stand at, and what you can throw to make the guard look the other way."**

## Player-facing behavior

- **Per-tile light level overlay.** M19H ships per-tile light (0–50000 lux). M37C consumes the same buffer and classifies into visibility brackets:
  - **Lit** (≥ 6000 lux) — daylight, bright lamp; visibility multiplier ×1.0.
  - **Partial** (1000–6000 lux) — twilight, distant lamp; ×0.6.
  - **Shadow** (200–1000 lux) — dim corner, side-lit; ×0.3.
  - **Concealed** (< 200 lux) — pitch black, deep shadow; ×0.1.
  Threshold values come from `content/perception/light_brackets.ron` and are tunable.
- **Per-actor visibility class.** For each (observer, target) pair, the per-tick stealth pass computes `visibility = base_actor_size × pose_modifier × movement_modifier × light_bracket × camouflage_biome_mult × ambient_attention_mod`. Output is `VisibilityScore ∈ [0, 1]`. Detection probability per tick = `1 − exp(−visibility × observer.attention × dt)`. `perception.visibility_score_changed` fires when score crosses a 0.1 boundary.
- **Facing cone + behind-target evaluator.** Each observer has a `facing_cone_deg` (default 120°, narrower while ADS, wider for Spotter archetype). `behind_target(observer, target) := angle_from_observer_to_target ∉ observer.facing_cone`. A target behind the observer is invisible to that observer this tick (still detectable by other senses — sound, smell, ally radio call).
- **Sneak attack damage multiplier.** When attacker fires AND `(attacker.visibility_class ∈ {Shadow, Concealed}) AND behind_target(target_facing, attacker_position) AND target.alertness < Combat`, the damage routing receives `is_sneak_attack=true`. The damage multiplier table:
  - Melee weapon + sneak attack: ×3.0 + always lands critical (M14 critical hit).
  - Suppressed firearm + sneak attack: ×1.8 + chance of one-shot-headshot if precise aim point lands on head zone.
  - Unsuppressed firearm + sneak attack: ×1.4 (still amplified; muzzle flash breaks concealment after).
  - Crossbow / silenced bow: ×2.5.
  - Throwing knife: ×2.2.
  The multiplier is consumed by M14 damage router; the event `combat.sneak_attack_landed` fires with multiplier + concealment class at strike time.
- **5 distraction items.**

| Item | Effect | Range | Duration | Tier | Replay event |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `noisemaker_decoy` | Emits 90 dB acoustic signal at deploy point (sound propagation kernel processes it like a gunshot but tagged `Distraction`) | 25 tile-radius alarm | 8 s | T1 | `distraction.noisemaker_emitted` |
| `smoke_grenade_visual` | Spawns smoke hazard tile (M16) ×30 tiles; blocks LOS in M6 sight kernel; cuts per-tile light level by ×0.2 inside the cloud | 5-tile radius cloud | 30 s | T1 | `distraction.smoke_grenade_visual_emitted` |
| `flashbang_visual` | All actors with LOS to detonation receive `Flash-Blinded` (M16) for 3–8s; per-actor stealth_meter floor temporarily reset | 8-tile radius cone | instant + 3–8 s affliction | T2 | `distraction.flashbang_visual_emitted` |
| `decoy_actor` | Deploys a stationary actor-shaped silhouette tagged `is_decoy=true`; M6 sight kernel reports it as the player's chassis for first detection; AI ignores after 3 false-positive ticks | static, 60 s | 60 s OR until 3 enemies inspect | T2 | `distraction.decoy_actor_deployed` |
| `acoustic_decoy` | Emits a recorded voice-line into M37 chatter pipeline tagged `Distraction`; M22C propagation routes it like real voice; AI BT receives "enemy voice heard" trigger from a bogus location | 30-tile propagation range | 12 s (one chatter line then deploy point silent) | T3 | `distraction.acoustic_decoy_emitted` |

- **4 camouflage equipment pieces** with per-biome visibility multiplier:

| Piece | Forest | Urban | Arctic | Desert | Default biome bonus |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `camouflage_ghillie` | ×0.25 (deep cover) | ×0.85 | ×0.7 | ×0.7 | best in dense vegetation; useless in concrete |
| `camouflage_urban` | ×0.9 | ×0.30 | ×0.8 | ×0.85 | best against brick / concrete; bad in forest |
| `camouflage_arctic` | ×0.95 | ×0.9 | ×0.25 | ×1.0 | best on snow tile; useless / worse on hot biome |
| `camouflage_desert` | ×0.95 | ×0.85 | ×1.0 | ×0.30 | best on sand / dry biome; useless in snow |

Multipliers compose: `final_visibility = visibility × camouflage_biome_mult(biome_tag)`. M3B per-tile `biome_tag` (forest / urban / arctic / desert / etc.) drives selection. Mismatched camo (arctic in desert) actively penalizes by ×1.0 (no bonus) — never below ×1.0 to avoid camo-ON being worse than camo-OFF.

- **Pose + movement modifiers.**
  - Standing: ×1.0; Crouched: ×0.7; Prone: ×0.45.
  - Idle: ×1.0; Walk: ×1.2; Run: ×1.8; Sprint: ×2.5; Sneak (M5 stealth-walk input): ×0.6.
- **Sniper archetype concealment posture.** A `Sniper` with `Scope Settle` active (M7 scope settle ≥1.5s) AND `Prone` AND `camouflage_ghillie` in `forest` biome AND `light_bracket = Shadow` collapses to `final_visibility = 0.05 × 0.45 × 0.6 × 0.3 × 0.25 = 0.001` → effectively a 5-tile-radius statue. Detection probability ≈ 0% beyond rifle range. ASCII-glyph reason label: `[stealth: ghillie-prone-shadow]`.
- **Light source disruption.** Shooting a lamp (per M19H light_propagation source) drops the per-tile light bracket in a 5-tile radius; the stealth pass re-evaluates affected actors next tick. A guard who walked into Lit before now walks into Shadow.
- **AI counter-stealth.** When `visibility_score < 0.1` for ≥3 ticks, observer `attention` decays toward `Idle`; perception alarm radius shrinks ×0.7. When score jumps from `< 0.1` to `> 0.4` (a sudden movement), `perception.startle_trigger` fires + `attention` jumps to `Alert`.
- **Reason-labelled HUD (M11 compliance).** Stealth state surfaces as ASCII glyph + label: `[shadow]`, `[lit]`, `[concealed]`, `[ghillie:forest]`, `[behind-guard]`, `[sneak-ready]`. Accessibility ACC-A: every visibility transition has a caption.
- **Replay-deterministic.** Visibility computation is a pure function of (light_level, cover, camouflage, pose, movement, biome_tag, observer_attention); per-tick scoring runs in stable (observer_id, target_id) order; sneak-attack predicate evaluated at fire-time using replay-stable observer facing snapshot; no `thread_rng`.

## Out of scope

- Vertical (Z-stacked) light propagation through multi-floor buildings — M19H is 2D; M37C inherits 2D scope.
- Per-eyeball pupil dilation modeling — abstracted into the light-bracket discretization.
- Thermal / IR vision overlays (heat-signature stealth) — M44 NVG / thermal vision spec; M37C does NOT consume IR signature.
- Smell / olfaction stealth — forward-compat only; not in M37C scope.
- Suppressor wear / degradation — M14 suppressor lifecycle owns; M37C only reads `is_suppressed` from the firing profile.
- Cloaking / active camouflage tech — sci-fi tier; forward-compat reserved.
- Multiplayer-specific stealth resync — M48 networked stealth spec owns; M37C is local-sim-only.
- LLM-driven AI behavior in response to distractions — M23 / M23B mind layer; M37C only delivers signals + standard BT consumer hooks.
- Bullet-time / slow-motion stealth-kill cinematic — M11 cinematic spec; M37C ships sim only.
- Camo modular pattern editor / paint-your-own-camo UI — forward-compat content pipeline.

## Notes for the implementer

- **cfctl write-merge convention** — multiple specs each add ~5 cfctl methods to `cf-control/src/server.rs`; to avoid merge conflicts and tangled handlers, **M37C creates `game/crates/cf-control/src/m37c_actions.rs`** containing the 3 cfctl method handlers (`act.player.deploy_distraction`, `act.player.equip_camouflage`, `srv.dump_visibility`) and registers from `cf-control/src/server.rs` via a `pub mod m37c_actions;` line plus a single `m37c_actions::register(&mut router)` call. `server.rs` itself stays a thin dispatcher; per-spec handler bodies live in their own files. Same pattern is mirrored by M33B / M33C / M33D.
- Visibility is a per-(observer, target) pair scalar; cull pairs where `distance > observer.max_sight_range` before the inner math.
- Per-tick stealth pass runs **after** M19H light propagation and **after** M22C sound propagation; sequencing belongs in `cf-control::engine::tick_perception`.
- Light bracket lookup uses an inlined ramp, not a `match`; brackets are content-driven in `light_brackets.ron`.
- Camouflage biome multiplier table is `[f32; N_BIOMES]` per camo piece; inline-loaded at scenario boot.
- Sneak-attack predicate is a const-fn over snapshotted state at trigger-time — no mid-tick re-evaluation.
- `behind_target` uses `signed_angle_delta(observer.facing, observer→target)` and tests `abs(delta) > observer.facing_cone_deg / 2`.
- Distraction items emit through existing event channels (`SoundEmission`, `Affliction`, M19H light writes) — do NOT invent a new event bus.
