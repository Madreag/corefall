## Player-facing behavior

### Scenario: micro_sabotage (60-90s)

- Player spawns near a guarded reactor complex (extends M2.5 reactor with hazards + atmospherics + chassis enemies)
- Objective: **disable reactor core** within 60-90s using ANY combination of:
  - **Physics**: redirect explosives, knock down support pillars, breach pressure walls
  - **Materials**: light oil trails on fire, freeze metal, corrode armor with acid flask
  - **Hazards**: set fire near volatile gas pockets → chain ignition; flood coolant chamber
  - **Stealth**: sneak past patrols, takedown isolated guards, suppress entry alarm
  - **Origin**: human + bypass G-load; android + battery overclock; robot + immune to hypoxia in vacuum chamber

Win: reactor disabled (HP=0 OR pressure_state=Destroyed). Lose: player dies, alarm escalates beyond recovery, OR timer expires. Tutorial-safety variant caps player damage.
