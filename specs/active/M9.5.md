**M9.5 is the voice + radio + comms milestone** — ACRE2-tier voice + radio simulation per DR-043. After M9.5, players communicate with squad via voice chat with realistic radio propagation, interference, EMP failure, captions, accessibility fallbacks, and AI reason labels explain what bots believe is happening.

## Player-facing behavior

### Voice chat (proximity-based)

- Voice transmits at proximity (similar to ACRE2)
- 3 channels: Squad (encrypted) / Public (open) / Faction (faction-only)
- Push-to-talk default; toggleable to voice activity
- Per-player mute / volume per channel

### Radio propagation (ACRE2-inspired)

- Radio sets in inventory: short-range (5km) / medium (15km) / long (50km+)
- Propagation: distance attenuation + occlusion + frequency band
- Antenna height affects range
- Underground / through walls: signal degraded
- Cave / heavy terrain: blocked

### Interference + EMP

- EMP grenades (from M2.2A) disable radio for 5-30s
- EMP from machines (generators, reactor breaches)
- Solar flares (Sol-zone scenarios) disrupt for minutes
- Comms blackout affliction (DR-040 hazard taxonomy)

### Subtitle system + captions

- All voice + radio captioned (per DR-012 accessibility)
- Subtitle modes: Off / Critical-Only / Standard / Expanded (per M4A caption modes)
- Per-actor identification in subtitles
- Speaker indicator (visual ring around current speaker)

### Vacuum no_voice

- In vacuum: no audio voice transmission (sound doesn't propagate)
- Radio still works (electromagnetic; not sound)
- HUD banner "VACUUM — radio only" surfaces

### AI radio chatter

- AI bots emit radio chatter on key events:
  - "Contact, west wall!"
  - "Reloading!"
  - "Down!"
  - "Need help over here!"
- Reason labels accompany (per DR-008 humanlike AI)
- Bots respond to player voice (via M6.5 MIND or scripted patterns)

### Comms HUD

- Voice channel indicator (which channel active)
- Per-speaker direction arrow (from where the sound came)
- Subtitle stream (top or bottom; configurable)

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Voice chat proximity
  Given Player1 + Player2 at 5m distance
  When Player2 talks:
    Then voice.transmission_received fires for Player1
    And subtitle displayed
  When Player2 moves to 100m:
    Then voice attenuated significantly

Scenario: Radio propagation underground
  Given Player1 above-ground + Player2 underground
  When Player1 transmits on radio:
    Then radio.signal_degraded fires
    And Player2 hears static-rich subtitle

Scenario: EMP cuts radio
  Given Player1 with radio + EMP grenade thrown at Player1
  When EMP detonates:
    Then radio.interference fires
    For 10s: radio disabled
    Subtitle: "RADIO OFFLINE"

Scenario: Vacuum no_voice
  Given Player1 + Player2 in vacuum
  When Player1 talks:
    Then voice.transmission_blocked fires with reason="vacuum_no_voice"
    Banner "VACUUM — radio only"

Scenario: AI radio chatter
  Given AI archetype Rifleman + player visible
  When AI fires shot:
    Then voice.transmission_started fires
    And subtitle "Contact, west wall!"

Scenario: All captions per DR-012
  Given Settings.caption_mode=Standard
  Then all voice + radio events surface as captions
  And speaker identified in subtitle

Scenario: Comms blackout
  Given comms blackout active (solar flare)
  Then all bots fall back to last-known orders
  And Player communications offline
  Subtitle: "COMMS BLACKOUT"
```
