# M44F — Boats / Submarines / Underwater Combat

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M44F is the surface-vessel + amphibious + submarine + aircraft-carrier + underwater-combat milestone.** M44 listed a "submarine" SKU as a single bullet and an "amphibious truck" as a hand-wave; M16 shipped water tiles + drowning + swim affliction; no spec has ever owned **how a watercraft floats, how an amphibious truck wades shore-to-shore, how a submarine dives, how sonar paints contacts, how a torpedo runs, how a depth charge sinks, how the hull crushes at depth, how an aircraft carrier presents a landing deck with arresting cables, or how an underwater life-support suit keeps a diver alive past the helmet-breach drowning timer.** M44F closes that gap.

After M44F, the player builds a zodiac inflatable on Earth to ferry a 4-man squad across a M16 water tile field; drives an amphibious truck from beach into surf with no transition penalty; builds a patrol boat with a deck gun + sonar dome to hunt enemy submarines off the coast of Europa's subsurface ocean; builds a hovercraft to cross shore-to-shore without caring about beach gradient; builds a diesel-electric submarine that dives to 200 m and uses passive sonar to hunt cargo freighters; builds a nuclear sub that dives to 600 m and carries 6 torpedo tubes + a vertical-launch cell array; builds an aircraft carrier with a 3-cable arresting deck for M44H VTOL + carrier-trap recovery. Boats sink when hull integrity drops to zero. Submarines implode when crush depth is exceeded. Divers in M19C life-support suits operate outside the hull on the sea floor for asset salvage, mine-laying, and Europa hydrothermal-vent research.

M44F's player-facing promise: **"every world with a water tile is a naval theatre. Drive into the surf. Surface or dive. Listen on sonar or paint with active. Outrun the torpedo or take the hit. Cross the crush threshold and the hull crumples in one tick. Recover a fighter onto cable #2 or bolter."**

## Canonical ownership

This spec is the **canonical owner of watercraft + amphibious-truck buoyant-wheeled chassis + submarine combat + aircraft-carrier hull-with-deck + underwater-suit life support integration**: 7 vessel SKUs, buoyancy + draft + displacement math, dive plane / ballast tank state, passive + active sonar with bearing/range/classification, torpedo guidance (wake-homing / wire-guided / acoustic-homing), depth charge ballistics, pressure-crush per-tier hull rating, diver-outside-hull life-support contract, arresting-cable engagement geometry (cable line-segment + tailhook swept-volume), carrier-deck landing pose anchor, and the underwater-combat action event surface. Aircraft tailhook + bolter logic itself = M44H consumer; carrier hull + deck + cables = M44F producer.

## Player-facing behavior

- Build a **Zodiac Inflatable (T1)** at T1 fabricator (30 steel + 10 rubber + 5 motor + 2 hr); seats 4; outboard motor; surface-only; sinks in 2 hits from any rifle ≥ 7.62 mm; the "infil/exfil" launch boat.
- Build an **Amphibious Truck (T2)** at T2 fabricator (250 steel + 50 motor + 4× sealed wheel hubs + 1× T3 battery + 4 hr); seats 6 + 1000 kg cargo; **buoyant-wheeled chassis** wades shore-to-water with no transition penalty (sealed wheel wells displace enough to float at full load); top speed 60 px/s on land, 12 kt in water; armament: optional pintle 7.62mm MG; M44D APC inherits the wade-pose + buoyancy override from this SKU; the "drive into the surf" personnel transport.
- Build a **Patrol Boat (T2)** at T2 fabricator (300 steel + 40 motor + 1× T3 battery + 1× diesel engine + 8 hr); seats 6; deck-mounted M2 .50 cal + bow-mounted depth-charge rack (8 rounds) + passive sonar dome; surface-only; armored gunwales survive small-arms fire; the sub-hunter.
- Build a **Hovercraft (T2)** at T2 fabricator (250 steel + 60 motor + 4× lift fan + 2× T3 battery + 6 hr); seats 8 + 500 kg cargo; transitions shore↔water without slowing; immune to shallow-water hazards; weak armor; the amphibious troop carrier.
- Build a **Diesel-Electric Submarine (T3)** at T3 fabricator (800 steel + 200 titanium + 100 motor + 50 capacitor + 4× T4 battery + diesel engine + 24 hr + dry dock); crush depth 200 m; submerges via ballast tank flood; 4 torpedo tubes + passive sonar + periscope; 10-actor crew; the prowler.
- Build a **Nuclear Submarine (T4)** at T3 fabricator + plasma forge (2000 steel + 600 titanium + 1× small_fission_reactor [M19C] + 300 motor + 6× T4 battery + 48 hr + dry dock); crush depth 600 m; nuclear reactor = no surface-to-recharge dependency; 6 torpedo tubes + 12-cell vertical launch + active/passive sonar + towed-array sonar; 20-actor crew + 30-day endurance; the apex.
- Build an **Aircraft Carrier (T4)** at T4 fabricator + dry dock + plasma forge (12000 steel + 1500 titanium + 1× small_fission_reactor + 800 motor + 8× T4 battery + 72 hr); crew 60 + 24 aircraft spots; flat-top landing deck with **3 arresting cables** (cable_1 / cable_2 / cable_3 — pilot aims for cable_2 or cable_3); catapult-assisted launch + tailhook-assisted recovery; the floating airfield. Carrier exposes: `deck_pose_anchor` (where landing aircraft frame is computed relative to), 3× `arresting_cable: { line_segment, hp, engaged_aircraft_id }`, and 4× `aircraft_spot: { occupied: Option<entity_id> }`. M44H consumers (Fighter / VTOL / Ground Attack) call into this hull for trap + bolter logic via `cf-vehicle::carrier_landing`. Carrier itself is a watercraft-class chassis (displacement 100000 t at full load) with hull HP 18000 + per-zone armor 100 / 60 / 30 mm + 4 belly torpedo bulkheads.
- Drop **Mk-9 Depth Charge** (T2 munition, 30 steel + 10 explosive + 5 capacitor; built at T2 ammo press) over a submerged contact; depth-fuze settable at 50 / 100 / 200 / 400 m; lethal radius 25 m at fuze depth; the surface counter to subs.
- Fire **Mk-48 Heavy Torpedo** (T3 munition, 80 steel + 40 explosive + 1× motor + 1× sonar_seeker; built at T3 ammo press; tube-launched from sub or surface-launched from patrol boat); 4 guidance modes: wire-guided (player-piloted via cfctl `act.player.torpedo_steer`), wake-homing (locks to surface-vessel propwash), passive-acoustic (locks to loudest contact), straight-run (unguided fallback); 8000 m max range; 60 kt run speed; 300 kg warhead.
- **Sonar contact loop** — passive sonar reports `{ bearing_rad, classification: "surface" | "submerged" | "torpedo" | "unknown", confidence: 0.0..1.0, signal_strength_db }` every 2 sim seconds; active sonar adds `range_m` but advertises the pinger's own bearing to every passive listener in 10 km (the "ping = paint a target on yourself" tradeoff).
- **Buoyancy + draft + displacement** — every watercraft has `displacement_kg` (water it shoves aside when at design draft) and aggregates mass via M14A; if `mass_kg > displacement_kg` the vessel rides low or sinks; sub `ballast_fill: 0.0..1.0` modulates net buoyancy from +5% (surfaced) to -5% (submerged); ballast pump rate sets dive/surface speed.
- **Crush depth** — sub `current_depth_m` ticks every 0.5 s; if `current_depth_m > hull_crush_depth_m` the hull pressure crush event fires; below 1.5× crush depth = catastrophic implosion (1-tick destroy + all crew killed); between 1.0× and 1.5× = progressive plate failure (rivet pop events + flooding hazard tiles spawn inside hull at 0.1 m³/s per breached compartment).
- **Diver outside hull** — actor leaves a submarine through an airlock module while wearing an M19C **dive suit** (own O2 reservoir + CO2 scrubber + active pressure-equalization heater); life-support burn rate scales with depth (deeper = colder = faster heater drain); the player walks on the sea floor with M14A's limb-driven walking loop in low-G mode (water buoyancy partially negates gravity per M19 atm density rule); helmet breach in salt water = M16 `drowning` affliction in 30 s OR vacuum-exposure-equivalent at depths > 100 m.

## Out of scope

- Aircraft themselves (helicopters / fighters / gunships) — covered by M44G + M44H. The carrier hull + deck + cables are owned here; the tailhook + bolter + go-around logic + the aircraft chassis are owned by M44H.
- Naval AI strategic layer (fleet command, convoy escort missions, naval doctrine at squadron scale) — single-vessel doctrine only.
- Underwater terrain generation (kelp forests, hydrothermal vents, abyssal plain biomes) — Europa launches with flat sea floor; world-gen layer is a future milestone.
- Surface-to-surface naval gun fire control (radar fire-control solutions) — deck gun is direct-fire only at launch.
- Cargo / passenger ferry economy (booking, scheduling, civilian routes) — vessels are player-operated only.

## Notes for implementer

- Sonar bearing math is just `atan2(dy, dx)` from listener to source; signal strength is `1/r²` attenuated by water density × thermocline factor (Europa has no thermocline so passive sonar reaches farther than Earth — flag this for content tuning later).
- Buoyancy net force: `F_net = (displacement_kg - mass_kg) * g` for surface vessels; for subs add `ballast_fill * ballast_capacity_kg` to mass. Apply to vehicle Y-velocity; clamp to terminal velocity at hull-rated speed.
- Crush depth check: compare `current_depth_m * water_density_factor` to `hull_crush_depth_m`. Europa ocean is fresh water (density 1.0) vs Earth ocean (1.025 salt), so Earth crushes shallower for the same hull tier. Worth a sentence of HUD copy.
- Torpedo wire-guided guidance is just a cfctl input loop into the torpedo's `desired_heading_rad` field; the torpedo's own swept-volume motion uses the existing M14 sweep. Don't reinvent collision.
- Depth-charge pressure shockwave through the water column uses an `1/r²` falloff with a `material_factor` from cf-material's water tile chemistry (this is already in M15 — reuse it; don't author a fresh model).
- AI naval doctrine should match the M44A `vehicle_driver_doctrine.rs` shape — single state-machine struct + utility scorer for {Hunt, FireSolution, Fire, Evade, Surface}. Don't ship a planner; a state machine is enough at launch.
- Active sonar paints the pinger is a single event broadcast — every passive sonar in range receives a `sonar_contact_gained` for the pinger. Trivial to wire and creates the "ping = die" meta moment we want.
- Reuse M44A's hot-swap battery module for the diesel sub's electric-propulsion battery bank; nuclear sub uses M19C's reactor module directly (no battery dependency).
- Dive suit reservoir tick rates: O2 0.5 L/s baseline + 0.01 L/s per metre depth; heater 50 W baseline + 5 W per metre depth + 100 W per °C below ambient. Tune in content RON, not code.
- The "diver walks on sea floor" loop is M14A's walking loop with two overrides: gravity scaled to `g * (1 - buoyancy_factor)` (≈0.1× at design draft) and atmospheric density forced to water density (so M14A's jetpack-vs-density rule auto-balances any aquatic jet thruster a future spec might add).
- **Amphibious Truck wade** dispatches at the contact-patch resolution layer: per-tick, check `current_tile.kind == Water`; if true, route the chassis through `cf-vehicle::amphibious::wade_force` (which calls into `cf-vehicle::buoyancy::net_force` with the truck's displacement number); else route through M44C wheeled tire-grip. Expose the dispatch via a public `AmphibiousWade` trait so M44D APC's amphibious mode binds the same code path (NO forked re-implementation in M44D — APC's .ron declares `drivetrain_kind = WheeledAmphibious` and inherits this trait). Sealed-wheel-hub flotation is just a content-tuning displacement number on the truck .ron; the buoyancy math is shared.
- **Aircraft Carrier deck + cables** are authored as named anchor + 3 line-segments on the carrier .ron, NOT in code. `deck_pose_anchor` is a 2D pose in carrier-local frame; cables are `{ start, end, hp }` line segments at deck plane.
