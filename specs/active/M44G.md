# M44G — Combat Helicopters: Attack + Utility + Scout + Rotor Physics + Autorotation + Door Gunner

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M44G is the combat-helicopter milestone.** M44 listed a "dropship" as a single hand-wave bullet; M44A shipped electric ground vehicles; M44B shipped cargo-drone logistics; M44D shipped tanks; M44E shipped mechs; M44F shipped boats + submarines. No spec has ever owned **how a rotor spools up, how lift modulates with collective pitch, how a tail rotor counters main-rotor torque, how a single 30mm round through a rotor disk causes blade-loss imbalance, how ground effect cheats hover power, how autorotation salvages an engine-out, how a door gunner fires inside a fixed arc, or how a cargo sling pendulum-swings under a fast lateral hover.** M44G closes that gap.

After M44G, the player builds an Attack Helicopter (AH-64-equivalent) at T4 fabricator, takes off, flies a nap-of-earth combat patrol, pops up behind a ridge, lases a target with the gunner's helmet sight, fires a Hellfire-A, returns to base; builds a Utility Helicopter (Huey-equivalent) to insert an 8-man squad over a hot LZ while two door gunners suppress with M134 miniguns from left+right windows; builds a Scout Helicopter (OH-58-equivalent) and uses the mast-mounted sight to peek over a 12 m ridgeline without exposing the fuselage to ground fire.

M44G's player-facing promise: **"rotor blades take damage. Engines fail. Tail rotors get shot off. When the engine quits at 150 m, you autorotate or you die. Hover too close to dust and you blind your own pilot. Carry too much external cargo and the sling oscillates you into a low-speed crash. Door gunners only fire on their side. Cortex Command's rocket-pawn was the wrong abstraction for helicopters — this is the right one."**

## Player-facing behavior

### Three helicopter SKUs

- **Attack Helicopter (AH-64-equivalent, T4)** — 2000 steel + 400 titanium + 80 motor + jet_engine + 1× T4 battery + 18 hr + T4 fabricator. Tandem cockpit (pilot in back, gunner in front); chain-gun 30mm chin turret slaved to gunner head pose; 8× Hellfire-A AGM rack; 38-tube 70mm rocket pod on each pylon; redundant flight controls; armored canopy; max airspeed 280 px/s; hull HP 1800; armor 60 / 30 / 15 mm.
- **Utility Helicopter (Huey-equivalent, T3)** — 800 steel + 100 motor + jet_engine + 1× T3 battery + 10 hr + T3 fabricator. Single main rotor + tail rotor; pilot + co-pilot; troop bay seats 8 actors OR 2 stretchers; 2 door-gunner stations (left + right) each mounting M60 7.62mm OR M134 minigun; external cargo sling capacity 2000 kg; max airspeed 220 px/s; hull HP 1200; armor 15 / 10 / 8 mm.
- **Scout Helicopter (OH-58-equivalent, T2)** — 400 steel + 60 motor + small_turbine + 1× T3 battery + 6 hr + T2 fabricator. Single rotor; pilot + observer; mast-mounted sight (sensor above rotor disk — peeks over ridgelines without exposing fuselage); 2 hardpoints (ATGM-light, rocket pod, or FLIR pod); max airspeed 240 px/s; hull HP 600; armor 8 / 5 / 3 mm.

### Per-helicopter rotor physics

- Main rotor + tail rotor (single-rotor SKUs). Per-rotor `rpm`, `collective_pitch_deg`, `cyclic_tilt_xy`, and `autorotation_engaged: bool`.
- Lift formula: `lift_n = blade_count × rpm² × collective_pitch_deg × air_density × rotor_area × blade_health_ratio`. Thrust vector tilts via cyclic; differential cyclic translates to forward / aft / lateral acceleration.
- Tail rotor counters main-rotor torque; tail-rotor failure → uncommanded yaw spin (loss of directional control unless pilot autorotates and forward airspeed restores weathervane stability).
- Engine drives rotor via transmission; engine HP = 0 stops powering rotor; rotor RPM bleeds at `-2.5 rpm/s` unless autorotation is engaged.
- Pedal input controls anti-torque (tail-rotor pitch); cyclic input tilts disk; collective input changes blade pitch on all blades simultaneously.

### Per-rotor independent damage

- Main rotor disk has 5 individually-tracked blades with `blade_hp: 300` each. 1 blade lost = imbalance vibration (+0.6 m/s lateral oscillation, -22% effective lift). 2 blades lost = rotor sheds in 3 s (catastrophic).
- Tail rotor: `tail_rotor_hp: 100`; destroyed = anti-torque lost.
- Per-blade decals: nicks at 70%, visible chips at 40%, sparking at 10%.
- Engine bay, transmission, hydraulic lines, fuel cell: each component HP separate from hull HP (reuses M44C `component_damage` cascade).

### Ground effect

- Within `1.0 × rotor_diameter` of terrain → `+15%` effective lift (less collective needed → less power draw → more payload tolerance at low hover).
- Hover-taxi at 1-3 m AGL is the "fast in-ground-effect cruise" mode; advanced pilots exploit.
- Sand / dust / snow kick-up rendered via M12A material lookup when IGE over loose ground; obscures pilot vision (M19 visibility hazard) during landing.

### Autorotation emergency landing

- On engine-out, the pilot issues `act.player.engage_autorotation`; stance transitions to `Autorotating`; collective auto-drops to neutral; rotor uses descent-driven airflow to maintain RPM.
- Autorotation rotor-RPM equation: `d_rpm = (descent_rate_ms × autorot_efficiency − collective_drag) × dt`. Stable if collective ≤ 4°.
- Descent rate ~8 m/s in stable autorotation; pilot must flare at 5 m AGL or below to survive landing.
- Rotor RPM < 50% at flare = hard crash (chassis destroyed).
- Successful flare = vertical velocity → 0 over 1.5 s; landing damage = scuffed skids only.

### Door gunner role (Utility)

- Two named slots `door_gunner_left` and `door_gunner_right` per Utility SKU.
- Each fires M60 or M134 minigun into a fixed-arc cone (180° hemisphere on their side; cannot fire across cockpit).
- Door gunner POV kill-cam fires per shot for the gunner; pilot retains separate POV.

### Chassis weapons (helicopter-class)

- **Chain gun 30mm** (Attack) — chin-mounted; slaved to gunner head pose; 1200 RPM; 1200-round belt; HE+AP mix.
- **Hellfire-A AGM** (Attack) — semi-active laser-homing OR fire-and-forget IR-lock; 6000 m range; 8 kg shaped-charge warhead; reuses M14C HEAT.
- **Rocket pod 70mm** (Attack / Scout) — 19-tube or 38-tube; unguided dive rockets; M14C HE warhead.
- **M60 door gun** (Utility) — 7.62mm belt; 600 RPM.
- **M134 minigun door gun** (Utility) — 7.62mm 6-barrel rotary; 3000-6000 RPM selectable.
- **ATGM-light rack** (Scout) — 2-missile rack; 4000 m range.

### Cargo sling (Utility)

- External cargo hook at fuselage belly; cfctl `act.player.attach_cargo_sling { entity_id }` / `act.player.release_cargo_sling`.
- Cargo mass adds to chassis mass aggregate; required lift = `(empty_mass + cargo_mass + crew_mass) × local_g`.
- Cargo pendulum oscillation at ~1.5 Hz; pilot must counter via cyclic input.
- Auto-release fires at hull integrity ≤ 25% (safety).

### Per-pilot skill curve

- Per-actor `helicopter_proficiency: 0..100` (M14A actor stat).
- 0-25 (Trainee): collective response 60% of commanded; +30% cyclic deadband; over/under-control oscillations.
- 26-50 (Operational): 90% response; small deadband.
- 51-80 (Veteran): 100% response; can autorotate to 100% rotor RPM at flare; crisp hover-IGE.
- 81-100 (Ace): 100% response + auto-snap turret slew + 1.2× recoil compensation; unlocks `nap_of_earth` autopilot mode.
- Proficiency rises +1 per 15 min flown without incident; falls -5 on crash.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Attack Helicopter spools up + lifts off
  Given an Attack Helicopter idle on a launch pad with crew seated

  And once lift_n > weight_n × local_g the helicopter rises off the pad
  And helicopter_spool_up + helicopter_lifted events fire

Scenario: Rotor blade lost causes vibration and lift loss
  Given an Attack Helicopter at 30 m altitude with 5 main-rotor blades intact
  When a 30mm AAA round impacts one blade and its HP drops to 0

  And lateral oscillation overlay activates (+0.6 m/s sway)

  And the HUD shows a red blade-status indicator on the affected disk

Scenario: Engine-out triggers the autorotation window
  Given a Utility Helicopter at 150 m altitude with engine HP > 0

  Then rotor RPM begins decaying at -2.5 rpm/s
  And the HUD shows "ENGINE OUT — AUTOROTATE" caution
  When the pilot issues act.player.engage_autorotation within 5 seconds

  And autorotation_engaged event fires

Scenario: Successful autorotation flare = survivable landing
  Given a helicopter in autorotation at 5 m AGL with rotor RPM 80%
  When the pilot issues act.player.helicopter_collective { delta: +1.0 } at flare height
  Then vertical velocity decays from -8 m/s to 0 within 1.5 s
```

## Notes for the implementer

- Rotor RPM curve is a low-order ODE; fixed-tick integrate consistent with the rest of the sim. Use the engine's deterministic RNG for blade-tip damage probability and imbalance jitter — never `thread_rng`.
- Autorotation rotor-RPM equation: `d_rpm = (descent_rate_ms × autorot_efficiency − collective_drag) × dt`. Tune `autorot_efficiency` so a typical T3 utility helicopter at 150 m has a ~12 s autorotation glide and lands survivably from the standard flare.
- Ground-effect lookup re-uses the M14A `walk_angle` terrain-pixel sampler; cache per-tick to avoid per-blade re-sampling.
- Door-gunner arc check happens at fire-event time and every 5 ticks while trigger is held; arc is a fixed cone in vehicle-local frame.
- Cargo-sling pendulum is a 2-body Verlet pair tied to the fuselage; do NOT introduce a separate physics solver. Re-use M14 ballistic integrator.
- Pilot-skill curve modifies INPUT before the sim, never sim output, so AI doctrine sees clean control signals.
- Helicopter lift uses M19 atmospheric density directly; for vacuum bodies (Moon, Mimas, Belt asteroids) `lift_n = 0` — these SKUs are atmosphere-only.
- All RNG (blade-strike probability per round, CEP scatter on Hellfire impact, autorotation flare windblast, sling-overswing tipping points) seeds off the engine's deterministic RNG.
- Tail-rotor loss recovery via forward-airspeed weathervane is a real flight-physics behaviour; preserve it because it gives skilled pilots a survivability lever.
- Mast-mounted sight on the Scout uses a virtual camera entity anchored 3 m above the rotor disk; LoS is computed from camera position, not fuselage position.
- Helicopter audio is loud and continuous — pre-mix the rotor whump loop at -6 dB headroom to avoid clipping when chain-gun fires layer in.
