# M7.8 — Crafting Tiers & Fabrication Chain

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M7.8 is the crafting tier ladder + fabrication chain milestone** — the player's progression spine for building weapons / armor / chassis / base modules / power equipment / vehicles / mods from raw materials. After M7.8, the game has a **5-tier crafting ladder** (T0 primitive → T1 industrial → T2 advanced → T3 endgame → T4 exotic), a **deterministic fabrication chain** (raw ore → refined material → component → equipment → assembly), **research-gated recipe unlocks**, **fabrication-station hierarchy** (workbench → fabricator → assembly line → smelter → enrichment reactor → exotic-matter forge), and **survival-friendly crafting UX** (Stationeers/Terraria-inspired) for both campaign and PvE-survival modes.

M7.8 promise: **"every weapon, every armor plate, every turret in your hands was forged from materials you sourced. Tier up by surviving."**

## Player-facing behavior

### 5 crafting tiers — locked launch ladder

| Tier | Theme | Materials needed | Fabrication station | Power requirement | Time per recipe | Unlock condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T0 — Primitive** | Stone-age survival | Wood / dirt / stone / hide / bone | Player hands + camp fire | None (or fire heat) | Fast (5-30s) | Start of any PvE-survival scenario; no research |
| **T1 — Industrial** | Early metallurgy + electricity | Iron / copper / coal / wood / silica | Workbench (T0-built) + Smelter (M8.6 mining) | Low (1-2 kW) | Medium (30s-2min) | Research at workbench (10 iron + 5 wood); Stationeers parity |
| **T2 — Advanced** | Steel + composites + reactor power | Steel / aluminum / titanium / electronics / advanced composites | Fabricator (T1-built) + Assembly Line + Nuclear Reactor power | Medium (5-15 kW) | Slow (2-10 min) | Research at fabricator (50 steel + 20 electronics); Factorio-tier progression |
| **T3 — Endgame** | Exotic + reactive + plasma | Plasma cells / superconductor / nanocarbon / reactive armor | Enrichment Reactor + Plasma Forge + Quantum Lab | High (50-200 kW + capacitor burst) | Very slow (10min-2hr) | Defeat M11 boss / acquire artifact / mid-campaign research |
| **T4 — Exotic** | Material-Lab unlocked (M8.5) | Modder-defined + 13 expansion materials | Exotic-Matter Forge (Material Lab integration) | Critical (1 MW+) | Endgame (2hr+) | Material Lab puzzle completion / endgame world unlock (Venus / Vulcan / etc.) |

**Locked launch invariant:** every tier MUST have at least 1 weapon, 1 armor piece, 1 chassis module, 1 tool, 1 power-generator, 1 base-object per tier. Total: 5 × 6 = **30 launch core recipes** per tier × 5 tiers = **150 recipes** at M7.8 close.

### Fabrication chain — raw to assembled

The full chain from ore to finished item:

```text
1. Raw ore (M8.6 mining)
   ↓ [Smelter station + heat from M5.9 atmospherics]
2. Refined material (iron, steel, aluminum, titanium, etc.)
   ↓ [Fabricator + power]
3. Component (electronics chip, motor, sensor, capacitor, armor plate, glass pane)
   ↓ [Assembly Line + IC10 program OR Lua script for complex items]
4. Equipment / weapon / chassis module
   ↓ [Player crafting station or Manufacturing Workbench (M8)]
5. Assembled product (chassis, vehicle, base structure)
```

**Per Stationeers parity:**
- Smelting + refining + alloying are real time + temperature + atmospheric reactions (per M5.9)
- Some recipes require specific atmospheric conditions (high-temp = volcanic; cryogenic = ice world)
- Cooling required for some endgame recipes (Mimas/Europa ambient bonus)
- Failure modes: wrong temperature = wasted material; impure ore = degraded output (rust / scrap)

### Recipe + research system

**Per Stationeers + Factorio + Terraria pattern:**

- Each recipe has: `recipe_id`, `tier`, `inputs` (per-material quantity), `outputs` (per-material quantity + condition), `station_required`, `power_required`, `time_seconds`, `temperature_k` (optional), `atmospheric_requirement` (optional), `research_required`
- Research unlocked via:
  - **Default unlocks**: T0 + basic T1 (no research needed; available at start)
  - **Workbench research**: spend research_points (earned by crafting, exploration, mission completion) on tech tree
  - **Faction unlocks**: per DR-031 faction quartermasters sell recipes
  - **Boss drops**: mini-boss + boss kills can drop rare recipe scrolls
  - **Codex archaeology**: hidden lore items grant recipe knowledge
  - **Material Lab puzzles**: M8.5 modder-authored puzzles unlock expansion recipes

### Crafting UX — Stationeers / Terraria hybrid (per DR-046 + DR-012)

**Recipe panel UI:**
- Top bar: tier filter (T0 / T1 / T2 / T3 / T4) + station filter + category filter (weapons / armor / chassis / tools / base / power / vehicles)
- Left: scrollable recipe list with rarity-color border (per M7 loot rarity matrix)
- Center: selected recipe card showing inputs (per-material count + have/need), outputs (preview), time bar, power requirement, station-availability check
- Right: queue-list (player can queue multiple crafts; auto-progresses)
- Bottom: "CRAFT" button + cost feedback + tooltip "Will require 5 iron + 2 coal + 30 seconds + 1 kW power"

**Visual feedback:**
- Color-coded inputs: green = have / yellow = partial / red = missing
- Station-availability pip: green = at station / red = move to fabricator
- Power-availability pip: green = power OK / yellow = brown-out risk / red = insufficient
- Recipe locked: greyed-out + tooltip "Requires Research: Industrial Metallurgy"

**Per DR-012 accessibility:**
- All UI text + state at 200% scale supported
- Color-independent state labels
- Captions: "Crafting started: rifle_m1 + 30s + 1 kW"
- Hold-to-confirm option for destructive recipes (smelting valuable materials)

**Terraria-friendly auto-craft:**
- Press hotkey → all available recipes shown
- Player drags inputs from inventory into auto-craft slots → automated progress
- "Craft All" for bulk operations (smelt 50 iron at once)

**Stationeers-friendly manual setup:**
- Player places individual pieces (smelter input + output + control + power connection)
- IC10 programmable: automate recipe selection based on demand
- Conveyor belts (M8.6 mining) carry materials between stations

### Power-coupled crafting

Per M7.6 integration:
- Fabricators consume power per recipe; brown-out STOPS crafting (in-progress recipe paused; resumes when power restored)
- Brown-out risk visible in recipe card: "Caution: 5 kW draw may trigger brown-out; current grid has 3 kW headroom"
- Player can queue recipes during off-peak hours (overnight when no combat)
- IC10 chip can pause / resume crafting based on grid state

### Survival-friendly progression curve (DR-023 onboarding-aligned)

T0 → T1 → T2 → T3 → T4 escalation pacing:

- **First 30 minutes (T0)**: player gathers wood + stone; builds camp fire; crafts spear; survives night
- **Hours 1-3 (T1)**: player builds workbench; mines iron; builds smelter; crafts rifle + iron armor + first solar panel
- **Hours 3-10 (T2)**: player builds fabricator; produces electronics; builds reactor; powered armor + advanced weapons
- **Hours 10-50 (T3)**: player explores other worlds; acquires titanium + plasma cells; builds mech + plasma weapons; defeats world bosses
- **Hours 50+ (T4)**: Material Lab puzzles; exotic materials; endgame combinations

### Repair + modification system

Per Cortex Command + Stationeers parity:

- Damaged items can be **repaired** at their fabrication station (consumes some materials; max repairs limited)
- Items can be **modified** with attachments (M2.2A weapon attachments + chassis modifiers M5 + 30+ Noita-style modifiers M5)
- Modifications are reversible (player can detach + reattach)
- Salvage: destroyed items can be broken down into partial materials at salvage station (75% material recovery)
