**M7.7 is the weather + day/night kernel milestone** — full dynamic weather state machine + 24-hour day/night cycle per world. After M7.7, scenarios have weather that affects gameplay (rain reduces traction, dust storms reduce visibility, lightning strikes), day/night cycles affect AI behavior + vision, and the remaining 3 worlds ship (12 total per README).

M7.7 promise: **"weather is gameplay — players adapt; bots adapt; missions feel alive with environmental drama."**

## Player-facing behavior

### 7 weather states + dynamic transitions

Per DR-040:
- `clear` — normal conditions
- `rain` — reduces traction (slides become unstable; washes blood/oil/fire)
- `storm` — rain + occasional lightning strikes (random damage in radius)
- `dust` — reduces visibility severely (sniper effective range × 0.4)
- `fog` — reduces sight range
- `snow` — cold affliction stacks; ground slippery
- `acid_rain` (on Venus) — damages exposed armor (M5.5 + M5.6 reactions)

Dynamic transitions per scenario weather_seed:
- `weather.changed` fires per state transition
- Weather can persist 30s-300s per state
- Player can observe oncoming weather (visual cues + caption)

### Day / night cycle

Per world:
- `time_of_day: f32 (0:00-23:59)` deterministic per tick
- `ambient_light_modifier` (0.3 dark night → 1.0 noon)
- Visibility range × time_of_day_modifier
- Night-vision goggles item: reduces dark penalty
- AI behavior at night: more patrolling, lower sight range
- Lighting events: dawn / dusk / midnight / noon

### 12 launch worlds shipping

Per README: 12 worlds × 3-5 biomes each.

M5.9 shipped 6; M7 added Sol-zone + Belt + Orbital (9 total). M7.7 adds:
- **Deimos** — vacuum, low gravity, ice deposits
- **Europa** — H2O ice caverns + sublimation atmosphere
- **Venus** — crushing pressure + acid rain + high heat
- Plus 3-5 biomes per world (60+ biomes across all worlds)

Biome types: tundra, desert, forest, mountains, swamp, cave, urban, industrial, ocean, ice, volcanic, abandoned_lab.

### Weather + day/night events

- `weather.changed { from, to, transition_duration }`
- `weather.lightning_struck { pos, damage }`
- `time.advanced { hour, minute }`
- `time.day_night_transition { from, to }` (dawn/dusk transitions)
