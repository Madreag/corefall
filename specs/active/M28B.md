# M28B — Base Thermal Engineering + Breach Response

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Expand M28's 10-module atmospheric roster to honor README's full thermal + breach contract: heaters / coolers / radiators / coolant loops / pressure doors / breach-repair tool / emergency vent — closes the M28 "heaters/coolers/radiators/coolant loops" prose-only gap surfaced in the materials audit.

## Player-facing behavior

- Build a 4-room base with a Cold Room (frozen food storage) using `cryocooler` + insulated walls; visible temperature gradient overlay (ONI parity).
- Reactor coolant loop: connect nuclear reactor (M29) → coolant_loop module → liquid-cooled_radiator → vacuum heat dump; failure to maintain loop = reactor overheat cascade.
- Pressure door (HP-rated 2000): holds against 200 kPa differential; fails open at 250 kPa (catastrophic decompression) or breaches at HP=0 (kinetic damage).
- Breach repair tool: handheld `vacuum_patch_kit` (T1; 4 iron + 2 plastic + 1 sealant); player walks to hole, holds [E] for 8s, hole sealed; consumes 1 patch per breach.
- Emergency vent (player-triggered): `act.player.emergency_vent` flushes a sealed room's atmosphere into vacuum/exterior in 5s; useful to extinguish base-wide fire or expel toxic gas; risk: kills any non-suited actor in the vented room.
- Visible temperature heatmap (F6 toggle); per-tile color from blue (-50°C) → green (20°C) → red (100°C); player can plan thermal layouts.

## Out of scope

- Heat-exchanger between two coolant loops (T3+ engineering; M48+ for crafting puzzle depth).
- Active reactor scram via control rod IC10 chip (M29A IC10 editor covers).
- Geothermal heat tap as a thermal module (M29 owns generator; M28B is consumer).
- Per-tile thermal radiation propagation through transparent materials (M48+ optical realism).

## Notes for the implementer

- Heater/cooler output scales with `room_volume_m3` + `insulation_quality` (sum of wall material thermal_conductivity values). A heater in an uninsulated room wastes 90% to exterior.
- Pressure door HP scales with tier: T1 = 2000 HP / 250 kPa max; T2 = 5000 HP / 600 kPa max; T3 (M48+) = 12000 HP / 1500 kPa max.
- Emergency vent has a 5-second cycle (atmosphere must physically flow through aperture). Cannot be cancelled mid-vent.
- The thermal heatmap (F6) layers on top of M28A's existing build overlays — single-key cycle Q-hold radial includes Thermal as one of the modes.
- All five new replay events MUST carry `cause_event_id` for replay-cause-chain walkability.
