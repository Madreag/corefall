# M44 — Inter-Planet Transport + Stations

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M44 is the inter-planet transport + space station + asteroid mining milestone** — the system that turns PvE Survival from a single-world game into a multi-world civilization. After M44, players can travel between planets via dropship / rocket / teleporter, build orbital stations with zero-g manufacturing bonuses, claim asteroid mining colonies, and pilot specialized vehicles (submarine for Europa / cargo freighter for inter-planetary trade / amphibious truck for Earth).

M44 splits this out of M43 because transport infrastructure is its own coherent scope distinct from PvE survival mode mechanics. M43 ships the survival mode + procgen + race difficulty; M44 ships the cross-world systems.

M44 promise: **"build your bunker on Earth; mine asteroids in the Belt; trade ore at Mars orbital station — every world becomes part of your civilization."**

## Player-facing behavior

### Inter-planet transport — 3 launch methods

Per DR-039 + spec/celestial-bodies-and-worlds-model orbital math + comms latency:

**1. Dropship (T1 — Industrial-era)**

- Player builds a dropship at base (T2 fabricator + 50 steel + 20 motor + power 10 kW + 30 min)
- Flight time = `distance_m / dropship_velocity` (1000 m/s baseline)
- Earth → Mars: 2-4 in-game hours (depends on orbital position at launch time)
- Player can pilot OR set autopilot (M23 LLM mind or scripted)
- Risk: anti-air weapons can shoot down; crash chance at landing (per DR-027 craft state); fuel consumption
- Cargo capacity: 5 actors + 10 weapons + 50 material units
- Multiple dropships can be queued / scheduled

**2. Rocket (T2 — Advanced-era)**

- Player builds a multi-stage rocket at base (T3 fabricator + nuclear engine + 500 steel + 200 titanium + 100 motor + power 100 kW + 4 hr + assembly line)
- Flight time = `flight_time = distance_km / 100 m/s` (acceleration profile from low Earth orbit; faster than dropship for inter-planetary)
- Earth → Vulcan: ~12-24 in-game hours
- Higher cargo capacity: 20 actors + 50 weapons + 500 material units
- Endgame option for true inter-planetary expansion

**3. Teleporter (T3 — Endgame; requires both endpoints + paired)**

- Player builds two teleporter pads (T3 fabricator + 100 titanium + 50 superconductor + 30 plasma_cell + 20 capacitor + power 50 kW + 8 hr + plasma forge)
- Pads must be **paired** (player marks one as "anchor" and other as "destination")
- Instant transport (1-tick) between paired pads
- Power cost per transport: 50 kWh per actor (large drain)
- Cannot transport items > 100 kg (player + small inventory only; not cargo)
- Pad cooldown: 30s between transports
- Endgame requirement: paired pads must be built on the destination world FIRST (so dropship is still required for initial expansion)

**Comms latency during transport:**
- Per DR-039: Earth ↔ Mars comms latency 6-44 minutes round-trip
- During dropship/rocket flight: player loses real-time base awareness (HUD shows "BASE COMMS DELAY 12 min")
- Teleporter is instant; comms instant on arrival

### Inter-planet travel — detailed flight mechanics

#### Dropship pilot mechanics (per M25 craft state extension)

**Pre-flight checks:**
- Fuel level: must be > 20% for safe flight (consumed in proportion to distance)
- Engine integrity: > 50%; below = crash risk
- Heat shield: must be intact for atmospheric re-entry
- Mass limit: cargo + passengers ≤ dropship capacity (5 actors + 10 weapons + 50 material units)
- Pre-flight system check: 5-tick checklist (player can skip but risks crash)

**Flight phases:**

1. **Take-off** (0-60s): vertical ascent; vulnerable to anti-air; consumes 20% fuel
2. **Orbital insertion** (60-300s): rocket burn into orbit; trajectory calculation via M19 + DR-038 gravity
3. **Inter-planetary cruise** (1-8 in-game hours): coast through space; passive comms delay applies
4. **Mid-course correction** (optional): consumes fuel; corrects trajectory for orbital changes (per DR-039 astrography)
5. **Deceleration burn**: approaching destination; consumes 20% fuel
6. **Re-entry** (if landing on planet with atmosphere): heat shield active; pulse buildup; need throttling
7. **Landing** (if planet) OR **docking** (if station): risk + skill check
8. **Cargo unloading**: actors + cargo deployed to LZ

**Risk events during flight:**

- **Engine fire** (5% chance per hour): heat damage; can be repaired or scuttle
- **Pirate intercept** (variable; depends on faction state): enemy ship spawns
- **Solar flare** (per M45 world event): all comms blackout; flight aborts to nearest world
- **Asteroid impact** (low chance): collision damage + course correction needed
- **Mechanical failure** (depends on engine integrity): crash risk
- **Successful landing**: 0.95-0.99 base chance; modified by skill + fuel + heat shield + weather

**Cargo capacity progression:**
- T1 dropship: 5 actors + 10 weapons + 50 material units; mass 5000 kg
- T2 dropship (improved): 8 actors + 20 weapons + 100 material units; mass 8000 kg
- T3 dropship (heavy lifter): 15 actors + 50 weapons + 300 material units; mass 15000 kg

#### Rocket inter-planetary flight (T2+)

**Multi-stage rocket** (per Kerbal-style mechanics; simplified):

- **Stage 1 (lift-off)**: vertical ascent; 5 minutes; consumes 60% fuel
- **Stage 2 (orbital injection)**: low orbit + circularize; 3 minutes
- **Stage 3 (trans-orbit injection)**: trajectory toward target world; 8-24 in-game hours
- **Stage 4 (capture burn)**: orbital insertion at target world
- **Stage 5 (descent)**: landing per dropship rules above

Rocket has limited inter-planetary range (depends on fuel + destination orbit):
- T2 rocket: Earth ↔ Mars OR Earth ↔ Moon ↔ Phobos cluster
- T3 rocket: full solar system access (Earth ↔ any world)
- T4 rocket (post-launch): inter-solar travel reserved for future

#### Teleporter pad pairing + cost mechanics (T3)

**Pair setup:**
1. Build pad A on world X (cost 100 titanium + 50 superconductor + 30 plasma cell + 20 capacitor; power 50 kW; 8 hr fabrication)
2. Build pad B on world Y (same cost; player must travel via dropship first to build it)
3. Pair pads via cfctl: `act.player.pair_teleporter <pad_a_id> <pad_b_id>`
4. Pads sync; cooldown begins

**Per transport:**
- Cost: 50 kWh per actor per transport
- Cooldown: 30s between transports
- Cannot transport items > 100 kg (player + small inventory only)
- Cannot transport between worlds with > 5 AU distance (signal loss)
- Cannot transport during solar flare (comms blackout)
- Failure mode: 0.1% chance per transport; transports player + materials disappear into "limbo" (recoverable via repair tool at either pad)

### Space stations + orbital bases

Per DR-039 + DR-035:

- **Orbital station** (per celestial spec representative class) can be built as a small base
- Built via T3 fabricator + special "orbital launch" recipe (300 steel + 100 nuclear_cell + power 1 MW + 24 hr)
- Once built, becomes a persistent base in orbit around a planet
- Player can dock dropships at the station
- Station provides:
  - Zero-g manufacturing (efficiency +20% for some recipes)
  - Comm relay (reduces comms delay to surface bases)
  - Mining drone bay (auto-mines belt asteroids in same orbital zone)
  - Defense turrets covering nearby asteroids
- Risks:
  - Asteroid impact (storm equivalent for stations)
  - Pirate raids
  - Atmospheric loss if breached (sealed life support)
  - Solar storm radiation

#### Orbital station construction

**Built via dropship + rocket fleet:**

1. Build "Orbital Foundation Kit" at base (recipe T3; cost 300 steel + 100 nuclear_cell + power 1 MW + 24 hr)
2. Load onto rocket; launch to orbit
3. Once in orbit: rocket auto-assembles foundation (10 minutes)
4. Player can dock dropship + add modules via assembly process
5. Each module = separate cargo load + assembly time
6. Per-station modules:
   - Solar arrays (massive; 100 kW each)
   - Mining drone bay (auto-mines belt asteroids)
   - Defensive turret arrays
   - Habitation pods
   - Hangar (dropship landing)
   - Comm relay (reduces planet-side comms delay)
   - Living quarters
   - Research lab (zero-g manufacturing bonus +20%)

**Failure modes:**
- Asteroid impact (random; mitigated by defensive turrets)
- Solar storm radiation (penalty to crew)
- Atmospheric breach (sealed via airlocks; player can repair)
- Power failure cascade (M29 reactor; cascading shutdowns)

### Asteroid mining colonies

- Belt asteroids (per DR-039 representative class) can be claimed as small bases
- Player builds drilling rig + ore extractor + cargo bay + thrusters
- Each asteroid has unique ore composition (1-3 ore types; rarer at outer belt)
- Asteroids slowly drift in orbital path (M19 + DR-038 gravity model)
- Auto-extraction via drone bots (per M13 drone allies)
- Player picks up cargo via dropship periodic visits

**Setup:**
1. Player surveys belt for asteroids (orbital map + sensor pulse)
2. Selects asteroid; checks composition + size
3. Builds drilling rig in cargo bay + transports via rocket
4. Asteroid claimed (per faction territory rules)
5. Drilling rig auto-extracts; ore goes to cargo bay
6. Periodic dropship pickup brings ore home

**Asteroid types:**
- Carbonaceous (C-type): water + organic compounds; low metal
- Silicaceous (S-type): silicon + iron + magnesium; balanced
- Metallic (M-type): pure iron + nickel + platinum; high value
- Rare-earth (post-launch): titanium + lithium + cobalt + earth-elements
- Exotic (T4; post-launch): superconductor materials; very rare

**Asteroid orbital drift:**
- Each asteroid follows simplified Keplerian path (DR-039 astrography)
- Player must compute intercept window for dropship docking
- Asteroid drift over months can move it out of player's optimal range
- Asteroid can be moved with thrusters (T3 player-built thruster ring on asteroid)

### Vehicle tank progression (Stationeers + ONI combined)

Vehicles require tanks per M17 + M44 integration. Tank tiers defined canonically in M19 § Gas tank inventory system.

#### Orbital shuttle
- Fuel tank (combustion)
- Crew O2 tank (1 hr capacity)
- Cargo: 20 material units
- Inter-orbit travel only (no inter-planetary)
- Required research: T2 rocket tech

#### Mech walker (T2 vehicle)
- Pilot O2 tank
- Cooling tank (M19 coolant)
- Oil reservoir
- Battery (electric variant)
- Variant types: light (jet) / medium / heavy

#### Cargo freighter (T3 vehicle)
- Massive fuel tank
- Crew quarters with closed-loop life support
- Cargo: 500 material units
- Inter-planetary capable
- Required research: T3 rocket tech

```gherkin

    Then rocket.stage_1_liftoff fires (5 min)

    Then rejected with reason="no_destination_pad"

    Then teleporter.activated fires
```
