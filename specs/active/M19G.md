# M19G — Room ↔ Tile Atmospheric Bridge + Hybrid Sim Strategy

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Canonical ownership of the **hybrid atmospheric simulation strategy**: Stationeers-style PV=nRT room atmospheres for performance/sealed-room readability + ONI-style per-tile single-element model for outdoor + cave + breach + active-combat zones — with explicit handshake protocols at room/tile boundaries (apertures / doors / breaches / open boundaries).

## Player-facing behavior

- A sealed bunker = room atmosphere (single PV=nRT bag). Performance + simple visualization. Open the door → atmosphere flows into the tile-grid outside per ONI aperture flow.
- Outdoor terrain = per-tile element. Walk outside → wind reads per-tile gas + temp. Stand at the door threshold → both models talk.
- Cave systems = per-tile (M3B handles). Mine a tunnel into a sealed room → tile-side breaches the room boundary → room atmosphere starts to leak per-tile.
- The player NEVER sees the boundary explicitly — both look like one game. The game implements two solvers + the bridge.
- When a tile becomes part of a sealed room (player walls it off), tile → room handshake aggregates the tile's mass into the room atmosphere.
- When a room is breached (terrain mutation or explosion), room → tile handshake distributes the room atmosphere across the new perimeter tiles.

## The hybrid strategy decision tree (locked)

For each tile per tick:

```
if tile is part of a sealed room (M19 room-aggregated atmosphere):
    sim_mode = PerRoom
    state lives in: room.atmosphere (M19 PV=nRT)
    
elif tile is in active-region (player nearby OR active combat OR active dig):
    sim_mode = PerTile (M3B)
    state lives in: tile_element_buffer

elif tile is in dormant outdoor:
    sim_mode = PerTile (M3B)
    state lives in: tile_element_buffer (low-cadence updates per active region)

elif tile is in vacuum boundary:
    sim_mode = PerTile (M3B) with vacuum constraint
```

When boundary detection fires (mining a wall / door opens / placing a wall / explosion):
- **Room → Tile**: room atmosphere decompresses into perimeter tiles per ONI aperture flow.
- **Tile → Room**: enclosed area sums per-tile mass → room atmosphere instantiated.

## ONI aperture flow formula (locked)

```
flow_kg_per_tick = aperture_area_m2 × 
                   density_diff_kg_per_m3 × 
                   wind_velocity_m_per_s × 
                   dt_seconds

where:
- aperture_area: 1.0 m² per fully-open tile (1 tile = 1 m × 1 m in ONI scale)
- density_diff: |room.density - tile.density|; sign drives direction
- wind_velocity: bounded by aperture diameter; ~5 m/s max per ONI mass-transport math
```

Choked-flow cap: when pressure differential exceeds 60 kPa, wind velocity caps at the speed-of-sound boundary; above that, flow is mass-limited not pressure-limited.

## Per-wall passive heat conduction (closed-boundary case — THE core ONI/Stationeers gameplay loop)

Even with no aperture (intact wall), **heat flows through every wall tile** between a sealed room (M19 PV=nRT atmosphere with one `T_room`) and the adjacent per-tile environment (M3B). Mass is blocked by the intact wall; **heat is not**. Without this, a Mimas base at -180°C ambient stays cozy at 20°C forever for free — breaking Stationeers/ONI parity.

### The contract

Each per-tick boundary pass, for every wall tile that separates a room interior from a per-tile exterior:

```
Q_through_wall_w = (k_wall × A_tile_m2 × (T_outside_k - T_room_k)) / wall_thickness_m

where:
- k_wall = material's `thermal_conductivity_w_per_m_k` (per M3B element registry)
  - Concrete: 1.5 W/m·K
  - Steel: 50 W/m·K
  - Foam insulation: 0.04 W/m·K
  - Vacuum / void: 0.0 (no heat flow; ONI thermos rule)
- A_tile_m2 = 1.0 (1 tile = 1 m × 1 m in ONI scale)
- wall_thickness_m = 0.5 (1 tile of wall material; modder-extensible per wall blueprint)
- T_outside_k = adjacent per-tile temperature (M3B)
- T_room_k = sealed-room atmosphere temperature (M19)
```

The room's thermal mass absorbs/releases this heat:

```
ΔT_room_per_tick_k = (Σ Q_through_wall_w × dt_seconds) /
                     (room.atmosphere_mass_kg × room.specific_heat_capacity_j_per_kg_k)
```

### Per-wall heat-transfer cap (matches M3B per-tile cap)

`Q_per_wall_tile_per_tick_j ≤ 10 J` (deterministic ceiling; prevents thermal flash from a single tick of extreme ΔT).

### Per-wall passive direction-aware behavior

- **Hot room → cold outside**: room loses heat through walls; without active heater, room cools toward outside ambient.
- **Cold room → hot outside**: room gains heat through walls; without active cooler, room warms toward outside ambient.
- **Equal temperatures**: zero net flow; equilibrium maintained.
- **Vacuum boundary (k=0)**: zero flow → perfect insulation → ONI thermos design works.
- **Insulated wall (k=0.04)**: 37× slower than concrete → player engineers insulated walls to fight ambient.

### Per-tick accumulation

The per-wall heat-flow pass runs at the M19G `boundary_detection` cadence (every N=4 ticks); accumulated `Q_through_walls_w` summed across all wall tiles per room → updated `T_room` per heat-flow tick. This amortizes the per-wall cost while keeping the room thermally responsive.

### Per-actor consequence

When `T_room` drifts outside per-actor comfort (per origin; M17), per-room comfort-affliction tie-in (M16A) fires. Cold room (T_room < 0°C for human) → hypothermia accumulator starts. Hot room (T_room > 49°C) → heatstroke. Player decides: insulate, heat/cool, or evacuate.

## Out of scope

- 3D atmospheric volume aggregation — Corefall is 2D; this is the canonical 2D bridge.
- Per-tile humidity (water vapor as mass within gas tile) — M19F owns humidity per-room.
- Per-tile decor / morale / germs / light — M19H owns those layers.
- Real-time room reshape detection beyond simple aperture scan — wall changes only.

## Notes for the implementer

- This is the **canonical hybrid policy** — every other atmospherics-related milestone reads this decision tree.
- Boundary detection runs at 1/N cadence (N=4 ticks default); aperture flow at every tick.
- The handshake direction is asymmetric: room → tile pours mass into perimeter tiles; tile → room sums mass.
- Choked-flow cap prevents single-tick decompression-to-zero on huge breaches (ONI realism).
- Determinism: boundary detection iterates chunks in (cx, cy) order; handshake events fire in deterministic order.
