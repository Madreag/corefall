# M22C — Sound Propagation Per-Material Acoustic Dampening + Footstep Hearing + Per-Actor Hearing Sensitivity

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Promote M6's clean distance-attenuated hearing kernel into a **full sound-propagation simulation** with per-material acoustic dampening, per-tile signal accumulation via BFS through M3B's per-tile element model, per-actor hearing sensitivity modulated by M16 afflictions (deafened / heightened / blinded compensation), and a unified AI listener layer that reads accumulated `signal_db` per actor per source — wiring M37 chatter, M14 gunfire, footsteps, and ambient hazard noise through the same propagation kernel so a flanker around the corner of a concrete wall actually loses the signal, a gunshot in a metal duct echoes through the whole base, and a deafened actor can be flanked silently.

M22C promise: **"sound is a real field — every wall thickens it, every duct carries it, and every actor hears it through their own ears."**

## Player-facing behavior

- **Per-material acoustic dampening.** Every entry in `material_registry.json` gains an `acoustic_dampening_db` field (decibels lost per tile traversal). Air ~0.1 dB/tile, sand ~3 dB/tile, wood ~6 dB/tile, concrete ~14 dB/tile, lead ~22 dB/tile, vacuum-tile = `INFINITY` (no transmission). M3B per-tile mass scales the table: `effective_dampening = base_dampening_db * (mass_kg / canonical_tile_mass)`. A breached concrete wall (mass 5 of 80) loses ~95% of its dampening.
- **BFS dampening accumulation.** Each emitter (gunshot, footstep, voice, hazard arc) seeds a `SoundEmission { origin_tile, source_db, frequency_band, duration_ticks }`. The propagation kernel runs a budgeted BFS outward from origin; per step the accumulated `signal_db = source_db - cumulative_distance_attenuation_db - cumulative_material_dampening_db`. Termination threshold = 10 dB above the loudest listener's `hearing_floor_db` (clamped at 20 dB world floor). Budget ≤1.5 ms p99 for 30 concurrent emitters on Steam Deck.
- **Per-actor accumulated signal layer.** Every actor has a per-tick `HearingState { sources: Vec<HeardSource> }`, where each `HeardSource = { source_id, signal_db, bearing_deg, frequency_band, source_kind }`. The listener layer ranks sources by `signal_db - hearing_floor_db` and routes top-N (default 4) into BotMemory + M7 AI utility scorer. Replay records `perception.heard_source` per tick per actor.
- **Per-actor hearing sensitivity.** Default `hearing_floor_db = 30`. Modifiers compose multiplicatively / additively:
  - `Deafened` affliction (M16): floor +60 dB (effectively silent for 30s ringing-ears + 60s recovery curve).
  - `Heightened-Senses` trait (M7): floor −10 dB.
  - `Blinded` affliction (M16) compensates: floor −5 dB (brain shifts attention to ears).
  - Helmet sealed (M13 chassis): floor +8 dB.
  - Ear-protection equipment (active gunfire muff): floor +20 dB at frequency_band=gunshot, +0 elsewhere.
  - Per-origin: Sound-Sensitive origins (Aqueous, Methane Breather) get −4 dB; Crystalline (resonant body) gets −6 dB.
- **Pipe / duct / vent transmission graph.** M3B base-module tiles tagged `acoustic_conduit = true` (steel duct, ventilation pipe, crawl-tube) form a parallel graph where dampening is much lower (~0.3 dB/tile) but signal exits at every grate / vent into the local tile. A gunshot in the boiler room travels the duct loop and audibly emerges 30 tiles away at the kitchen vent. The kernel runs the conduit-graph BFS in parallel to the tile BFS; the listener layer takes the max of the two paths per (source, listener) pair.
- **Per-source frequency band.** Emissions tag a `FrequencyBand` (gunshot / footstep / voice / mechanical / hazard). Bands attenuate differently: low-freq mechanical penetrates concrete easier (−40% dampening), high-freq footsteps are easier to localize (bearing accuracy ±5° vs ±15° for low-freq). AI uses the band tag to filter (a `Sniper` listens for footsteps; a `Spotter` listens for voice).
- **M37 chatter routed through propagation.** Every M37 voice line emits a `SoundEmission { source_kind: Voice, frequency_band: Voice, source_db: 55 }` at the speaker's tile. Listeners receive the accumulated `signal_db`; AI BT consumes it for "enemy voice heard" triggers. Radio voice bypasses propagation (M37 radio kernel owns that path).
- **Footstep emission integrated.** M6's `footstep_loudness` + `SurfaceKind` is preserved; per-surface base_db table lives in `material_registry.json` (carpet 28 dB, wood 42 dB, metal grating 52 dB, dry leaves 48 dB, broken glass 58 dB). The footstep emitter feeds the propagation kernel like any other source.
- **Sustained emitters (machinery, hazards).** Continuous noise (generator, electric arc tile, fire) emit per second; the kernel coalesces same-source-same-tile emissions and keeps a `decaying_signal_field` cached per chunk for cheap reads.
- **Replay-deterministic.** BFS visits in stable tile-id order; conduit-graph traversal in stable conduit-id order; per-actor source ranking is a stable sort by `(signal_db DESC, source_id ASC)`; no `thread_rng`, no `HashMap` default hasher.
- **AI utility integration.** M7 archetypes consume `HearingState.top_sources()` in the existing perception layer. `Sniper` priority on Voice; `Assault` priority on Gunshot bearing; `Engineer` priority on Mechanical (likely a saw / drill = target of opportunity); `Patrol` archetype on any source above floor.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Per-material dampening attenuates signal across a concrete wall
  Given an emitter at A with source_db=140 (gunshot)
  And a 4-tile concrete wall (mass_kg=80/tile) between A and a listener at B
  When the propagation kernel runs
  Then the listener receives signal_db ≈ 140 − distance_attenuation − (4 × 14) = source - 56
  And the perception.heard_source event records signal_db within ±1 dB of analytic prediction
  And the listener's hearing_floor_db=30 still detects the source (signal above floor)

Scenario: Breached wall loses dampening proportional to mass
  Given the same concrete wall now breached to mass_kg=5/tile (M3 dirty-region applied)
  When the propagation kernel re-runs
  Then per-tile dampening drops to 14 × (5/80) ≈ 0.88 dB/tile
  And the listener's signal_db is ~52 dB higher than the unbreached case
  And cf-perception's per-chunk cache invalidates on terrain_dirty_region_batch

Scenario: Duct graph carries gunshot 30 tiles around corners
  Given a gunshot at the boiler room
  And a steel-duct conduit chain from boiler → kitchen vent (30 tiles, dampening 0.3 dB/tile)
  And the tile-graph path is occluded by 6 concrete walls (cumulative 84 dB dampening)
  When the propagation kernel runs both graphs
  Then the conduit path delivers signal_db = 140 − distance_atten − (30 × 0.3) = ~120 dB
  And the tile path delivers signal_db ≈ 56 dB (much lower)
  And the listener layer takes the max → reports 120 dB with source_kind=Gunshot
  And replay.duct_transmission fires with the conduit chain id

Scenario: Per-actor hearing floor reflects Deafened affliction
  Given an actor with hearing_floor_db=30 (default)

  Then hearing_sensitivity_changed fires with new_floor_db=90
  And subsequent sources with signal_db < 90 do NOT appear in perception.heard_source
  And after 30s the floor decays back to 60, then to 30 over the next 60s
```

## Out of scope

- 3D sound (Z-layer floors / ceilings) — M22C is 2D; vertical conduit transmission ships only via explicit conduit chains tagged in M3B base-module data.
- Doppler shift / pitch modulation — purely audio-rendering concern; M22C delivers `signal_db + bearing + band` only.
- Stereo HRTF localization — M37A's audio renderer owns the player-side audio mix; M22C is sim-only.
- Echo / reverb tail rendering — audio mix concern, not sim.
- Per-listener time-of-arrival delay (sound travels slower than light) — forward-compat field reserved; default zero.
- LLM-driven AI interpretation of heard sources — M23 / M23B mind layer; M22C only delivers signal_db + bearing + band.
- Voice-line content / TTS generation — M37 / M37A own that pipeline.
- Radio propagation kernel — M37 / M37B own that pipeline; M22C voice path is for proximity-only voice.
- Hearing sensitivity from drugs (focus stim, etc.) — M16C psych meds spec; M22C only consumes the per-actor modifier value.

## Notes for the implementer

- BFS frontier is a `BinaryHeap` keyed by `(signal_db DESC, tile_id ASC)`; pop until `signal_db < global_min_listener_floor − 10`.
- Conduit graph is per-chunk-bounded; build at chunk-load + invalidate on M3B base-module placement / removal.
- Per-chunk signal cache key = `(chunk_id, source_kind, frequency_band, terrain_revision)`; LRU evict when chunk count > 64.
- Material dampening is `Vec<f32>` indexed by interned `MaterialId`, not `HashMap`.
- Per-actor `hearing_floor_db` is recomputed only on affliction / equipment / origin change (not per-tick).
- Frequency-band attenuation is `[f32; 5]` per material (Gunshot / Footstep / Voice / Mechanical / Hazard), inlined into the per-tile traversal.
- Top-N source ranking uses a fixed-size min-heap of 4 entries; allocate-free on the hot path.
- Sustained emitters use `decaying_signal_field` cached per chunk; invalidate on chunk dirty or emitter cessation.
- `cf-perception` is a sim crate — inherit M8A invariants exactly (no `thread_rng()`, no default-hashed `HashMap`, no `f64` in propagation math).
- Bearing computed via `atan2(target.y - listener.y, target.x - listener.x)`; quantize to band's bearing accuracy *only* in the replay event, not in the underlying signal.
- Replay event `heard_source` includes `(tick, actor_id, source_id, signal_db_q8, bearing_deg_q8, band)` — quantize floats for bit-exact replay.
- No `unwrap()` on cfctl-driven emit_sound; clamp source_db to `[0, 200]`.
