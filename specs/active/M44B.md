# M44B — Drone Logistics + Cargo Convoy AI

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Ship the autonomous-cargo-convoy + drone-logistics layer that connects mining sites (M30/M35) to base storage (M28C) to dropship loading (M44) via auto-pathing vehicles + IC10-programmed routes + drone-swarm coordination — the autonomous-base-logistics that turns Corefall into Factorio-scale supply networks.

## Player-facing behavior

- **Cargo convoy**: 2-5 electric ATVs or mining haulers (M44A) on a defined route; auto-pilots between waypoints; reuses fuel/recharge at every base they visit.
- **Drone swarm**: tethered repeater drones (M37B) + mining drones (M35 AutoExtractor) + cargo skids (M44A) coordinate via IC10 messaging.
- **Route designer**: place waypoints on world map; convoys follow per-route schedule + priority; player can override with manual control.
- **Loadout per vehicle**: per-vehicle cargo manifest; drone auto-loads from stockpile per category match.
- **Convoy collision avoidance**: convoy AI uses M22 pathfinder + checks for obstacles ahead; brakes/halts on conflict.
- **Anti-grief security**: convoys can carry escorts (M22 squad members ride along); enemy intercepts trigger combat + reinforcement requests.
- **Per-convoy metrics**: throughput per hour + uptime + breakdown report; the player optimizes (Factorio-style).

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Player creates a route between mine + refinery
  Given a mine site at [200, 50] + refinery at [800, 50]
  When player uses route designer + creates "mine_to_refinery" route with 4 waypoints
  Then route_created event fires
  And route data persists in content/routes/
  And player can assign convoys to this route

Scenario: Convoy auto-runs the route + delivers cargo
  Given a "mine_to_refinery" route + dispatched convoy of 2 mining haulers
  When 30 minutes elapse
  Then convoy travels: mine → hauler 1 loads → drives to refinery → unloads
  And convoy_arrived events fire at each waypoint
  And refinery cargo bin fills as expected
  And per-hauler battery recharges during refinery dock time

Scenario: Convoy AI avoids collisions
  Given 3 convoys converging on same route segment
  When the AI detects path conflict
  Then convoys queue + take turns through the contested segment
  And per-convoy speed slightly reduces to enable safe passage
  And no convoy.collision events fire (deterministic + safe)

Scenario: Enemy intercepts convoy + triggers combat
  Given a route through enemy territory + dispatched convoy
  When enemy AI detects convoy
  Then convoy_intercept_engaged event fires
  And convoy halts; escort AI engages enemies
  And player can request reinforcements (M25 commander AI escalation)
  And mission completes when convoy reaches destination or is destroyed

Scenario: IC10 chip-controlled convoy route customization
  Given a player writes an IC10 chip for "convoy_priority_high_ore"
  And the chip routes cargo to a different destination based on cargo content
  When the convoy carries gold ore (high priority)
  Then chip routes it to vault storage instead of standard stockpile
  And player gains automation depth (Factorio-tier programmability)

Scenario: Convoy breakdown recovery
  Given a convoy vehicle's battery dies mid-route
  When convoy.breakdown fires
  Then HUD shows warning + map marker
  And player can dispatch a recovery convoy with spare battery
  And after recovery, convoy resumes from broken-down position

Scenario: Per-convoy metrics summary
  Given a convoy that has run 8 hours of operation
  When player opens convoy panel
  Then convoy_metric_summary shows: trips_completed=42, cargo_delivered_kg=10500, uptime_pct=87%, average_speed_m_s=8.2
  And player can optimize: "add 1 more vehicle to increase throughput"

Scenario: Convoy security via escort squad
  Given a convoy carrying high-value cargo (gold ore)
  And player assigns squad of 4 soldiers as escort
  When the convoy moves
  Then escorts ride along (per M14A walking on vehicle)
  And on intercept, escorts engage enemies (M22 squad doctrine)
  And convoy survival rate is significantly higher with escort

Scenario: Determinism across full convoy chain
  Given two engines running m44b_full_supply_chain with same seed
  When 24 hours of convoy operation elapse
  Then identical cargo delivery sequence
  And identical SaveBlob.checksum at tick 5,184,000
```

## Out of scope

- Inter-world cargo (cross-planet convoy) — that's M44 dropship + M44 rocket; M44B is intra-world only.
- Adaptive route learning (AI learns better routes from observed performance) — M48+ AI.
- Public auction / trade of convoy services to other factions — M48 covert ops.
- 3D convoy formation (V-formation; column; line abreast) — M48+ AI doctrine.

- M24 AI environmental competence (active): convoy doctrine reads environmental signals

## Notes for the implementer

- Route follows M22 pathfinder's hierarchical A* — convoy AI re-paths if obstacles detected mid-route.
- Per-convoy IC10 chip slot for advanced behavior customization; default chip handles basic routing.
- Convoy throughput metric is the player's optimization target — Factorio-tier visibility into supply chain.
- Per-convoy escort routing reuses M22 squad escort behavior.
- Determinism: convoy decisions seeded; pathfinder deterministic; no random "patrol jitter" in route follow.
