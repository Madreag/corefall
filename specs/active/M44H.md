# M44H — Fighter Jets + Ground Attack + Bomber + VTOL Combat Aircraft + Recon Drones

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M44H is the fixed-wing combat aircraft + VTOL + recon-drone milestone.** M44G closed the rotor-wing gap (helicopters). M44B shipped cargo drones for logistics. No spec has ever owned **how a turbofan spools differently from a turbojet, how a ramjet refuses to start below Mach 0.6 inlet speed, how an AESA radar paints a target while simultaneously broadcasting the painter's location to every RWR in 200 km, how an AIM-120 transitions from inertial mid-course to active terminal, how an A-10's GAU-8 produces enough recoil to visibly pitch the airframe up, how a B-1's variable-geometry wings sweep from cruise to dash, how an F-35B's vectored-thrust nozzles transition hover→forward without a stall hole, how an ACES II ejection seat behaves at Mach 1.1 at sea level versus 30000 m and 0 airspeed, how a tailhook engages cable #2 on a pitching carrier deck, or how a probe-and-drogue refueling link drops on excessive relative motion.** M44H closes that gap.

After M44H, the player builds a Fighter (F-16-equivalent) for air-superiority CAP, a Ground Attack jet (A-10-equivalent) for close air support, a Bomber (B-1B-equivalent) for standoff strike, and a VTOL (Harrier / F-35B-equivalent) for carrier ops + austere-field operation; the player also fields three classes of recon drone (Micro quadcopter / MALE Reaper-class / Stealth Strike) that are explicitly NOT M44B cargo drones — different chassis registry, different AI doctrine, different mission palette.

M44H's player-facing promise: **"radar means risk. Active emission paints you. Going supersonic at sea level rattles ground crews. Ejecting at 1080 px/s breaks your spine. Carrier landings hit cable #2 or you bolter. Mid-course datalinked AIM-120s extend NEZ. A-10 GAU-8 strafe runs feel exactly as nasty as they should. VTOL transition has a stall hole if you mis-time it. Recon drones are not cargo drones — they spawn from a different pipeline and they fly differently."**

## Player-facing behavior

### Four manned aircraft SKUs

- **Fighter (F-16-equivalent, T4)** — 2400 steel + 600 titanium + 200 motor + turbofan + radar_pd + ejection seat + 1× T4 battery + 24 hr + T4 fabricator. Single-seat air-superiority. Turbofan with afterburner. Max airspeed 1100 px/s (subsonic + supersonic toggle). Pulse-doppler radar (lookdown/shootdown). 4× AIM-9 + 4× AIM-120 + 1× M61 20mm gun (500-round belt). Armor 12 / 6 / 3 mm — energy through speed, not plate. Hull HP 700.
- **Ground Attack (A-10-equivalent, T4)** — 1800 steel + 200 titanium + 160 motor + 2× turbofan + 1× T4 battery + 18 hr + T4 fabricator. Single-seat close air support. Twin turbofan. Max airspeed 480 px/s (slow on purpose). 30mm GAU-8 chin gun (1350 rounds). 8× AGM-65 Maverick + 4× AIM-9 (self-defense). Titanium "bathtub" pilot cockpit (60 mm front armor). Hull HP 1500 — built to survive.
- **Bomber (B-1B-equivalent, T4+)** — 6000 steel + 800 titanium + 400 motor + 4× turbofan + 1× T4 battery + 48 hr + T4 fabricator. 4-crew (pilot / co-pilot / WSO / DSO). Variable-geometry wings (sweep angle commanded for cruise vs dash). 3 internal bays = 24× Mk-82 GP bombs OR 8× AGM-158 standoff cruise OR 12× thermobaric ordnance. No air-to-air. Hull HP 2400.
- **VTOL (Harrier / F-35B-equivalent, T4)** — 2800 steel + 600 titanium + 220 motor + vectored-turbofan + radar_aesa + ejection seat + 1× T4 battery + 30 hr + T4 fabricator. Single-seat multirole. Vectored-thrust nozzles (4 modes: takeoff / hover / transition / forward). VTOL from any rough field OR carrier deck. Max forward airspeed 700 px/s. 1× GAU-12 25mm gun + 4× AAM + 4× AGM mixed. Hull HP 900. Hover endurance limited by 3× cruise fuel burn.

### Three recon-drone SKUs (distinct from M44B cargo drones)

- **Micro Recon Drone (T1)** — 30 steel + 5 motor + 1× T2 battery + 2 hr + T1 fabricator. Electric quadcopter, 40 cm. EO + thermal camera. Range 2 km from operator. Endurance 25 min. Hull HP 30. Pilot-controlled OR M23 LLM mind. Built for over-the-ridge peek.
- **MALE Drone (Reaper-equivalent, T3)** — 400 steel + 80 motor + small turbofan + 1× T4 battery + 14 hr + T3 fabricator. Medium-Altitude Long-Endurance fixed-wing. Satellite-linked. Endurance 18 hr. 2 hardpoints (2× AGM-114 Hellfire OR 4× small-diameter bombs). Ground-station control. Hull HP 200.
- **Stealth Strike Drone (RQ-170 / X-47B-equivalent, T4)** — 800 steel + 300 titanium + 120 motor + turbofan + 1× T4 battery + 30 hr + T4 fabricator + plasma forge. Fixed-wing low-observable. Range 4000 m operator OR full autonomy. 2 internal bays for AAM or PGM. Passive-only sensors by default (active radar reveals the drone). Hull HP 450. RCS reduction multiplier reduces hostile-radar detection.

### Per-aircraft engine subsystem

- **Turbojet** — high specific thrust, fuel-hungry, no bypass; used by early jets and ramjet-equipped cruise weapons.
- **Turbofan** — bypass air for fuel efficiency; afterburner adds ~1.8× thrust at ~3× fuel rate; default for Fighter / Ground Attack / Bomber / VTOL.
- **Ramjet** — compression via inlet speed only; works above Mach 0.6 inlet speed; below threshold = no thrust + flameout; used by AGM-158 cruise + later hypersonic SKUs.
- Engine subsystem owns: `thrust_max_n`, `sfc_kg_per_n_s` (specific fuel consumption), `afterburner_thrust_n`, `inlet_min_mach` (ramjet only), `spool_time_s`, `compressor_stall_threshold` (debris ingestion = stall + flameout).

### Per-component damage on aircraft (reuses M44C cascade)

Each aircraft component — engine bays (1-4 per SKU), fuel cells, hydraulic lines, flight-control surfaces (ailerons / rudder / elevator), radar antenna, ejection seat rocket, tailhook actuator, vectored-thrust nozzle, internal-bay door, wing-sweep actuator — has its own HP separate from hull HP. **All component HP / degradation / cascade-failure logic reuses M44C `component_damage`** — do NOT fork. M44H authors only the per-SKU component layouts (which components exist on which body graph) and per-component thresholds; the damage cascade machinery (penetrating-hit → component HP → cascade → vehicle-state effect) is unchanged from M44C. Examples:

- Hydraulic line damaged → roll / pitch / yaw authority degrades (flight-control surface response lag rises proportionally to remaining hydraulic HP).
- Single engine destroyed on Bomber → 3 remaining engines limp the airframe home at 60% max thrust.
- Both Ground Attack engines destroyed → engine-out / glide / eject decision per M44G autorotation cousin (no autorotation for fixed-wing — glide-only).
- Radar antenna destroyed → `radar_mode` forced to `off` regardless of cfctl input; observe.aircraft.radar_mode reports `disabled`.
- Vectored-thrust nozzle stuck (VTOL) → nozzle angle frozen at last commanded value; pilot may still recover if frozen in `Forward` mode.

### Radar

- Per-aircraft `radar_mode: off | passive | active_search | active_track | stt`.
- Active emission reveals the emitter to passive-RWR-equipped enemies in detection cone (mirrors M44F active-sonar tradeoff).
- Detection range scales with `target_RCS_m² × own_radar_power_dbw / range^4`.
- Lookdown / shootdown filter: pulse-doppler discriminates ground clutter using closure rate; only the Fighter + VTOL ship with pulse-doppler (lookdown-capable); Ground Attack has INS-only.

### Air-to-air missiles (AAM)

- **AIM-9 IR-homing** (short range, ~18 km): seeker locks on engine plume IR signature; flare countermeasures defeat at 70% probability per launch.
- **AIM-120 active-radar-homing** (medium-long, ~105 km): inertial mid-course + active terminal homing; "fire-and-forget" but mid-course datalink updates extend the NEZ (no-escape zone).
- **AIM-7 SAR** (semi-active radar; legacy SKU): requires shooter radar to keep illumination through impact; cancels if shooter dies or loses lock.

### Air-to-ground missiles (AGM)

- **AGM-65 Maverick** (electro-optic, ~27 km): pilot designates aim point pre-launch; missile flies it.
- **AGM-114 Hellfire** (laser-homing, ~8 km): cross-reuse with M44G Attack Helicopter rack.
- **AGM-158 JASSM** (standoff cruise, ~925 km): GPS + INS + terminal IIR; long-leg standoff weapon for Bomber.

### Ejection seat

- All manned SKUs include ACES II ejection seat (Bomber has 4 in sequence at 0.2 s intervals).
- cfctl `act.player.eject_pilot` triggers: canopy explosive bolts blow → seat rocket fires → pilot separates from seat at apogee → parachute auto-deploys.
- Ejection envelope: 0-30000 m altitude AND 0-1100 m/s airspeed. Outside envelope → pilot suffers windblast / spinal-compression injury (M19 affliction).

### Autopilot

- 6 modes: `heading_hold`, `altitude_hold`, `airspeed_hold`, `waypoint_route`, `terrain_following` (nap-of-earth low-level), `formation_keeper`.
- M23 LLM mind wraps autopilot for full autonomy; scripted mode wraps for fixed missions.

### Carrier-deck landing

- Aircraft carrier (M44F-built hull OR mission-spawned) hosts 3 arresting cables (pilot aims for cable #2 or #3).
- Tailhook engages cable: deceleration from 220 px/s → 0 in 1.5 s; structural load on chassis.
- Miss (bolter): full afterburner go-around; one more attempt or divert.
- VTOL aircraft bypass cables: vectored-thrust vertical descent onto deck spot.

### Air refueling

- Tanker aircraft (KC-135-equivalent) extends boom (probe-and-drogue OR flying boom).
- Receiver pilot stabilises within boom-envelope box (1 m × 1 m × 1 m) for 2 s to establish link.
- Fuel transfers at 80 kg/s up to receiver tank capacity.
- Link disengages on receiver drift outside envelope for 0.5 s OR pilot toggle.

```gherkin
  And the drone proceeds to its cruise leg
```

## Notes for the implementer

- Aircraft lift uses a simplified `L = ½ × ρ × v² × S × CL(α)` with stall at `α > 18°` (linear pre-stall, soft post-stall). Drag uses `D = ½ × ρ × v² × S × CD(α, M)` with transonic drag rise at `M > 0.85`.
- Mach-1 boom event fires when airspeed crosses local sound-speed; sonic boom propagates a pressure-wave hazard (M19) within 100 m of the ground track.
- Afterburner is a multiplier (~1.8×) on `thrust_max_n` at a fixed fuel rate; do NOT model it as a separate engine state machine.
- VTOL nozzle slew is a single scalar `nozzle_angle_deg ∈ [0, 90]` per nozzle; lift = thrust × sin(nozzle), forward = thrust × cos(nozzle). Differential-nozzle slew is the yaw control in hover.
- Radar contact list uses RCS² × power / range^4; clamp by horizon (line-of-sight at altitude).
- Air refueling: receiver pose must stay within a 1 m × 1 m × 1 m boom-envelope box for 2 s to establish; outside box for 0.5 s breaks the link.
- Carrier landing: deck pose is M44F carrier entity local frame; cable engagement is a swept-volume check against the cable line segment at deck plane.
- Recon drones are NOT M44B cargo drones. `cf-vehicle::recon_drone` is a separate trait, separate chassis registry, separate AI doctrine. M44B cargo convoys SHOULD NOT spawn recon drones; the doctrines are independent.
- Pilot eject envelope check uses the same atmospheric-pressure lookup as M14A jetpack force calc.
- AIM-9 IR seeker uses M19 hot-gas detection (engine plume = hot pixel cluster); flare module emits decoy hot pixels for 4 s and reduces lock probability by 70%.
- Ramjet `inlet_min_mach` gate is a hard threshold — below threshold thrust = 0 N; above threshold thrust ramps over `spool_time_s`. Do NOT linearly interpolate around the threshold.
- All RNG (CEP scatter on missile impact, fuze variance, missile-track jitter, bolter probability, ejection injury severity) seeds off the engine's deterministic RNG; never `thread_rng`.
- Carrier-trap deceleration profile is fixed-tick integrated to preserve replay determinism.
- AESA radar emitter visibility broadcast uses the same event surface as M44F active-sonar ping (both are "active-emission reveals you"); reuse the broadcast pattern.
- Recon-drone operator HUD camera feed is a virtual-camera entity at the drone pose; cf-render-2d composites it as a picture-in-picture overlay.
- Bomb drop spacing uses time-on-target arithmetic from current ground speed; clamp `spacing_m` to integer ticks of bomb-release timer to keep replay determinism.
