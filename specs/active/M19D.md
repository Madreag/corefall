# M19D — Pipe Network Failure Cascade + Ice Plug + Over-Pressure Rupture Chain

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Stationeers-deep pipe-network failure modes: ice plug formation in cold zones + over-pressure rupture chain + slow leak detection + cascade rupture across pipe segments — turns M19's pipe network from a single-event rupture model into Stationeers-grade engineering tension where a single bad design choice cascades catastrophically.

## Player-facing behavior

- **Ice plug formation**: gas pipe carrying water vapor in a cold zone → vapor condenses → ice forms → pipe pressure builds → rupture cascade upstream (Stationeers DR-037 rule: solid > 0.05 mol/L = rupture).
- **Over-pressure rupture chain**: one segment ruptures → downstream pressure spikes → next segment ruptures → cascading wave through the network. Player must close isolation valves.
- **Slow leak detection**: pinhole leak in pipe → gradual pressure drop → player notices via PV=nRT math (M19 pressure gauge or M29A power overlay pipe-tooltip).
- **Iso valves + pressure regulators**: place valves at strategic points; close them mid-cascade to isolate the broken segment.
- **Welded vs bolted pipes**: bolted pipes (cheap) fail at 50% rupture threshold; welded pipes (T2; weld craftable per M32) fail at full threshold.
- **Double-welded frames** (Stationeers exploit per DR-037): kept as visual but with a small overhead so it doesn't trivialize design.
- **Repair gameplay**: M28B breach_repair_tool extended to pipe segment repair; T2/T3 repair kits restore pipe integrity.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Ice plug forms in cold zone with water vapor
  Given a gas pipe network running water vapor through a -50°C zone
  When 60s elapse
  Then water vapor cools below freezing in the cold zone
  And ice accumulation reaches 0.06 mol/L (above 0.05 rupture threshold per DR-037)
  And pipe_ice_plug_formed event fires
  And HUD warning "PIPE ICE — RUPTURE IMMINENT"
  And player must reroute or heat the zone

Scenario: Over-pressure rupture cascades upstream
  Given a base pipe network at 4 MPa baseline + one segment with iron rust degradation (integrity 40%)
  When pressure spike to 6.2 MPa (above 60 atm rupture threshold)
  Then weakest segment ruptures (pipe_segment_ruptured fires)
  And local pressure spike propagates upstream to next segment
  And within 5 ticks, cascade fires across the network
  And player must close iso_valves to isolate the break

Scenario: Iso valve closure stops cascade
  Given an in-progress pipe failure cascade
  When player triggers act.player.close_iso_valve at a strategic point
  Then iso_valve_closed_emergency fires
  And the cascade stops at the closed valve
  And only segments downstream of the cascade location are lost
  And player can repair them with pipe_repair_kit after closing more valves

Scenario: Slow leak detected via pressure drop
  Given a pipe network at 4 MPa baseline + a pinhole leak (0.1 mm)
  When 30s elapse
  Then pressure drops from 4.0 to 3.6 MPa
  And pipe_leak_detected event fires (early warning)
  And HUD shows "PIPE LEAK — search for source"
  And player can patrol the network + find the leak before it becomes catastrophic

Scenario: Welded pipe holds higher pressure
  Given two parallel pipes: one bolted + one welded
  When pressure rises to 5 MPa
  Then bolted pipe ruptures (50% threshold)
  And welded pipe holds (full 60.795 MPa threshold per DR-037)
  And HUD shows "BOLTED PIPE FAILED — REROUTE THROUGH WELDED"

Scenario: Pipe repair kit restores segment integrity
  Given a ruptured pipe segment (integrity 0%) + player carrying T2 pipe_repair_kit
  When the player approaches + presses [E] for 5s
  Then segment integrity restores to 80%
  And HUD displays "SEGMENT REPAIRED"
  And player can resume flow

Scenario: Pressure regulator prevents over-pressure
  Given a base reactor at 50 MPa output through a network rated 10 MPa max
  When pressure_regulator_advanced is placed inline (setpoint 8 MPa)
  Then output to downstream segments is capped at 8 MPa
  And any spike above 8 MPa is diverted to a safety relief valve
  And the network avoids the over-pressure cascade

Scenario: Determinism across cascade
  Given two engines with identical pipe network + identical failure trigger
  When cascade plays out across 600 ticks
  Then identical pipe rupture sequence
  And identical SaveBlob.checksum at tick 600
```

## Notes for the implementer

- Per-segment integrity decays per Stationeers stress formula: `stress_pct = 5000 × liquid_l / pipe_network_volume`. M19D adds ice plug as a separate trigger.
- Cascade simulator runs as single-threaded post-pass (deterministic; no parallelism). Same algorithm as M29C power_cascade simulator.
- Iso valves IC10-controllable (per M29A IC10 chip editor) — auto-close on detected ΔP spike.
- Welded pipes are M32 craftable via the pipe_weld tool + M28C dock setting.
- Repair kits consume on use (1 kit = 1 segment repair); higher-tier kits repair faster + with less material cost.
