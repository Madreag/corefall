# M30E — Refining Byproducts + Slag Recovery + Secondary Extraction

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Close the refining-chain byproduct gap: slag from smelting → back-piles as `loose_fill` material → can be re-processed for secondary metal extraction + waste gas routes to atmospherics (M19) → reactive sludge can be neutralized or weaponized. Stationeers-grade closed-loop refining where nothing is wasted.

## Player-facing behavior

- **Slag back-pile**: smelting iron from iron_ore → produces 100 kg iron + 30 kg slag (loose_fill material). Slag piles up at the smelter; player must clear it via cargo crate or auto-conveyor (M28C).
- **Secondary extraction**: slag still contains trace metals (~3% recoverable iron + copper + nickel). Player runs slag through Acid Bath (T3 base module) to extract — slow but profitable in late game.
- **Waste gas to atmospherics**: cracker (M19B) emits CO2 + Pollutant during operation; routes to base scrubber via pipe network; if scrubber is full or down, gas accumulates → toxic room.
- **Sulfur byproduct from coal smelt**: M30 coal ore → coal coke + sulfur. Sulfur fuels chemical reactions (acid production; M15 alchemy table); also for M14C HE rounds.
- **Reactive sludge** (rare; from uranium enrichment): radioactive waste; cannot be vented; must be containerized + buried OR weaponized as dirty bomb (M48 covert ops content).
- **Per-refining-chain waste output table**: every recipe declares its byproducts; modders can extend; M30E provides the canonical waste-handling registry.

## The byproduct table (locked)

| Primary recipe | Output | Byproduct | Waste gas |
|---|---|---|---|
| iron_ore → iron | 100 kg iron | 30 kg slag (loose_fill) | 5 kg CO2 |
| gold_ore → gold | 90 kg gold | 35 kg slag | 2 kg CO2 |
| coal → coal_coke | 80 kg coke | 12 kg sulfur | 5 kg CO2 + 1 kg pollutant |
| uranium_ore → uranium_fuel_rod | 1 fuel rod | 80 kg slag + 5 kg radioactive_waste | 0.5 kg CO2 |
| slag → secondary_extraction (Acid Bath) | 3 kg iron + 1 kg copper + 0.5 kg nickel | 95 kg neutralized_slag (compactable) | 2 kg pollutant |
| crude_oil → diesel + kerosene + heavy fuel (distillation) | per M19B | 5 kg tar (waste) | 1 kg CO2 |

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Smelter produces slag byproduct
  Given a smelter active with iron_ore input + 5 kW power
  When 60s elapse and 100 kg iron is produced
  Then refining_byproduct_generated event fires with slag=30 kg
  And the slag bin fills with the byproduct
  And waste_gas_vented fires with CO2=5 kg
  And M19 atmospherics receives the CO2 into the room's atmosphere

Scenario: Acid bath recovers secondary metals from slag
  Given 95 kg slag in storage + acid_bath active + 1 kW power
  When 120s elapse
  Then slag_extracted_secondary fires with extracted_iron=3 kg + copper=1 kg + nickel=0.5 kg
  And the remaining slag is neutralized + compactable
  And the recovered metals route to base stockpile

Scenario: Waste gas accumulates in unscrubbed room
  Given a base with active smelters + a broken atmospheric scrubber
  When 120s elapse
  Then CO2 partial pressure rises ≥ 15% in the smelter room
  And actors entering the room without filter experience hypercapnia per M19C
  And M11 HUD displays warning "TOXIC GAS — REPAIR SCRUBBER"

Scenario: Radioactive waste containerization
  Given uranium enrichment running + 5 kg radioactive_waste produced
  When player diverts the output to radioactive_storage_t3
  Then radioactive_waste_stored event fires
  And the radioactive waste is locked in shielded container
  And no radiation hazard escapes (M16 radiation hazard not triggered)

Scenario: Dirty bomb weaponization (M48 covert ops content)
  Given radioactive_waste in storage + chemical explosive (M14C HE)
  When player triggers act.player.weaponize_waste
  Then dirty_bomb_weaponized event fires
  And a deployable dirty bomb item is crafted (consumes 5 kg radioactive_waste)
  And the bomb deploys per M14C HE round mechanics but with M16 radiation aftermath

Scenario: Slag pile fills + must be cleared
  Given a smelter running for 10 hours with no slag clearance
  When slag bin reaches 500 kg capacity
  Then HUD warns "SLAG BIN FULL — SMELTER OFFLINE"
  And smelter pauses production
  And player must move slag via cargo crate (M28C) or auto-conveyor to clear

Scenario: Determinism across full refining loop
  Given a 24-hour bake with iron + gold + coal smelting + secondary extraction
  When 24 in-game hours elapse
  Then all byproducts deterministically tracked
  And ledger checksum identical across replay
```

## Out of scope

- Bio-extraction (using microorganisms to leach trace metals from slag) — M48+ exotic.
- Per-pixel granular slag particles for visual realism — visual abstraction at chunk-level.
- Adaptive AI sentry that defends radioactive waste sites — M48+ AI doctrine.

## Notes for the implementer

- Per-recipe byproduct table is the canonical source — mod authors extend by adding `byproducts:` field to their recipe RON files.
- Waste gas routes through M19 pipe network when scrubber is wired; falls back to atmospheric venting when offline.
- Slag bin is similar to ore stockpile UX (M28A); auto-conveyor (M28C) is the auto-clearing mechanism.
- Radioactive_waste shielding: multi-layer lead per kg waste; container has integrity HP that degrades when hit.
- Determinism: byproduct generation is deterministic per recipe + input mass; not randomized.
