
## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M5.7 is the hazard package + afflictions milestone** — turns M2.5's reserved hazard + affliction event surface into a full simulation. After M5.7, hazards spread + dissipate per material rules, all 22 affliction kinds have producers + mechanics (18 baseline + 4 survival), anomaly hazards from STALKER ship as scenario surfaces (gravity / electric / time / chemical / bloodsucker / psy_storm), artifact loot drops from anomaly proximity, swimming + underwater combat works on water tiles, and the affliction strip HUD reflects real player + actor state.

M5.7's player-facing promise: **"the environment is dangerous in a thousand subtle ways the player learns to read."**

## Player-facing behavior

### Hazard tile full mechanics (M2.5: 5 launch hazards surface → M5.7: producers + spread rules)

**Storyteller integration:** Hazard-spawned events (fire_spread_event / electric_arc_cascade / acid_pool_growth / radiation_storm) register via M7's `cf-storyteller::registry.register()` API. M5.7 ships hazard mechanics + storyteller event hooks; M7 owns the storyteller runtime. See `specs/active/M7.md` § Storyteller event registration API.


| Hazard | Spread rule | Dissipation | Counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **fire** | Spreads to adjacent flammable (wood, oil, volatiles) at 1-tile/s rate | Burns out 30s OR water | Water flask, foam gun, suffocate |
| **smoke/gas** | Pressure-driven diffusion 2-tile/s (uses M5.9 atm kernel when active) | Vents via aperture OR filter | Mask, ventilation |
| **electric** | Arcs to adjacent metal/conductive within 3 tiles | Lose charge 5s after source removed | Insulation, water (counter — spreads via water!) |
| **wet/slippery** | Pools downward (gravity) following trenches | Evaporates 30s; faster in heat | Drying tool |
| **hot/cold** | Conduction through adjacent tiles | Equilibrates 20s after source removed | Insulation, contrary temperature |
| **acid pool** | Spreads as liquid; pH neutralized by alkali | Diluted by water | Alkali, water |
| **radiation_zone** | Static field (no spread; sticks to source) | Decay over hours | Shielding, distance |
| **toxic_atmosphere** | Spreads via M5.9 atm flow | Filtered by atmospherics | Suit, filter, vent |

Per-hazard producer:
- `hazard.spawned` (initial)
- `hazard.spread` (per spread)
- `hazard.actor_contact` (when actor enters)
- `hazard.tick` (cosmetic; batched 10:1 ratio for determinism)
- `hazard.dissipated` (reasons: time/doused/spread-out)

### 22 affliction kinds — full mechanics (18 baseline + 4 survival)

Baseline 18 afflictions cover combat + environment. Survival-mode afflictions (4 new: hunger / thirst / sleep_dep / sanity_low) are required when PvE Survival mode is active (per M11.5). All 22 use the same affliction system (stack / escalate / clear) defined below.

Each affliction has full producer at M5.7:

| Affliction | Trigger | Effect | Cleared by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `burning` | fire contact | -5 HP/s + bloom on reticle | Water, time, suffocation |
| `wet` | water/wet contact | -slow movement, slip on ice | 30s drying |
| `electrified` | electric contact | -mobility (1 tick in 4 skipped) | Insulator, time |
| `poisoned` (toxic) | toxic gas | -3 HP/s + reduced vision | Antidote, suit |
| `hypoxic` | atm O2 < threshold | -2 HP/s | Atm recovery, suit |
| `combustible_atmosphere` | atm volatiles > threshold | Ignition cascade risk | Vent, filter |
| `breach_decomp` | sealed atm < 0.5 atm | -1 HP/s + drift toward breach | Seal, suit |
| `hyperthermic` | env temp > threshold | -2 HP/s + reduced aim | Cooling |
| `hypothermic` | env temp < threshold | -1 HP/s + reduced speed | Warming |
| `radiation` | radiation zone | -1 HP/s + latent dose | Shielding, decon |
| `concussed` | high-impulse impact | -aim, recoil bloom 3s | Time |
| `deafened` | high-loudness event | -hearing range 5s | Time |
| `bleeding` | wound contact | -1 HP/s | Medikit, bandage |
| `internal_shock` (robot) | hi-impulse robot hit | Module damage cascade | Repair |
| `low_battery` (chassis) | chassis power < threshold | Module degradation | Recharge |
| `coolant_leaking` (chassis) | heat + breach | Heat increase | Repair |
| `oil_leaking` (chassis) | chassis damage | Combustion risk | Repair |
| `overheating` (chassis) | heat > 90% | Weapon disabled | Cooling |
| `hunger` | caloric_energy < 20 (per M5.8) | -0.1 HP/s + speed × 0.8 + caloric drain × 2 (death spiral) | Eat food (per M7.8 cooking) |
| `thirst` | water reservoir empty | aim wobble + mental fog + speed × 0.85 | Drink water (base condenser per M7.5; lake/river extraction) |
| `sleep_dep` | 4+ in-game hours missed sleep | vision tunneling + reaction time +50% | Sleep at bed (per M7 base object) |
| `sanity_low` | sanity accumulator < 30% | panic affliction + AI decisions impaired (player actor) + storyteller intensity spike | Recreation per M7 schedule + therapy NPC (M7.1) |

Stacking rules: affliction severity stacks (severity 0.5 → exposure → severity 0.8); HUD shows total severity; events: `affliction.applied`, `affliction.escalated`, `affliction.cleared`.

### Swimming + underwater combat (water material from M5.6)

- Water tiles: player can swim
- Swim stance: 70% speed; reduced weapon range; submerged camera (visual blur)
- Underwater-specific weapons: harpoon, spear gun
- Drowning: oxygen drain when underwater; affliction.hypoxic stacks
- Forward-compat: water material is M5.6; swim mechanics M5.7
- Events: `actor.swim_started`, `actor.drowning_started`, `actor.drowning_lethal`

### Affliction strip HUD widget

- Per-actor affliction stack visible under body silhouette
- Max 5 icons visible; 6th evicts with "+N more" counter
- Per-affliction icon + severity indicator
- Tooltip shows full affliction details
- Banners fire on critical afflictions

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Fire spreads to flammable + extinguished by water
  Given fire at (X,Y) + adjacent wood
  Then hazard.spread fires from fire to wood (1-tile/s)
  When water flask thrown on fire:
    Then hazard.dissipated fires (reason=doused)
    And steam tile spawns

Scenario: Affliction stacks + escalates
  Given affliction.burning severity=0.3
  When actor stays in fire 5 more seconds:
    Then affliction.escalated fires (from=0.3, to=0.8)
  When severity reaches 1.0:
    Then affliction.escalated kind="severe"

Scenario: Anomaly damages on entry
  Given electric_anomaly tile + actor enters
  Then anomaly.entered fires
  And periodic zap damage every 0.5s
  And affliction.electrified stacks

Scenario: Artifact bonus when carried
  Given a Stone Blood artifact picked up
  Then artifact.picked_up fires
  And actor.max_hp += 20 (passive while carrying)
  When dropped:
    Then bonus removed

Scenario: Swim through water
  Given player adjacent to water tile
  When entering water:
    Then actor.swim_started fires
    And stance=Swim; speed × 0.7
  When underwater too long:
    Then actor.drowning_started fires
    And affliction.hypoxic stacks rapidly
    When prolonged drowning:
      Then actor.drowning_lethal fires; HP=0

Scenario: Anomaly detector reveals positions
  Given anomaly detector in inventory
  Then HUD minimap shows nearby anomaly positions

Scenario: Affliction strip max 5 icons
  Given actor with 6 active afflictions
  Then HUD shows 5 + "+1 more" counter

Scenario: Hazard tile cosmetic events batched per M3A
  Given fire tile active for 60 ticks
  Then hazard.tick events emitted with cosmetic=true (batched 10:1 ratio)
  And determinism scope excludes cosmetic events

Scenario: Survival afflictions only active in PvE Survival mode
  Given PvP arena mode (per DR-042 match grammar)
  Then hunger / thirst / sleep_dep / sanity_low afflictions are NOT spawned (PvP doesn't have survival mechanics)
  Given PvE Survival mode (per M11.5)
  Then survival afflictions are tracked per actor + escalate per M11.5 timer
  And affliction.applied fires when threshold crossed
```
