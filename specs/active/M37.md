**M37 is the voice + radio + comms milestone** — ACRE2-tier voice + radio simulation per DR-043. ### Voice chat (proximity-based)

- Voice transmits at proximity (similar to ACRE2)
- 3 channels: Squad (encrypted) / Public (open) / Faction (faction-only)
- Push-to-talk default; toggleable to voice activity
- Per-player mute / volume per channel

### Radio propagation (ACRE2-inspired) — locked numeric contract

Three radio classes ship at M37; per-equipment radio depth + tier ladder owned canonically by **M37B** (5-tier ladder: handheld VHF → squad UHF → vehicle HF → base LF → HQ MHz). M37 baseline:

| Radio class | Tx power | Frequency | Free-space range | Through 1 wall | Underground | Antenna height multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short-range | 5 W | VHF (150 MHz) | 5 km | 3 km | 0.6 km | × `√(antenna_height_m / 1.5)` |
| Medium-range | 25 W | UHF (450 MHz) | 15 km | 8 km | 1.2 km | × `√(antenna_height_m / 3.0)` |
| Long-range | 100 W | HF (10 MHz) | 50+ km (skywave ducting) | 30 km | 3 km | × `√(antenna_height_m / 9.0)` |

- Attenuation formula: `signal_dB = tx_dBm - 20·log10(distance_km) - 20·log10(freq_MHz) - 32.45 - occlusion_dB`
- `occlusion_dB`: 0 per air-tile traversed; 12 dB per wood/wall; 30 dB per concrete/rock; 45 dB per metal/Faraday tile (M37B owner of per-tile dB material constants)
- Reception threshold: `signal_dB ≥ -110` = clear; `-110 to -125` = static-rich; `< -125` = no signal
- Antenna mast (M37B) raises effective `antenna_height_m` proportional to mast tier (1.5 m hand-held → 3 m squad → 9 m vehicle → 30 m base → 90 m HQ)
- All numeric constants live in `cf-audio::radio::propagation` and are read by M22C's sound-propagation kernel for cross-domain consistency

### Vacuum / EM constants

- Vacuum voice tile attenuation: `+∞ dB` (signal-blocking; EM still propagates)
- EMP grenade interference: `signal_dB - 80` for 5-30s window (per M6 EMP class)
- Solar flare (M45C `solar_flare` event): `signal_dB - 40` for 30-300s
- Comms blackout affliction (DR-040 hazard class `comms_blackout`): radio disabled hard

### Interference + EMP

- EMP grenades (from M6) disable radio for 5-30s
- EMP from machines (generators, reactor breaches)
- Solar flares (Sol-zone scenarios) disrupt for minutes
- Comms blackout affliction (DR-040 hazard taxonomy)

### Subtitle system + captions

- All voice + radio captioned (per DR-012 accessibility)
- Subtitle modes: Off / Critical-Only / Standard / Expanded (per M11 caption modes)
- Per-actor identification in subtitles
- Speaker indicator (visual ring around current speaker)

### Vacuum no_voice

- In vacuum: no audio voice transmission (sound doesn't propagate)
- Radio still works (electromagnetic; not sound)
- HUD banner "VACUUM — radio only" surfaces

### AI radio chatter

- AI bots emit radio chatter on key events:
  - "Contact, west wall!"
  - "Reloading!"
  - "Down!"
  - "Need help over here!"
- Reason labels accompany (per DR-008 humanlike AI)
- Bots respond to player voice (via M23 MIND or scripted patterns)

### Comms HUD

- Voice channel indicator (which channel active)
- Per-speaker direction arrow (from where the sound came)
- Subtitle stream (top or bottom; configurable)
