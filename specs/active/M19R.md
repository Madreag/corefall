# M19R — Hydroponics + Soil Agriculture + Crop Lifecycle + Per-Plant Genetics + Pest + Greenhouse Climate

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M19R is the agriculture + hydroponics depth milestone.** M19I shipped ONI critters (Hatch / Slickster / Puft / Pip / Pacu / Drecko / Shine Bug) + 4 plant farms with bare-bones gas-consumption/production cycles. M28A's build palette mentions "Hydroponics" + "algae terrarium + plant beds + grow lamps" but no spec ships the deep food-production gameplay loop: per-plant lifecycle, soil quality, hydroponic nutrient mix, grow-lamp spectrum, water cycle, fertilizer chemistry, pest infestation, greenhouse climate, crop rotation, seed genetics, yield curves, harvest UX. M19R fills that.

After M19R, players can build a fully-simulated greenhouse: seed wheat in soil beds, install grow lamps tuned to wheat's photosynthesis spectrum, irrigate with M19's water from a roof catchment, fertilize with composted M19I-critter dung, watch wheat go through seed → sprout → vegetative → flowering → grain → harvest over 12 in-game days, harvest at peak quality, replant. Or upgrade to hydroponics: NFT troughs + nutrient pumps + chiller-pasteurized water + 24h LED grow cycle → 2.5× yield + 1.7× speed at 5× the power draw + a single power outage kills everything in 4 hours.

M19R promise: **"food is not a slider — food is a system. Soil quality matters. Light spectrum matters. Water pH matters. Pests are real. Crop rotation is real. Hydroponics is faster but fragile. Genetics drift over generations. A well-tended farm feeds your colony; a neglected farm starves it."**

## Player-facing behavior

### Per-plant lifecycle (5 phases)

Every authored plant cycles through:

1. **Seed** — dormant; needs ground contact + water + min temp to germinate.
2. **Sprout** — vulnerable; needs light + water; slow growth.
3. **Vegetative** — main growth; needs light + water + nutrients + appropriate temperature; pest-vulnerable.
4. **Flowering / Fruiting** — needs less light + steady water + sometimes a pollinator (insect critter or fan-assisted air flow).
5. **Harvest** — ripe; quality decays after peak window if not harvested.

Per-plant timing values (in-game hours per phase) authored in `content/plants/<plant_id>.ron`. Default crop catalog at M19R close:

| Plant | Seed→Sprout | Sprout→Vegetative | Vegetative→Flowering | Flowering→Harvest | Yield (kg/plant) | Soil pref | Light pref |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wheat | 24h | 48h | 96h | 72h | 0.8 | loam | broad spectrum |
| Potato | 36h | 60h | 120h | 96h | 1.6 | loose dirt | broad spectrum |
| Tomato | 48h | 72h | 96h | 120h | 2.4 | rich loam | red-shifted |
| Mushroom | 24h | 24h | 72h | 48h | 0.6 | compost | dark (UV opt) |
| Soy | 36h | 60h | 96h | 96h | 1.2 | loam | broad spectrum |
| Hemp | 48h | 96h | 144h | 96h | 1.0 (fiber) | sandy loam | red-shifted |
| Bamboo | 0h (cutting) | 96h | 240h | n/a (continuous) | 4.0 (pole) | wet loam | broad spectrum |
| Algae | 0h | 8h | 24h | 12h | 0.3 (paste) | water tank | blue-shifted |

Algae is the bridge into M19I's algae terrarium (existing M19I).

### Soil quality model

Per-tile soil has 4 attributes (read from M3B per-tile element fields + extended by M19R):

| Attribute | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `nutrient_n` (nitrogen) | 0.0 - 1.0 | Drained by every harvest; restored by fertilizer + nitrogen-fixing crops (soy) |
| `nutrient_p` (phosphorus) | 0.0 - 1.0 | Drained slower; restored by fertilizer |
| `nutrient_k` (potassium) | 0.0 - 1.0 | Drained by fruiting crops more than leafy; restored by fertilizer + composted critter dung |
| `ph` | 4.0 - 9.0 | Acid-loving (potato @ 5.5) vs alkaline-loving (algae @ 7.5); off-pH halves yield |

Soil quality computed each harvest: `yield_multiplier = nutrient_match × ph_match × moisture_match`. A wheat field with `nutrient_n = 0.2` yields 0.4× baseline.

### Hydroponics (NFT trough + DWC tank)

Two hydroponic tech tiers:

- **NFT (Nutrient Film Technique) trough** (T2) — shallow trough, recirculating nutrient solution, plant roots in foam plugs. Yield × 2.5; speed × 1.7; power draw 0.4 kW per 4-plant trough; water-loop fail = plants die in 4 hr.
- **DWC (Deep Water Culture) tank** (T3) — full-immersion roots in oxygenated nutrient solution. Yield × 3.0; speed × 1.9; power draw 0.8 kW per 8-plant tank; oxygen pump fail = plants die in 90 min.

Hydroponic plants do NOT use the soil model; they use a `nutrient_solution` model:

| Attribute | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `EC` (electrical conductivity, mS/cm) | 1.2 - 2.4 | Higher EC for fruiting; lower for leafy |
| `ph` | 5.5 - 6.5 | Hydroponic-tight range vs soil's 4-9 |
| `temp_k` | 291 - 295 | Cold = slow; hot = root rot |
| `dissolved_o2_ppm` | 6.0 - 8.0 | DWC needs higher than NFT |

Nutrient solution drifts per tick; sensors + auto-dosers (T3) maintain.

### Grow-lamp spectrum

Per-lamp WGSL-computed photosynthesis curve:

| Lamp tier | Spectrum | Power | PAR (photosynthetic active radiation) μmol/m²/s |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fluorescent T1 | broad | 0.04 kW | 80 |
| HPS (high-pressure sodium) T2 | red-orange peaked | 0.25 kW | 350 |
| MH (metal halide) T2 | blue-peaked | 0.25 kW | 320 |
| LED full-spectrum T3 | tunable RGB+UV | 0.18 kW | 480 |
| LED targeted (per-plant) T4 | plant-matched | 0.12 kW | 520 |

Per-plant `light_pref` field reads what spectrum the plant prefers; mismatch = -30% yield.

### Irrigation + water cycle

- Plants consume water from M19's water registry; irrigation can be manual (hand watering can), drip (tier-2 piped drip line + 1 kW pump), or sprinkler (tier-3 overhead).
- Roof catchment feeds a base water tank (per M28); rain (per M31 weather) auto-fills tank.
- Greywater from sinks + showers (M19N kitchen + M19O bathroom) can be routed back into irrigation after passing through a `cf-base-modules` filter unit (per M28E filter chain) — reduces fresh-water draw 60%.
- Drought (per M31C seasons) halves rainfall; multi-day drought triggers a `m19r.crop_drought_stress` event.

### Pest infestation lifecycle

- Per-tile `pest_population: f32` counter (0.0 - 1.0); seeded at base rate per outdoor tile per cycle.
- Pest types (each with M16 affliction-vector for actors who eat infested crops):
  - **Aphids** (vegetative leaf damage; -20% yield if untreated 3 days)
  - **Mites** (hydroponic-loving; thrive in 27 °C + humid air)
  - **Locusts** (rare; outdoor crops only; M45C `locust_swarm` event triggers; can destroy entire field in 1 cycle)
  - **Fungal rot** (cool + humid + low light; mushroom field intentional but other crops devastating)
- Treatments: predator critters (M19I shine bug eats aphids), pesticide spray (M32 crafted; -5 morale to nearby actors per M19P health-conscious trait), cultural (crop rotation + row spacing breaks pest cycles).

### Greenhouse climate

A `Greenhouse` room type (per M28A room detection) requires: 4 walls + ceiling + at least 1 grow lamp + at least 1 water source. Inside greenhouse:

- M28D HVAC maintains temperature in plant-pref range.
- M19F humidity tracking matters: too dry = stomata close; too humid = mold.
- M19H light propagation reaches plants (skylight modifier + grow-lamp combined).
- M19E gas registry tracks CO2: depleted CO2 (plants consume) reduces growth; M19R adds optional CO2 enrichment tank (T3) for +30% yield.

### Crop rotation rules

Per-tile `rotation_history: VecDeque<plant_id; 4>` — remembers last 4 crops planted. Planting the same family twice in a row triggers `m19r.crop_rotation_violation`: -25% yield, +40% pest susceptibility, soil nutrient depletion accelerated. Players rotate `wheat → soy (nitrogen-fix) → potato → wheat` to maintain soil health.

### Seed genetics + drift

Each harvested seed inherits the parent's quality_tier + a small random mutation:

```
child.yield_modifier = parent.yield_modifier × (1.0 + uniform(-0.05, +0.05))
child.disease_resist = parent.disease_resist × (1.0 + uniform(-0.05, +0.05))
child.taste_modifier = parent.taste_modifier × (1.0 + uniform(-0.05, +0.05))
```

Save best seeds for replanting → over 10+ generations of selective breeding, players develop strains with +30% yield or +50% disease resistance. M48 covert ops can steal rival faction's bred seed strains.

### Harvest UX

- Per-plant `ripeness: f32` (0.0 - 1.2; peak at 1.0, decay starts > 1.05).
- Harvest UI surfaces ripeness color: green (still growing) → yellow (peak window) → brown (overripe; spoiling).
- Right-click "Harvest" pulls ripe plants in radius; auto-replanted if `auto_replant: bool` set + seed available.
- Yield ledgered via M28G `production_rates` (per-base food production rate).

## Notes for the implementer

- Plant lifecycle is a pure FSM driven by per-plant `accumulated_growth_hours: f32` — the only inputs are tick-delta + light + water + nutrient sufficiency multipliers. Easy to test deterministically.
- The soil model is per-tile and persists in save; back-fill default values for existing dirt/loam tiles in the save migration (per M4B).
- Hydroponic nutrient solution drift is a deterministic ODE — straightforward to test.
- Pest population grows as logistic; carrying capacity per-tile = 1.0; growth rate per-pest is a content value in `content/pests/<id>.ron`.
- Selective breeding RNG MUST use the seeded sim PRNG, never `thread_rng` (per AGENTS.md sim conventions).
- The 8 launch plants are the minimum; mod authors can add more — register via `cf-agriculture::plant_registry`.
- Greenhouse-climate integration is read-only against M28D; M19R consumes the thermostat setpoint, doesn't write it.
- Greywater filter module reuses M28E filter lifecycle — M19R does not own filter clogging.
- Drop the locust swarm acceptance scenario behind M45C; if M45C is not closed, scenario is `pending`.
