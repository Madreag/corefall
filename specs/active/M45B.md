# M45B — 5 Named Boss Deep Specs (Hollow King / Frozen Heart / Crimson Tide / Eclipse Walker / Last Star)

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M45B is the per-boss deep design milestone.** M45 ships the 5-row `BossDef` data summary against M25's canonical schema. M25 ships the multi-phase boss engine, attack scheduler, and arena machinery. Neither owns the deep, per-boss authored content: phase-state machines, signature attacks, per-zone per-phase HP, kill conditions, storyteller variants, drop tables, environmental hazards during the fight, escape rules, or co-op vs solo balance. M45B is that body.

After M45B, every named boss — **The Hollow King** in Earth's volcanic core, **The Frozen Heart** in Europa's caverns, **The Crimson Tide** in the Mars dust ocean, **The Eclipse Walker** in Mimas microgravity, and **The Last Star** on Vulcan — has a deterministic 3-to-5-phase state machine, 4-6 signature attacks with authored telegraphs and counters, per-phase per-zone HP rows that read from M13's chassis grammar, kill conditions that can require more than "HP to zero" (Frozen Heart requires core-overload; Last Star requires sequential biome-challenge clearance), a loot table that ledgers a unique recipe + artifact + 1 cosmetic trophy per boss + 5 lore-codex unlocks, per-storyteller variants (Cassandra makes Hollow King's enrage harder; Phoebe gives the player a 30s breather between phases; Randy can spawn a second boss mid-fight; Ironman makes every wipe permanent), per-difficulty tuning (chill / tough / extreme / nightmare), co-op vs solo balance (boss HP scales by player count but signature attacks add a second target when ≥2 players are alive), arena escape rules per M25 EscapeRule enum, and per-fight environmental hazard (Hollow King's arena floods with lava during phase 3; Frozen Heart's chamber drops to -180 °C below; Crimson Tide is a moving fight inside an authored dust storm; Eclipse Walker can invert gravity twice per fight; Last Star's arena cycles through 5 biome zones).

M45B's player-facing promise: **"each of the 5 bosses fights differently, dies differently, drops differently, and feels different. The Hollow King is a 3-phase pyroclastic siege. The Frozen Heart is a cold-soak puzzle that punishes plate armor. The Crimson Tide is a 4-phase split-and-reform swarm. The Eclipse Walker is a microgravity duel where the floor can become the ceiling. The Last Star is a 5-phase endgame mountain that asks you to have survived everything the campaign taught you."**

## Player-facing behavior

### Per-boss promise (one sentence each)

- **The Hollow King** (Earth volcanic biome, T3 plasma weapon required) — *"The siege of a dying god in the volcanic core. Three phases. The floor becomes lava in phase 3. If you cannot keep moving, the King cannot be killed."*
- **The Frozen Heart** (Europa caverns, T2 weapons + heat-resistance gear required) — *"A puzzle wrapped in a fight. Standard damage barely scratches it. Overheat its core via reflected thermal beams until it melts itself."*
- **The Crimson Tide** (Mars dust storm boss, T2 mech + ammo stockpile required) — *"A swarm that thinks. Four phases. It splits in phase 3. You have to defeat all three split-bodies inside 90 seconds or they reform with full HP."*
- **The Eclipse Walker** (Mimas microgravity, T2 weapons + jetpack required) — *"A duel in zero-g. Three phases. The Walker can invert gravity twice. Your jetpack is your only floor."*
- **The Last Star** (Vulcan endgame, T3-T4 endgame, campaign-long unlock) — *"The mountain. Five phases. Each phase cycles the arena into one of five Vulcan biomes. You must have survived everything to survive this."*

### Per-boss content rows (data shape)

Every boss authored in M45B ships:

- **Lore strap** — a 2-3 sentence in-fiction summary tied to M25A's narrative bible. Surfaced on arena-entry cutscene + codex page header.
- **Arena** — host world + biome cell + tileset reference + lighting profile + per-fight environmental hazard override.
- **Phase FSM** — 3-5 phases, each with `entry_condition`, `exit_condition`, `attack_pool`, `hp_threshold_per_zone`, `environmental_overlay`, `script_hooks`.
- **Signature attacks (4-6)** — each with name, telegraph window (ms), telegraph visual + audio cue, hitbox, counter (dodge / cover / interrupt / specific tool), per-difficulty parameter knob.
- **Per-zone per-phase HP** — using M13 chassis 15-zone grammar; boss zones are not symmetric across phases (Hollow King's chest is exposed in phase 1 + 2, armored in phase 3).
- **Kill condition** — explicit. "HP to zero" is the default but Frozen Heart requires core-overload, Crimson Tide requires kill-all-splits-within-window, Last Star requires sequential 5-biome clearance + final core kill.
- **Loot table** — unique recipe + unique artifact + 1 cosmetic trophy + 5 lore unlocks + standard rolls; ledgered via M27 + M40.
- **Per-storyteller variant rows** — Cassandra dread / Phoebe quirk / Randy chaos / Ironman permadeath / Sandbox banked-rewards.
- **Per-difficulty tuning** — chill (0.6× HP / 0.8× damage) → tough_crowd (1.0× / 1.0×) → extreme (1.4× / 1.3×) → nightmare (1.8× / 1.6× + new attack added in phase 3).
- **Co-op vs solo balance** — HP scales `1.0 + 0.7 × (players - 1)`; signature attacks gain a `second_target` when ≥ 2 players are alive AND not downed.
- **Arena escape rule** (M25 EscapeRule enum) — `OneWayCutsceneEntry` / `TwoWayDoor` / `TimedRetreat`.

### The Hollow King — deep spec

**Lore strap** *(authored against M25A bible `content/narrative/bosses/hollow_king.lore.ron`)*: *"In the volcanic basalt of Earth's last hot zone, the Hollow King survives because nothing can be left of it that the world has not already burned. Its body is the cooled crust over a pyroclastic core. To kill it you must split the crust."*

**Arena:** Earth volcanic biome, sealed magma chamber, OneWayCutsceneEntry. 80 × 40 tile bowl. Authored tileset = `volcanic_basalt_bowl`. Lighting = orange / red key-light + ambient red haze. Ambient temperature = 320 °C; player without heat-resistance gear takes 0.5%/s burning per M16.

**Per-fight environmental hazard:** lava floor flood. Phase 1 = floor solid (basalt). Phase 2 = 30% of floor tiles convert to lava over 20s. Phase 3 = 80% of floor converts; player must be airborne (jetpack) or on the 4 elevated platforms.

**Phase FSM:**

| Phase | Entry | Exit | Pool | Per-zone HP (chest / head / arms / legs) | Floor state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Crowned** | Arena-entry cutscene | Chest HP < 70% | RangedFlame, BasaltSweep, HornShout | 2400 / 800 / 600 / 600 | solid basalt |
| **2 — Cracking** | Phase-1 exit | Chest HP < 35% | RangedFlame, MinionSummon, EarthSplit | 1800 / 600 / 800 / 800 | 30% lava |
| **3 — Enraged Pyroclast** | Phase-2 exit + 5s enrage cutscene | Chest HP ≤ 0 (kill condition) | PyroclasticBurst, MinionSummon, FlareLance, BasaltSweep | 1200 / 1200 / 400 / 400 (chest armored) | 80% lava |

**Signature attacks:**

| Name | Telegraph | Hitbox | Counter | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RangedFlame** | 600 ms — King raises right arm; orange particle bloom | Forward cone 8 tiles, 60° arc | Sidestep OR cover behind raised tile | Phase 1 + 2 + 3 |
| **BasaltSweep** | 900 ms — King plants left foot; ground rumble + screen shake | 360° sweep, 4 tiles radius, ground only | Jetpack ascend OR mount elevated platform | Phase 1 + 3 |
| **HornShout** | 400 ms — head tilts back; audio bed swells | All players in arena; -30% movement for 4s | Suppress with interrupt damage to head ≥ 200 in window | Phase 1 only |
| **MinionSummon** | 1200 ms — King kneels; lava cracks open | Spawns 2-4 lava minions (M9 hostile pawn) | Kill minions OR ignore + tank | Phase 2 + 3 |
| **EarthSplit** | 1000 ms — King slams both fists; floor cracks | Converts 6 random floor tiles to lava | Move OR jetpack; advances Phase 2 hazard timer | Phase 2 only |
| **PyroclasticBurst** | 1500 ms — full-body charge; red bloom + audio crescendo | Whole arena, AOE 50% HP damage to anyone on floor | Must be airborne when burst fires | Phase 3 enrage; fires every 25s |
| **FlareLance** | 700 ms — point arm at one player; bright tracer line | Hitscan; 80% max HP | Roll perpendicular OR break line-of-sight with platform | Phase 3 only |

**Kill condition:** Phase-3 chest HP ≤ 0 with head not concurrently armored. Standard.

**Loot table** (ledgered via M27):

- 1× **Pyroclastic Armor recipe** (T3, unique, requires obsidian + neutronium gem)
- 1× **Crown Fragment** artifact (passive: -30% burning duration on equipped actor)
- 1× **Lava-Walker Boots** cosmetic trophy (per M45A pipeline; visible flame VFX on feet during walking)
- 5× **Codex unlocks** referencing M25A `hollow_king.lore.ron`: origin myth / war history / minions origin / volcanic dynasty / last words
- Standard rolls: 2-4 plasma cells, 1-2 T3 weapon mods, 200-400 credits

**Per-storyteller variants:**

- **Cassandra** — Phase 3 PyroclasticBurst fires every 20s instead of 25s; FlareLance gains +1 charge.
- **Phoebe** — 30s breather between phases (King kneels, no attacks); BasaltSweep telegraph extended to 1200 ms.
- **Randy** — 25% roll on phase 2 entry to spawn a second mini-boss (Lava Captain) for the duration of phase 2; if spawned, both bosses share aggro tables.
- **Ironman** — Permadeath on player wipe; no respawn; arena seals; player must commit before entry.
- **Sandbox** — Banked rewards on partial kill (player gets recipe + 1 lore unlock if Phase 1 cleared even if they retreat in phase 2 or 3).

**Per-difficulty tuning:** chill 0.6×/0.8×, tough_crowd 1.0×/1.0×, extreme 1.4×/1.3× (PyroclasticBurst hits 70% HP), nightmare 1.8×/1.6× + new attack `LavaGeyser` added to phase 3 pool (random target, 800 ms telegraph, vertical column).

**Co-op vs solo balance:** HP scales `1.0 + 0.7 × (players - 1)`. With ≥ 2 alive players, FlareLance gains `second_target` = next-nearest player.

**Arena escape rule:** `OneWayCutsceneEntry`. Player commits on cutscene start. No mid-fight retreat. Wipe forces respawn at world spawn + arena cooldown of 10 minutes.

### The Frozen Heart — deep spec

**Lore strap** *(`content/narrative/bosses/frozen_heart.lore.ron`)*: *"It was a reactor once. The crew tried to shut it down. The crew is still here, frozen, holding the levers. The Heart kept running. It learned to be cold."*

**Arena:** Europa cave system, ice cathedral, TwoWayDoor. 60 × 60 tile cavern. Tileset = `europa_ice_cathedral`. Ambient temperature = -180 °C; without heat-resistance gear, player takes 1%/s frozen per M16. Light = blue / cyan key with thermal-reflection tiles (4 floor pads + 4 wall pads).

**Per-fight environmental hazard:** thermal beam puzzle. The Heart shoots thermal beams that bounce off the reflective pads. Player must position pads (via cfctl pad-rotate action) so beams converge on the Heart's exposed core.

**Phase FSM:**

| Phase | Entry | Exit | Pool | Per-zone HP (core / shell / arms / legs) | Hazard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Crystalline** | Arena-entry | Shell HP < 60% | CryoBlast, IceWall, ChillAura | 9999 (immune) / 1600 / 400 / 400 | ambient cold |
| **2 — Mech-Adds** | Phase-1 exit | Shell HP < 20% | CryoBlast, IceMechSummon, ReflectiveShard | 9999 / 800 / 600 / 600 | ambient cold + 4 mechs |
| **3 — Meltdown** | Phase-2 exit + 8s reveal cutscene | Core HP ≤ 0 (kill condition) | CoreVent, CryoBlast, SelfCool | 2400 (exposed) / 400 / 400 / 400 | thermal beam puzzle live |

**Signature attacks:**

| Name | Telegraph | Hitbox | Counter | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CryoBlast** | 700 ms — Heart's surface cracks blue | Forward cone 12 tiles, 45° | Sidestep; on hit applies stack of `frozen` (M16) | All phases |
| **IceWall** | 900 ms — wall rises from floor | Wall hitbox + 200 HP wall | Destroy wall OR navigate around | Phase 1 only |
| **ChillAura** | passive — radius 6 tiles | Continuous frozen tick on anyone in range | Stay outside aura OR equip thermal gear | Phase 1 + 2 |
| **IceMechSummon** | 1400 ms — ice cracks from floor | Spawns 4 IceMech adds (per M9 pawn registry) | Kill adds; each adds drops 1 thermal cell | Phase 2 only |
| **ReflectiveShard** | 600 ms — shard fires + bounces | 4-bounce hitscan; 40% HP on direct | Stand outside bounce path | Phase 2 only |
| **CoreVent** | 1800 ms — Heart opens core hatch | Core exposed 5s; reflect thermal beam → 800 damage to core | Aim mirror pad; beam convergence damages core | Phase 3 only; fires every 20s |
| **SelfCool** | 1200 ms — Heart freezes itself | Restores 400 core HP; 1-time use per Phase 3 cycle | Interrupt with sustained damage during telegraph (≥ 600 dmg in 1.2s) | Phase 3 only |

**Kill condition:** Phase 3 — Core HP ≤ 0 via beam reflection. Direct damage to core during open window is 1.0× modifier; beam-reflected damage is 4.0× modifier. **Standard rifle fire alone cannot finish the boss** — requires use of the puzzle.

**Loot table:**

- 1× **Cryo-Plasma Rifle recipe** (T2, unique; thermal + plasma hybrid)
- 1× **Ice-Walker Boots** artifact (passive: ignore traction penalty on snow / ice tiles)
- 1× **Frozen Heart Pendant** cosmetic trophy (visible blue-glow pendant on chest)
- 5× Codex unlocks: reactor origin / frozen crew log / European dynasty / thermal-beam fragment / heart's last words
- Standard rolls: 3-6 thermal cells, 1-2 T2 weapon mods, 250-500 credits

**Per-storyteller variants:**

- **Cassandra** — SelfCool fires every 18s; ChillAura radius +1 tile.
- **Phoebe** — Telegraphs +200 ms; reflective pads pre-aligned within 30° of optimal angle on arena entry.
- **Randy** — 30% roll on phase 2 entry to spawn a roaming Frost Wraith (M9 pawn; uses freezing aura).
- **Ironman** — Wipe = permadeath; arena sealed for the campaign run.
- **Sandbox** — Reward banked on partial kill; player can retreat via TwoWayDoor anytime to refit; on return, boss HP restores to phase entry value.

**Per-difficulty tuning:** chill 0.6×/0.8×, tough_crowd 1.0×/1.0×, extreme 1.4×/1.3× + IceMech adds gain ranged attack, nightmare 1.8×/1.6× + Phase 3 adds `RegrowingShell` (core re-armors every 12s if not vented).

**Co-op vs solo balance:** HP scales `1.0 + 0.7 × (players - 1)`. With ≥ 2 alive, Phase 2 spawns 6 IceMech adds instead of 4; CoreVent puzzle requires 2 simultaneous beam reflections.

**Arena escape rule:** `TwoWayDoor`. Player can retreat to refit; on retreat the boss HP resets to phase-entry threshold (it heals on disengage to prevent attrition farming).

### The Crimson Tide — deep spec

**Lore strap** *(`content/narrative/bosses/crimson_tide.lore.ron`)*: *"The Tide is not one body. The Tide is a shape that emerges from the dust when the storm thinks. Kill its body and the Tide remains. Kill the Tide and a hundred bodies fall."*

**Arena:** Mars dust storm boss, moving fight inside an authored M31B `global_dust_storm` weather instance, TimedRetreat. 200 × 200 tile open desert. Tileset = `mars_dust_basin`. Per-fight environmental hazard = the active `global_dust_storm` weather state (visibility 0.3, traction 0.7, equipment wear ×2 per M31B), AND the boss generates its own storm intensification (visibility floor drops to 0.15 in phase 4).

**Per-fight environmental hazard:** the M31B dust storm runs the whole fight; the player cannot see more than ~15 tiles. The Crimson Tide phases shift the storm's intensity.

**Phase FSM (4 phases):**

| Phase | Entry | Exit | Pool | Per-zone HP | Storm intensity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Sand-Walker Vanguard** | Arena-entry | 8 vanguard adds defeated | SandSwarm, DustBlind | 200 each × 8 adds | normal (vis 0.4) |
| **2 — Storm Rising** | Phase-1 exit | Tide chest HP < 60% | DustSpear, WhirlwindRush, BlindingGust | Chest 1200 / Head 600 / Arms 400 / Legs 400 | rising (vis 0.3) |
| **3 — Split Trinity** | Phase-2 exit | All 3 splits defeated within 90s OR phase resets | DustSpear, MinionSwarm, BodyRecall | 400/400/400 (3 split bodies, kill all in window) | high (vis 0.2) |
| **4 — Reform Apex** | Phase-3 exit OR phase-3 timer expired | Tide chest HP ≤ 0 | DustSpear, WhirlwindRush, FinalGust, RegrowingArm | Chest 1800 / Head 800 / Arms 600 / Legs 600 | apex (vis 0.15) |

**Signature attacks:**

| Name | Telegraph | Hitbox | Counter | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SandSwarm** | passive — 8 sand-walker adds spawn at arena entry | Per-add melee + ranged dust spit | Kill adds | Phase 1 |
| **DustBlind** | 500 ms — sand cloud forms | Cone 6 tiles; -90% vis to anyone inside for 3s | Sidestep cloud | All phases |
| **DustSpear** | 800 ms — body coalesces a sand spear | Hitscan line, 60% HP, 15 tiles | Roll perpendicular | Phase 2 + 3 + 4 |
| **WhirlwindRush** | 1100 ms — body spins into vortex | Vortex hitbox 4-tile radius, moves 8 tiles/s toward nearest player for 3s | Outrun + jetpack | Phase 2 + 4 |
| **BlindingGust** | 900 ms — body raises arms; storm intensifies | Drops arena vis to 0.05 for 5s | Equip thermal-sight scope (M44A) OR hold position | Phase 2 only |
| **MinionSwarm** | 1500 ms — split bodies summon 6 lesser sand-walkers each | 18 total adds (6 per split) | Kill quickly; adds die when their parent split dies | Phase 3 only |
| **BodyRecall** | 1800 ms — split bodies try to reform | If all 3 splits not dead within 90s of phase entry, they recall to one location and reform with full HP; reset to phase 2 | Kill all 3 splits in window | Phase 3 only — the kill condition |
| **FinalGust** | 1200 ms — arena-wide telegraph; whole storm pulses | Whole arena 40% HP + 1 stack `blinded` | Cover behind tile OR drop prone | Phase 4 only; fires every 30s |
| **RegrowingArm** | 1000 ms — Tide regrows a destroyed arm | Restores 1 arm zone HP fully | Destroy the arm again before regrowth completes | Phase 4 only |

**Loot table:**

- 1× **Crimson Mote** artifact (passive: +1 tile vision in any dust-class weather state)
- 1× **Tide-Walker Helm** cosmetic trophy (visible swirling-dust VFX around helmet)
- 5× Codex unlocks: dust origin / storm intelligence theory / sand-walker biology / split-recall myth / tide's last form
- Standard rolls: 4-8 standard ammo, 2-3 dust mods, 300-600 credits

**Per-storyteller variants:**

- **Cassandra** — Phase 3 window shrinks to 75s; BodyRecall telegraph -200 ms.
- **Phoebe** — Phase 3 window extends to 120s; sand-walker adds health -25%.

- **Ironman** — Wipe = permadeath.
- **Sandbox** — Banked rewards; player can retreat via TimedRetreat (5-min window at arena edge after each phase).

**Per-difficulty tuning:** chill 0.6×/0.8×, tough_crowd 1.0×/1.0×, extreme 1.4×/1.3× + Phase 3 window -10s, nightmare 1.8×/1.6× + Phase 3 splits get +1 add each (4 splits instead of 3).

**Co-op vs solo balance:** HP scales `1.0 + 0.7 × (players - 1)`. With ≥ 2 alive, Phase 3 spawns 4 splits instead of 3 (one per player up to cap of 4). Phase 4 FinalGust gets `second_target` (covers the player's flank).

**Arena escape rule:** `TimedRetreat`. Player has a 5-minute window at arena edge between phases to retreat; arena seals after 5 min of continuous engagement.

### The Eclipse Walker — deep spec

**Lore strap** *(`content/narrative/bosses/eclipse_walker.lore.ron`)*: *"On Mimas the gravity is a suggestion. The Walker is what happens when something agrees with the suggestion harder than the rock does."*

**Arena:** Mimas microgravity, suspended platform cluster, TwoWayDoor. 100 × 100 tile microgravity arena (gravity = 0.064 g per M16 / M31). Tileset = `mimas_platform_cluster`. Per-fight environmental hazard = gravity inversion. Walker can flip the arena's gravity vector twice per fight; floor becomes ceiling. Jetpack is the player's only persistent floor.

| **2 — Gravity Twist** | Phase-1 exit | Chest HP < 25% | BladeRush, GravityInvert, OrbitStrike | Chest 1200 / Head 600 / Arms 400 / Legs 400 | inverted (0.064 g up) — first inversion |
| **3 — Sustained Duel** | Phase-2 exit | Chest HP ≤ 0 | BladeRush, GravityInvert, OrbitStrike, FinalArc | Chest 1800 / Head 700 / Arms 500 / Legs 500 | toggles every 25s |

**Signature attacks:**

| Name | Telegraph | Hitbox | Counter | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BladeRush** | 500 ms — Walker dashes 12 tiles forward | Cone 6 tiles forward, 40° | Sidestep + jetpack | All phases; dash-melee |
| **JetDodge** | 200 ms — passive evade frame after taking ≥ 200 dmg in 0.5s | Walker translates 4 tiles random direction | Predict + lead aim | Phase 1 only |
| **GravityProbe** | 900 ms — Walker scans player | Marks player; next BladeRush homes 30% | Break LOS during probe window | Phase 1 only |
| **GravityInvert** | 2000 ms — Walker plants both feet; arena pulses | All actors lose footing for 2s; gravity vector flips | Equip jetpack OR mag-boots (M44A) | Phase 2 (1× use) and Phase 3 (∞ use, every 25s) |

**Loot table:**

- 1× **Micro-Gravity Boots recipe** (T2, unique; passive +30% jetpack efficiency in <0.2 g)
- 1× **Cyborg Eye** artifact (passive: predicts BladeRush telegraphs 100 ms early in all enemy actors)
- 1× **Eclipse Visor** cosmetic trophy (visible black-glass visor with thin orange aperture)
- 5× Codex unlocks: cyborg origin / gravity-twist mechanism / Mimas dynasty / orbital duel myth / walker's vow

- **Sandbox** — Banked rewards via TwoWayDoor retreat.

**Per-difficulty tuning:** chill 0.6×/0.8×, tough_crowd 1.0×/1.0×, extreme 1.4×/1.3× + Phase 3 gravity toggle every 20s, nightmare 1.8×/1.6× + Phase 3 adds `OrbitalLance` (long-range hitscan, 90% HP, narrow telegraph).

**Co-op vs solo balance:** HP scales `1.0 + 0.7 × (players - 1)`. With ≥ 2 alive, OrbitStrike targets both players in alternation; GravityInvert affects all players simultaneously.

**Arena escape rule:** `TwoWayDoor`. Microgravity allows retreat; boss heals on disengage.

### The Last Star — deep spec

**Lore strap** *(`content/narrative/bosses/last_star.lore.ron`)*: *"Five biomes. Five lives. The Last Star is what becomes of a star that refused to die in one piece. To kill it you must die a little in each of the lives it remembers."*

**Arena:** Vulcan endgame, multi-biome arena cycler, OneWayCutsceneEntry. 300 × 300 tile composite arena with 5 sub-zones (volcanic / ice / dust / microgravity / void). Tileset = `vulcan_composite`. Per-fight environmental hazard = the arena cycles through all 5 biome zones, each lasting one phase.

**Phase FSM (5 phases — campaign capstone):**

| Phase | Entry | Exit | Biome zone active | Pool | Per-zone HP (core/arms/legs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Volcanic Body** | Cutscene | Arms HP ≤ 0 | volcanic (lava floor + 320 °C ambient) | LavaSweep, FireLance, MinionSummon | 9999 / 1200 / 800 |
| **2 — Frozen Body** | Phase-1 exit | Arms HP ≤ 0 | ice cathedral (−180 °C + reflective pads) | CryoBlast, ReflectiveShard, IceWall | 9999 / 1200 / 800 |
| **3 — Dust Body** | Phase-2 exit | Arms HP ≤ 0 | dust storm (vis 0.2 + traction 0.7) | DustSpear, WhirlwindRush, SandSwarm | 9999 / 1200 / 800 |
| **4 — Gravity Body** | Phase-3 exit | Arms HP ≤ 0 | microgravity (0.05 g + flip) | BladeRush, GravityInvert, OrbitStrike | 9999 / 1200 / 800 |
| **5 — Void Core** | Phase-4 exit | Core HP ≤ 0 (kill) | void (no atmosphere + zero g) | VoidPulse, StarBeam, BlackArc, FinalLight | 3600 / 800 / 800 |

**Signature attacks:** (per phase, the Last Star inherits the signature attacks of the corresponding boss biome, plus 1 unique attack per phase)

- **Phase 1 (volcanic):** LavaSweep, FireLance + unique `SolarFlare` (1200 ms telegraph; 360° HP-40% AOE).
- **Phase 2 (frozen):** CryoBlast, ReflectiveShard + unique `ThermalLensing` (1500 ms; doubles damage to player from next reflected beam).
- **Phase 3 (dust):** DustSpear, WhirlwindRush + unique `SandHymn` (800 ms; pulls all sand on the arena into a single column of HP-70% damage).
- **Phase 4 (gravity):** BladeRush, GravityInvert + unique `OrbitalChoir` (3 simultaneous orbit-strike passes from 3 angles).
- **Phase 5 (void):** VoidPulse / StarBeam / BlackArc / FinalLight — all unique to Last Star.

**Phase-5 unique attacks:**

| Name | Telegraph | Hitbox | Counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VoidPulse** | 1000 ms — core dims | Whole arena -1 oxygen tank stack per player (M16 vacuum-exposure) | Equip backup oxygen OR Methane-Breather origin |
| **StarBeam** | 600 ms — pinpoint laser tracer | Hitscan; 90% HP | Roll perpendicular |
| **BlackArc** | 1500 ms — slow-motion arc; visible darkening | Single 5-tile arc, 100% HP on direct | Break LOS |
| **FinalLight** | 3000 ms — full arena flash; cutscene | Whole arena 50% HP; cosmetic flash | Cover behind void platform (only 4 available; player must rotate) |

**Kill condition:** Phase 5 core HP ≤ 0. The player MUST complete phases 1-4 in order; cannot skip. Phase 5 is locked until 4 is cleared.

**Loot table:**

- 1× **Stellar Forge recipe** (T4, unique; only crafting station that produces neutronium-core ammo)
- 1× **Last Star Crystal** artifact (passive: +10% damage in all 5 biome types; auras stack)
- 1× **Star-Crown Halo** cosmetic trophy (visible orbital halo VFX rotating around helmet)
- 5× Codex unlocks: solar dynasty origin / 5-biome myth / void core theory / starforge history / last words of the star
- Standard rolls: 5-10 plasma cells, 2-4 T4 mods, 1000-2000 credits, 1 T4 weapon

**Per-storyteller variants:**

- **Cassandra** — Phase 5 FinalLight fires every 30s; phases 1-4 each have +1 enrage attack at HP threshold.
- **Phoebe** — 60s breather between phases (Last Star kneels in a star-fold); telegraphs +200 ms.
- **Randy** — 25% roll PER PHASE to spawn a previous-phase mini-boss as a parallel threat (Phase 3 dust + Phase 1 Hollow King mini = 25% roll).
- **Ironman** — Wipe = permadeath. Last Star is the campaign capstone; an Ironman wipe ends the campaign.
- **Sandbox** — Banked rewards; player can retreat between phases via in-arena lift; on return, boss restarts at retreat-phase entry.

**Per-difficulty tuning:** chill 0.6×/0.8×, tough_crowd 1.0×/1.0×, extreme 1.4×/1.3× + every phase gains 1 enrage attack at 50% HP, nightmare 1.8×/1.6× + Phase 5 FinalLight platforms reduced from 4 to 2.

**Co-op vs solo balance:** HP scales `1.0 + 0.7 × (players - 1)`. Each phase's unique attack gains `second_target` per the canonical rule. Phase-5 VoidPulse threat doubles with co-op (one oxygen stack per player per pulse).

**Arena escape rule:** `OneWayCutsceneEntry`. Final commitment. Player must complete all 5 phases or wipe.

## Notes for the implementer

- **Per-boss content lives in `content/bosses/<id>/*.ron`.** The Rust modules under `cf-boss/src/content/` are thin wrappers that load these RON files at registry init. Do not hardcode attack tables in Rust.
- **Phase HP rows must reference M13's 15-zone grammar by zone-id string** (`"chest"`, `"head"`, `"arm_l"`, `"arm_r"`, `"leg_l"`, `"leg_r"`, etc.). Do NOT introduce a boss-specific zone enum.
- **Telegraph windows are sim-ticks, not wall-clock.** Convert authored ms to ticks using `cf-sim-core::tick_rate` at registry init. Do NOT hardcode 60 Hz; M14J + others have already proven the value of tick-rate configurability.
- **All boss RNG (attack pool selection, loot rolls, minion spawn positions) MUST seed off the engine's deterministic RNG.** `thread_rng` is banned per AGENTS.md.
- **Per-storyteller variants are deltas, not overrides.** The base attack table is authored once in `attacks.ron`; the variant file applies a delta (telegraph_ms += / cadence_s -=). Avoid duplicating the full table per variant.
- **Boss arena hazard transitions (lava flood / gravity flip / storm intensify / biome cycle) MUST emit `boss.environmental_hazard_changed` events at the tick of transition.** Replay determinism depends on it.
- **Kill conditions beyond "HP to zero" need explicit `KillCondition` enum extension** in M25's `cf-boss` (`HpToZero` / `HpToZeroDuring(Phase)` / `MultiBodyAllDeadWithin(secs)` / `CoreOverloadVia(damage_type)` / `SequencedPhasesCompleted`). Coordinate with M25's owner if any new variants required.
- **Co-op `second_target` selection MUST be a deterministic next-nearest-eligible-player function**, not random. Determinism is non-negotiable for replay export.
- **Per-difficulty tuning lives in `difficulty.ron` per boss.** Do NOT add difficulty multipliers to the base attack table; the scheduler reads the difficulty file at fight start and applies as a multiplier matrix.
- **The Hollow King's lava-floor flood and the Crimson Tide's storm intensification interact with M16 hazard + M31B weather subsystems.** The boss engine must emit the right driver events (`hazard.fire_spread`, `weather.state_transitioned`) so those subsystems own the actual tile updates; the boss never directly writes to terrain.
- **The Last Star's 5-biome arena cycler is the largest scope risk in this spec.** Author it as a sequence of `biome_zone_active` state transitions where each transition swaps the per-fight environmental hazard module; do NOT try to keep all 5 biome simulations live simultaneously.
- **Co-op scaling formula `1.0 + 0.7 × (players - 1)` matches M48's canonical multiplier**; if M48 changes the formula, update M45B's `coop.ron` files in lockstep.
