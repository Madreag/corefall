# M19B — Fuel Production + Refining Chain

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Ship the full Stationeers-grade **fuel production + refining chain** that drives vehicles, generators, and rockets — hydrocarbon harvesting + cracking + distillation + storage + dispensing — the missing piece between M30/M35 mining and M44 transport that currently leaves "where does dropship fuel come from?" as a hand-waved.

## Player-facing behavior

- **Harvest raw fuel material**: oil reservoir geyser (M19) extracts crude oil; natural gas geyser yields methane; uranium ore (M30) feeds reactor fuel rods; coal ore feeds chemical generators.
- **Refining chain**: crude oil → distillation column → kerosene / diesel / heavy fuel oil + waste tar. Each fraction has a calorific value + boiling point + storage requirement.
- **Cracking + reforming**: heavy fuel → cracker → lighter fractions; natural gas + steam → hydrogen (electrolyzer M19 alternative path).
- **Storage**: fuel tanks (T1 50 L / T2 200 L / T3 1000 L) + pressurized canisters + flammable hazard markings; rupture cascade if punctured.
- **Dispensing**: M28C refuel pump pulls from base storage to vehicle fuel tanks; per-fuel-type compatibility check (diesel ≠ kerosene; wrong fuel = engine damage).
- **Per-world fuel availability**: Earth = oil + gas + coal abundant; Mars = limited; Vulcan = oil from organic deposits; Mimas = methane abundant (planetary atmosphere itself).
- **Player learns**: rocket needs liquid hydrogen + liquid oxygen → must run electrolyzer 24 hours + cool to cryo → strategic supply-chain gameplay.

## The fuel chain (locked launch chemistry)

| Raw input | Process | Output | Energy density (MJ/L) | Storage |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Crude oil (from oil geyser) | Distillation @ 350°C | Diesel + Kerosene + Heavy Fuel + Tar | 36 / 43 / 42 / waste | T1/T2/T3 tank |
| Heavy Fuel Oil | Catalytic cracker @ 600°C | Diesel + LPG + Naphtha | up | Pressurized |
| Natural gas (methane) | Direct burn OR steam reform | Energy OR Hydrogen | 50 / via H2 | Pressurized |
| Water | Electrolysis (M29 power coupled) | Hydrogen + Oxygen | 0.888 kg O2 / 0.112 kg H2 per kg water | Pressurized cryo |
| Hydrogen (gas) | Cryocooler -253°C | Liquid Hydrogen | 8.5 (cryo) | Cryo |
| Oxygen (gas) | Cryocooler -183°C | Liquid Oxygen | (oxidizer) | Cryo |
| Uranium ore | Enrichment reactor | Uranium fuel rod (90% U-235) | nuclear | Reactor chamber |
| Coal (M30 ore) | Direct burn in chemical_solid generator | Energy + CO2 | 24 (per kg) | Inventory or hopper |

## Out of scope

- Synthetic biology fuel (bioreactor producing hydrocarbons from algae) — M48+ exotic.
- Fusion reactor fuel (deuterium / tritium) — M48+ endgame tech tree.
- Antimatter (sci-fi exotic fuel) — never.
- Per-actor manual fuel mixing (Stationeers-style chemistry minigame) — M34 Material Lab.

## Notes for the implementer

- Per-fuel-type Stationeers values: diesel calorific value 36 MJ/L, kerosene 43, heavy fuel 42, hydrogen (gas) 12 MJ/kg, liquid hydrogen 8.5 MJ/L, methane 50 MJ/kg.
- Distillation column requires per-fraction temperature control (350°C for diesel / 200°C for kerosene); IC10 chips can automate the per-cut temp.
- Cracking + reforming chemistry uses M19 combustion table's stoichiometric solver — same kernel.
- Electrolyzer-cracker chain is the gateway to high-energy chemical economy; ~24 hours of base operation = enough fuel for one rocket flight = real strategic pacing.
- Compatibility check at refuel: per-vehicle accepts a `fuel_type` whitelist; rejecting wrong fuel prevents engine damage but applies "Compatibility issue" warning.
