# M14B — Gravity Field + Wind Force Producers

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Universal gravity field with per-cell/per-region overrides + atmospheric wind force producer + gas stratification — the producer side of every "M14A consumes wind / M14A reads `GravityField::sample(pos)`" PARITY gate that today reads from stubs returning Earth defaults.

## Player-facing behavior

- Gravity well anomalies bend walk paths and projectile trajectories visibly per cell.
- Low-g labs let actors jump 2× higher, jet 2× longer; magnetic boots cancel the effect.
- Damaged grav generator → progressive collapse from edges; visible wave-front through chunked grid.
- Pipe rupture → high-velocity jet streams; standing in the jet path applies `wind_force_applied` impulse that staggers light actors and pushes heavy actors.
- CO2 sinks to floor; H2 + He rise to ceiling; oil floats on water; visible per-cell layering at scenario-edit time + replay-stable per tick.
- Walking into a wind-tunnel cell (per-cell pressure differential ≥ 10 kPa) drifts the chassis lateral per Stationeers wind formula `F = ΔP × aperture_area`.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: GravityField::sample returns vector with magnitude + direction
  Given a scenario with default gravity { magnitude: 9.81, direction: [0, -1] }
  When cf_physics::GravityField::sample([100.0, 50.0]) is called
  Then it returns GravityVec { magnitude: 9.81, direction: [0.0, -1.0] }
  And the result is deterministic across 1000+ ticks

Scenario: Gravity well override bends actor trajectory
  Given a scenario with override { kind: UniformWell, center: [200, 100], radius: 50, magnitude: 25.0 }
  When an actor walks tangent to the well center at 10 px range
  Then their velocity vector curves toward the well center by ~0.05 rad/tick
  And gravity_override_activated event fires at entry

Scenario: Low-g lab cell doubles jump height
  Given an actor in a cell with override { kind: RegionLowG, local_g: 4.9 }
  When the actor jumps with the same impulse as on Earth
  Then peak vertical displacement is 2× baseline ± 5%
  And M14A PARITY-97 acceptance also passes

Scenario: Magnetic boots cancel grav override locally
  Given an actor in a low-g cell with magnetic_boots equipped
  When the override would multiply gravity by 0.5
  Then the actor's local gravity returns to baseline 9.81
  And HUD shows "MAGNETIC ANCHOR" banner

Scenario: Wind from pressure differential applies actor impulse
  Given two adjacent cells: cell_A at 101 kPa, cell_B at 96 kPa
  And an aperture between them with area 0.5 m²
  When the actor stands in cell_B's flow path
  Then atmos.wind_force_applied fires per tick with force_n proportional to (5 kPa × 0.5 m² × constant)
  And the actor's velocity gains a lateral component toward cell_B
  And the M14A consumer (PARITY-86) observes the wind

Scenario: CO2 sinks to floor; H2 rises to ceiling
  Given a sealed 16-tile-high room with mixed CO2 + H2 + N2 atmosphere
  When 120 ticks elapse
  Then bottom-row CO2 fraction has increased ≥ 5%
  And top-row H2 fraction has increased ≥ 5%
  And atmos.gas_stratified fires per stratification step
  And replay is byte-identical across two runs of the same seed

Scenario: Pipe rupture jet stream pushes nearby actor
  Given a Stationeers gas pipe with 70 MPa pressure that ruptures
  And an actor 4 tiles away on the jet axis
  When the rupture event fires (cf-atmos pipe rupture)
  Then a high-pressure flow vector emits from rupture point
  And the actor receives lateral impulse via cf-physics::angular_impulse_from_offcenter_hit
  And atmos.wind_force_applied fires with force_n > stagger_threshold for light actors

Scenario: Determinism replay across 600 ticks
  Given two engines with the same seed and identical wind + gravity override scenario
  When 600 ticks elapse
  Then identical actor.position per tick
  And identical event stream (every wind_force_applied + gas_stratified + gravity_override matches)
  And identical SaveBlob.checksum at tick 600
```

## Out of scope

- Magnetic field separate from gravity (M37 owns EM disruption; M19 owns charged-gas effects).
- Per-pixel resolution of gravity (per-cell is the contract — pixel resolution is M48+ ambition).
- Anti-gravity weaponization beyond consumer reading (M48 covert ops may use it).
- Full Stationeers pipe-network gas-flow model (M19 owns that; M14B only consumes rupture events).

## Notes for the implementer

- Wind impulse direction must include gravity bias (vertical wind from chimney-effect = real). Combine `wind_horizontal` from ΔP + `wind_vertical` from temperature + buoyancy.
- Gravity overrides MUST stack deterministically. Order: scenario-base → per-region → per-cell → per-actor (magnetic boots). Last writer wins.
- Stratification step runs at 1/4 the atmospherics tick rate (every 4th tick) to amortize the per-cell reorder cost; checksum still per-tick.
- Test with M14A's PARITY-86 (wind) + PARITY-97 (low-g jump arc) + PARITY-121 (per-cell GravityField sampling) — those three close the loop.
