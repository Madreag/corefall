# M44D — Combat Ground Vehicles (Tank + IFV + APC + SPG + Recon)

## Status

`active`

## Intent

5 combat ground-vehicle archetypes — **Light Tank**, **IFV**, **APC**, **Self-Propelled Gun (SPG)**, **Armored Recon Vehicle (ARV)** — built on M44A's `cf-vehicle` chassis + M44C's per-component damage + M13's chassis grammar (3-layer armor on a hull body-graph) + M14C's HEAT/APFSDS/ERA rounds. Closes the "trucks but no tanks" gap. Each vehicle is **chassis-grade**: hull is a body graph with front/side/rear/top/belly armor zones; turret is a sub-chassis with its own traverse + elevation; crew sit in named slots (driver / gunner / commander / loader); main gun reloads in real seconds from a real ammo rack; co-axial MG slaves to turret; radio is a hard-killable component.

## Canonical ownership

Owns the **combat-vehicle chassis layer**: turret sub-chassis, crew-slot ergonomics, ammo-rack-as-physical-compartment, chassis-mounted-weapon distinct from infantry-weapon, vehicle-grade ERA mount points, hull-armor-zone × round-kind interaction. Does NOT redefine: drivetrain (M44A), suspension / tire grip / per-component HP (M44C), chassis grammar / armor cascade (M13), ammo physics (M14C), penetration ray (M14).

## Player-facing behavior

- **Light Tank (T3, tracked)** — 1200 steel + 200 titanium + 80 motor + tank_gun_75mm + 1× T4 battery; 12 hr. Driver + gunner + commander; co-axial MG; 40 main / 800 MG. Armor: front 80 / side 40 / rear 25 / top 20 / belly 15 mm. 4 ERA mounts.
- **IFV (T3, tracked)** — 800 steel + 80 motor + autocannon_30mm + ATGM_launcher + 1× T3 battery; 8 hr. Driver + gunner + commander + 6 passengers (rear-door dismount). 30mm HE + AP belts; 2× ATGM. Armor: 30 / 20 / 12 mm.
- **APC (T2, wheeled-amphibious)** — 500 steel + 50 motor + heavy_mg_12_7mm + 1× T3 battery; 6 hr. Driver + commander + 8 passengers. RWS 12.7mm HMG (no co-axial). Armor: 20 / 15 / 8 mm. Amphibious — reuses M44F Amphibious Truck buoyancy + wade pose.
- **SPG (T3, tracked)** — 1500 steel + 100 motor + howitzer_155mm; 8 hr. Driver + gunner + commander + loader. 25 rounds, 0-65° elevation, 15°/s turret. **No loader = 4× slower reload.**
- **ARV (T2, wheeled)** — 400 steel + 60 motor + autocannon_20mm; 4 hr. Driver + gunner + commander + radio-operator. Thermal scope + long-range radio. Armor: 15 / 10 / 6 mm. Top speed 240 px/s (vs Light Tank 120).

### Per-SKU datasheet locked field: `wheeled_or_tracked`

Each combat-vehicle .ron declares an explicit `drivetrain_kind` enum so the sim can dispatch to the right contact-patch model:

| SKU | `drivetrain_kind` | Physics source |
|---|---|---|
| Light Tank | `tracked` | M44E `cf-vehicle::tracked` (per-tread contact + skid-steer + track-shed) |
| IFV | `tracked` | M44E `cf-vehicle::tracked` |
| APC | `wheeled_amphibious` | M44C wheeled + M44F buoyancy override when on water tile |
| SPG | `tracked` | M44E `cf-vehicle::tracked` |
| ARV | `wheeled` | M44C wheeled |

Enum: `enum DrivetrainKind { Wheeled, Tracked, WheeledAmphibious }`. Lives on `combat_vehicle.ron` alongside `chassis_kind`. The combat-vehicle sim dispatches contact-patch resolution off this enum — wheeled goes through M44C tire-grip; tracked goes through M44E tread-contact-patch; wheeled-amphibious dispatches to M44F buoyancy when over a M16 water tile and M44C wheeled otherwise.
- **Crew matters.** Driver killed = immobilized until reseat (10s). Gunner killed = main + co-ax offline. Loader killed = 4× reload. Commander killed = 50% slower target acquisition. Inherits M13 pilot-eject + M14 wound model.
- **Ammo rack is a physical compartment** with HP + position. Penetrating round triggers cookoff per M14: turret pops off, hull crew die, wreck explodes 5s; kill-cam plays ammo_rack_cookoff.
- **Co-axial MG slaves to turret traverse.** Hull MG (if equipped) is a separate gunner station with its own arc.
- **Reload is real.** Each main-gun round drawn from rack by the loader actor (or auto-loader sub-module). SPG: 12s auto / 18s manual. Light Tank: 8s auto / 12s manual. IFV autocannon: belt-fed, no per-round reload.
- **Radio module is hard-killable.** Penetrating hit (rear hull / turret top) → no commander override, no convoy waypoints (M44B), no artillery call. Aerial decal blown off.
- **ERA blocks physically mounted.** Per M14C they pre-detonate HEAT + reduce APFSDS at angle. Field-refit via cfctl; detonated slot empty until reloaded.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Crew slot binding routes player input per role
  Given a Light Tank with empty crew slots
  When the player issues act.player.enter_seat { vehicle_id, seat_id: "driver" } (per M44A canonical)
  Then the actor moves into the driver slot, drive inputs steer the vehicle, turret-aim inputs are rejected, and observe.vehicle reports the binding

Scenario: Main gun reload timing depends on loader presence
  Given a Light Tank with gunner + commander but no loader
  When the gunner fires the main gun
  Then the reload timer is 12s (manual fallback)
  And when a loader enters the loader slot mid-reload, the remaining time scales down to 8s (auto-loader rate) and vehicle_main_gun_reloaded emits with loader_present=true

Scenario: Ammo rack detonation on penetrating hit
  Given a Light Tank with 20 rounds in the rack
  When an APFSDS round penetrates the rack module
  Then vehicle_ammo_rack_detonated emits, hull crew take fatal damage, turret detaches as debris, and kill-cam plays ammo_rack_cookoff

Scenario: ERA pre-detonates incoming HEAT
  Given an IFV with ERA on the front hull
  When a HEAT round impacts the ERA slot
  Then the block detonates per M14C era_pre_detonates_heat, residual jet penetration falls below hull RHA, IFV takes no internal damage, and the slot is marked empty until refit

Scenario: Co-axial MG slaves to turret traverse
  Given an APC with RWS 12.7mm HMG
  When the gunner traverses the turret by 30°
  Then the co-axial line-of-fire follows the same offset, and firing draws from the secondary belt (not main rounds)

Scenario: Radio destruction takes the vehicle off-net
  Given a Light Tank linked to a convoy via M44B
  When a penetrating hit destroys the radio module
  Then vehicle_radio_destroyed emits, convoy waypoints stop reaching it, and commander override is unavailable until repair

Scenario: SPG indirect fire arcs over terrain
  Given an SPG with 155mm howitzer at elevation 45°
  When the gunner fires at a target 600m away behind a hill
  Then the trajectory traces a parabola clearing the crest, the shell impacts within HE splash radius, and vehicle_main_gun_fired records elevation + indirect=true

Scenario: Driver killed by penetrating round
  Given a Light Tank with all 3 crew slots bound
  When an APFSDS round penetrates the driver compartment
  Then the driver takes fatal damage, the vehicle is immobilized for 10s, crew_member_killed emits with role=Driver, and the commander may reseat via act.player.enter_seat { vehicle_id, seat_id: "driver" } (M44A canonical)

Scenario: IFV dismount via rear door
  Given an IFV with 4 infantry in passenger slots
  When the commander issues exit_crew_slot for each
  Then each spawns at the rear-door egress, resumes M14A walking, and observe.vehicle reports 0 passengers
```

## Notes for implementer

- Turret = **sub-chassis** on the hull via a rotation-axis joint. Reuse M13 chassis grammar — turret gets its own body graph (turret_face / sides / rear / commander_cupola). Do not collapse to a flat "module" or you lose zone-level armor resolution.
- Crew binding sets `actor.stance = CrewSeated { role }`. Actor mass still aggregates into the vehicle; sim state freezes wrt walking but stays alive for damage routing.
- Ammo rack cookoff: rack HP → 0 → spawn N explosion entities (1 per round) → apply per-zone damage to hull → detach turret as debris via M14 chassis-salvage path.
- ERA refit (cfctl `refit_era`): consume one era_block from inventory, snap to named slot, increment `era_slot.loaded`. Slot data lives in vehicle .ron (slot_id + position + arc).
- SPG indirect fire uses M14 ballistic integration with M19 wind. HUD shows a dashed-arc preview during aim.
- Tank-crew doctrine shares M22 pathfinder at vehicle scale; treat the tank as one navmesh agent of width = hull_width + buffer.
- Per-vehicle .ron mirrors M13's chassis .ron — do NOT fork the schema. Declare crew slot positions, ammo rack position, ERA slots, weapon mounts, armor zone HP per layer.
- `fire_coax` is a separate cfctl from `fire_main_gun`; both inherit turret pose; co-axial draws from an ammo pool flagged `feed=belt`.
