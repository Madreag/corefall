# M26C — Trading Post UX + Per-Faction Currency + Exchange Rates + Bartering Grammar + Contraband + Market Simulation

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M26C is the deep-trade economy milestone** — the layer that turns M25's "buy menu" + M26's quartermasters into a populated market economy. After M26C, every faction issues its own currency with volatile cross-rates, every NPC vendor rotates inventory per cycle, every transaction can be cash, barter, or contraband, and prices respond to supply-and-demand pressure rather than a flat lookup table.

M26C splits this out of M26 because trade-economy depth is its own coherent scope (8 currencies + exchange-rate engine + barter grammar + contraband classification + per-vendor rotation + market simulation) that would overwhelm M26's social/narrative pass.

M26C promise: **"every faction has its own money; the rates between them swing day to day; you can pay in cash, swap goods, or smuggle contraband past the customs check — and tomorrow's prices reflect today's mining boom or grain shortage."**

## Player-facing behavior

### 8 launch currencies (per-faction issuance)

Each currency is issued by one faction / race coalition and is the *preferred* legal tender at vendors aligned with that group. Currencies are NOT interchangeable at face value — they convert via the exchange-rate engine (see below).

| Currency | Issuer | Symbol | Form factor | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Coalition Credits** (`CC`) | Allied Resistance / Coalition bloc | ⊕ | Chip card | Reserve currency; lowest volatility; accepted at most neutral ports |
| **Ronin Marks** (`RM`) | Mercenary Guild / Marauder coalition | ◇ | Stamped alloy coin | Combat-economy bias; spikes during faction wars |
| **Insectoid Chitin** (`IC`) | Insectoid hive faction | ⌬ | Carved chitin plate | Biological; degrades 1% per cycle in non-hive storage |
| **Crystalline Shards** (`CS`) | Crystalline race faction | ✦ | Polished mineral fragment | Indestructible; doubles as crafting reagent |
| **Methane-Breather Glyphs** (`MG`) | Methane Breather coalition | ⧗ | Etched cryo-tile | Requires cryogenic transport; spoils above -90 °C |
| **Aqueous Pearls** (`AP`) | Aqueous race faction | ◯ | Cultured pearl | Per-pearl size grades into denominations (1/5/25/100) |
| **Photosynth Seeds** (`PS`) | Photosynthetic race faction | ❋ | Live seed pod | Loses 5% value per cycle in darkness; ideal for solar bases |
| **Heavy-Biomech Bio-Tokens** (`BT`) | Heavy Biomech race faction | ◉ | Bio-printed wafer | Cryptographically signed; impossible to counterfeit but expensive to mint |

**Wallet model** — the player carries a `Wallet { BTreeMap<CurrencyId, u64> }` per actor; the build-point (BP) base currency from M46 is currency-zero (`BP`) and exchanges with all 8 launch currencies. Transactions that exceed available funds *of the asked currency* either fail or trigger an exchange offer at the vendor's posted rate (see below).

### Exchange-rate engine (volatile cross-rates)

A single `ExchangeRateMatrix` of size `9 × 9` (BP + 8 launch currencies) lives in `cf-market`. Each cell `rate[from][to]` is a `f32` updated per market tick.

Rate drivers (per tick, deterministic per-shard seed):

- **Base rates** seeded from `content/markets/launch_rates.json` (BP-anchored).
- **Supply-shock multiplier** from cf-supply-chain (M28G): a faction with grain shortage sees its currency *appreciate* against food-rich currencies because food sellers demand a premium. A faction with mineral surplus sees its currency *depreciate*.
- **Diplomatic-tension multiplier** from cf-faction (M26): hostile relations widen the spread (buy-sell gap); allied relations narrow it.
- **Faction-war shock**: when M26 `faction.war_declared` fires, the warring factions' currencies spike +/-20% within one cycle and decay over 5 cycles.
- **Random-walk noise**: ±2% per cycle per pair, seeded off the deterministic engine RNG (NEVER `thread_rng`).

Volatility cap: ±35% from base rate per cycle (prevents currency death-spirals from one bad random roll).

Events: `market.rate_updated { from, to, prev, new }`, `market.rate_spike { currency, magnitude_pct, cause }`.

### Trading Post UX

Every NPC vendor (M26 quartermasters + new neutral vendors at trading posts) opens a unified Trading Post panel via `act.player.open_trading_post`. The panel has 4 tabs:

1. **Buy** — vendor inventory grid; per-item price displayed in vendor's preferred currency *and* in player's wallet currencies at current rate.
2. **Sell** — player inventory grid; vendor offers a price (typically 50-70% of buy-back at neutral standing; scaled per M26 relationship).
3. **Barter** — drag player items left, vendor items right; relative-value engine displays delta and accepts when delta ≤ vendor's barter tolerance.
4. **Exchange** — convert one currency to another at vendor's posted rate (which includes a 2-8% spread above the market rate, scaled by relationship).

Visual indicators:

- **Affordability colors**: green (can afford in preferred currency), yellow (can afford via exchange), red (insufficient).
- **Contraband chip** (red ⚠ glyph) appears on any item flagged as contraband for the vendor's home faction (see Contraband below).
- **Rotation badge** appears on items added in the current rotation cycle ("NEW").
- **Demand indicator** appears on items with `demand > supply` at this vendor ("HOT — +18% over base").

### Bartering grammar (item-for-item with relative-value engine)

Barter is a structured offer: player puts N items on the table, vendor puts M items on the table, the engine computes a delta and the vendor accepts / rejects / counter-offers.

```rust
pub struct BarterOffer {
    pub player_side: Vec<(ItemId, u32)>,
    pub vendor_side: Vec<(ItemId, u32)>,
    pub cash_top_up: Option<(CurrencyId, i64)>,  // optional cash sweetener (either direction)
}

pub enum BarterVerdict {
    Accept,
    Reject { reason: BarterRejection },
    CounterOffer(BarterOffer),
}

pub enum BarterRejection {
    UndervaluedByPct(f32),            // delta exceeds vendor tolerance
    ItemNotWanted(ItemId),            // vendor refuses this item entirely
    ContrabandRefused(ItemId),        // vendor refuses contraband for their faction
    InsufficientRelationship,         // vendor won't barter below threshold
    InventoryFull,                    // vendor can't take more of this item
}
```

Relative-value computation per side:

- Each item has a base value in BP (from `content/items/<id>.ron` `base_value_bp` field).
- Multiplied by vendor's per-item demand factor (per cf-market supply-demand curves).
- Multiplied by item condition (per M27 durability).
- Multiplied by faction-of-origin adjustment (vendor pays premium for items from their faction; discount for items from rivals).
- Summed for both sides; converted to vendor's preferred currency for display.

Vendor barter tolerance = base 5% + relationship-bonus (max 25% at +90 relationship). Below tolerance threshold → reject or counter-offer.

### Contraband per faction

Each faction publishes a `ContrabandPolicy` declaring which item categories / specific items are forbidden in their territory.

```rust
pub struct ContrabandPolicy {
    pub faction_id: FactionId,
    pub forbidden_categories: BTreeSet<ItemCategory>,    // e.g. {Explosives, Narcotics, BioWeapons}
    pub forbidden_items: BTreeSet<ItemId>,               // specific items (e.g. "intel_dossier_xyz")
    pub penalty: ContrabandPenalty,
    pub customs_check_chance: f32,                       // 0.0..1.0 chance per cycle / per cross
}

pub enum ContrabandPenalty {
    Confiscate,
    Confiscate_PlusFine(CurrencyId, u64),
    Confiscate_PlusReputationLoss(i32),
    Confiscate_PlusJail(u32),                            // tick count in M26 holding cell
    Confiscate_PlusKill,                                 // hardcore: shoot on detection
}
```

Launch baseline:

| Faction | Forbidden categories | Default penalty |
|---|---|---|
| Allied Resistance / Coalition | Narcotics, BioWeapons | Confiscate + Fine (200 CC) |
| Mercenary Guild | (none — anything-goes) | n/a |
| Insectoid hive | Pesticides, Fire weapons | Confiscate + Kill |
| Crystalline | Acid, Sonic weapons | Confiscate + Reputation -30 |
| Methane Breather | Combustibles, Heat sources | Confiscate + Jail 600 ticks |
| Aqueous | Toxins, Heavy metals | Confiscate + Fine (50 AP) |
| Photosynthetic | Herbicides, Defoliants | Confiscate + Reputation -50 |
| Heavy Biomech | Cybernetic-disruptor weapons | Confiscate + Fine (10 BT) |

### Smuggling mechanics

Players can attempt to bring contraband through customs:

- **Customs check** triggers when entering a faction-controlled zone with contraband in inventory.
- Check is a roll: `succeed = roll(customs_check_chance × concealment_factor)`.
- **Concealment factor** comes from:
  - Container choice (hidden compartment in chassis modules from M14; +30% concealment).
  - Smuggler perk (M27 perk tree, +20% per rank up to 3 ranks).
  - Bribery: at customs gate, offer cash; reduces check chance proportional to bribe / customs-officer-greed.
- **Smuggler reputation** (per faction): each successful smuggle increases shadow-reputation with that faction's black market (unlocks higher-tier illicit vendors); each failed check reduces standard reputation.

Events: `smuggling.customs_check { zone, items_flagged, success }`, `smuggling.bribe_offered`, `smuggling.contraband_confiscated`, `smuggling.shadow_reputation_changed`.

### Per-NPC vendor inventory rotation per cycle

Each vendor has a `VendorInventoryProfile`:

```rust
pub struct VendorInventoryProfile {
    pub vendor_id: NpcId,
    pub rotation_cycle_days: u32,            // default 3 days
    pub stock_pool: Vec<StockSlot>,          // pool to draw from
    pub stock_slots_per_rotation: u32,       // how many items rotate in per cycle
    pub guaranteed_items: BTreeSet<ItemId>,  // always in stock (vendor staples)
}

pub struct StockSlot {
    pub item_id: ItemId,
    pub weight: f32,                         // probability weight for selection
    pub min_quantity: u32,
    pub max_quantity: u32,
    pub min_relationship: i32,               // gated by relationship
}
```

At each rotation tick:

1. Vendor retains guaranteed items + items not yet sold from previous cycle.
2. Engine draws `stock_slots_per_rotation` new items from `stock_pool` weighted by `weight × demand_modifier`.
3. Quantities rolled uniformly in `[min_quantity, max_quantity]` per item.
4. `vendor.inventory_rotated { vendor_id, added, removed }` fires.

Per cf-market, items in *high demand globally* are *less likely* to be in any one vendor's stock (scarcity pressure); items in *high supply* are more likely.

### Market price simulation (supply/demand curves)

Per item category, the global market tracks supply (units produced in last N cycles) and demand (units sold in last N cycles).

```rust
pub struct MarketCurve {
    pub item_id: ItemId,
    pub base_price_bp: f32,
    pub global_supply_last_window: f32,      // sliding window, default 7 cycles
    pub global_demand_last_window: f32,
    pub current_multiplier: f32,             // applied to base_price_bp
}
```

Multiplier formula (clamped to `[0.4, 2.5]`):

```text
ratio = (demand + epsilon) / (supply + epsilon)
multiplier = clamp(ratio.powf(0.6), 0.4, 2.5)
```

Effects:

- Each vendor's posted price = `base_price_bp × multiplier × vendor_markup × relationship_modifier`.
- A mining boom (supply spike) drops mineral prices across all vendors.
- A faction-war drop in production (supply collapse) doubles weapons prices.
- M26 quest rewards that pay "in goods" auto-revalue per current multiplier so quest payout retains intent.

Events: `market.demand_recorded { item_id, qty }`, `market.supply_recorded { item_id, qty }`, `market.multiplier_updated { item_id, prev, new }`.

#### Storyteller-event market hook (M25 / M45C integration)

`cf-market::curves` subscribes to `act.storyteller.event_fired { event_id, severity }` (emitted by M25's storyteller registry when M45C events execute) and applies authored MarketCurve shifts per event kind. The shift table is data-driven in `content/markets/storyteller_event_market_shifts.ron`:

| Storyteller event | Affected item categories | Multiplier shift | Decay over |
|---|---|---|---|
| `trader_arrival` | `consumer_goods`, `luxury`, `recipes` | × 0.85 (prices drop while trader is in market) | duration of event (default 30 in-game min) |
| `mineral_rush` | `ore_iron`, `ore_copper`, `ore_uranium`, mineral derivatives | × 0.70 supply spike → price drop | 3 cycles linear decay |
| `famine` | `food_*`, `nutrient_paste`, `meal_*`, `crop_*` | × 1.80 demand spike → price climb | duration of event + 2 cycles |
| `pirate_raid` (any world) | `weapon_*`, `ammo_*`, `medkit_*` | × 1.25 demand spike on target world | 1 cycle decay |
| `volcanic`, `asteroid` | `ore_rare_earth`, `crystal_*` on impacted world | × 0.80 supply windfall | 5 cycles linear decay |
| `plague` | `medkit_*`, `antidote_*`, `respirator_*` | × 1.60 demand spike; `food_perishable` × 0.75 (rotting) | duration of event + 3 cycles |
| `faction_war` | `weapon_*` × 2.0, `ammo_*` × 1.8, `repair_*` × 1.4 | per-currency cross-rate spike per existing faction-war shock | 5 cycles |
| `eclipse` | `solar_panel_*` × 0.5 (useless), `battery_*` × 1.5 (in demand) | demand surge while dark | duration of event |

Per-event shift fires `market.storyteller_shift_applied { event_id, item_category, prev_multiplier, new_multiplier, decay_ticks }`. The shift is **stacked multiplicatively** on top of the supply/demand multiplier — both layers compose, neither replaces the other. Shifts decay linearly back to the supply/demand baseline over the authored `decay_ticks` window. Multiple concurrent events accumulate via `product(shifts)`, clamped to `[0.4, 2.5]` per the base multiplier cap.

Events: `market.storyteller_shift_applied { event_id, item_category, prev_multiplier, new_multiplier, decay_ticks }`, `market.storyteller_shift_decayed { event_id, item_category }`.

## Out of scope

- **Player-issued currency / banks** — players can't print their own currency in M26C (deferred to a much later milestone).
- **Loans + interest rates** — no credit instruments in M26C (deferred).
- **Stock exchange / commodity futures** — no derivative trading (deferred).
- **Per-shard global market events** — cross-server market coupling is M49 MMO scope.
- **Black-market shadow currency** — shadow-reputation exists, but a separate black-market scrip is not introduced in M26C.
- **Mercenary contract payment terms** — see M46B.
- **Taxes / tariffs / tribute** — see M46B.
- **Caravan escort missions / faction trade routes** — see M46B.

## Notes for the implementer

- The 9×9 ExchangeRateMatrix MUST be deterministic per shard seed. All random walks MUST seed off the engine RNG (no `thread_rng`).
- Currency-decay rules (Insectoid Chitin spoiling, Photosynth Seeds losing value in darkness) are PASSIVE drains on the wallet; do not surprise the player — surface a Wallet HUD chip when any held currency is decaying this cycle.
- Methane-Breather Glyphs spoiling above -90 °C should integrate with M19/M28B thermal kernel; if the player is in a non-cryogenic zone, the chip should show a countdown.
- The relative-value engine must handle currency-of-origin premium / rival-discount; do NOT short-circuit this to a flat multiplier (the asymmetry is the design intent — Mercenary Guild vendors should pay premium for Mercenary Guild gear).
- Customs-check RNG MUST seed off the engine RNG. Do NOT short-circuit the check at high concealment — keep the roll deterministic and observable.
- The Trading Post panel must reuse the M27 inventory-grid widget; do NOT fork a new grid. The drag-and-drop for barter is a new gesture on top of the existing grid.
- Vendor rotation tick should coexist with M46 upkeep cycle (both fire on cycle boundaries); ensure no double-firing if both are enabled.
- Do NOT silently round currency amounts — keep `i64` integer semantics for cash, `f32` only for rates. Currency arithmetic must be free of float drift.
