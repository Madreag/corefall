# M19F — Body Heat + Crew Heat Load + Humidity + Condensation

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Per-actor body-heat output (~100W continuous) + crew heat load × room volume modeling + humidity tracking + condensation formation on cold surfaces — Stationeers/ONI-grade gameplay where crowding a small room heats it up + breathing adds humidity + cold surfaces fog over.

## Player-facing behavior

- **Body heat output**: each actor in a room contributes per-origin baseline (human 100W, robot 50W, android 75W, heavy biomech 150W). 4 humans in a small room ≈ 400W of passive heat — visible temperature rise.
- **Crew heat × HVAC interplay**: large crew in cold-set room → HVAC must compensate with more cooling capacity → power draw scales.
- **Humidity tracking**: water vapor in atmosphere tracked per-room; breathing adds humidity; cooking adds humidity; cold surfaces condense it.
- **Condensation**: cold metal walls + warm-humid air → visible water droplets form → can pool into puddles on the floor → puddles evaporate when temp rises OR drain to atmospherics.
- **Dehumidifier device**: T2 base module; pulls humidity from atmosphere; produces clean water as byproduct (closed-loop benefit).
- **Per-actor sweat (humans)**: in hot rooms, humans sweat → drains hydration (M17 caloric/hydration) + adds humidity → reinforcing loop.
- **Per-room "stuffiness"**: combination of high humidity + low O2 + high CO2 → "stuffy" affliction → minor stamina drain + comfort penalty.
- **Frost formation**: ultra-cold surfaces (-30°C or below) build up frost from any humidity present → frost is a visual + minor obstacle.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: 4 humans in small room raise temperature
  Given a sealed 16-tile room at 18°C
  And 4 humans inside (4 × 100W = 400W body heat)
  When 300 ticks elapse without active cooling
  Then room temperature rises ≥ 0.5°C
  And body_heat_accumulated fires per actor per tick
  And HUD shows "ROOM TEMP RISING — 400W CREW LOAD"

Scenario: HVAC compensates for crew heat
  Given the same crowded room + thermostat setpoint 20°C
  And M28B AC unit active
  When the AC cycles
  Then temp holds at 20°C ±2°C despite 400W crew load
  And M29 power draw shows higher AC consumption
  And player sees "HVAC: 2.4 kW (crew compensation)"

Scenario: Humidity rises from breathing
  Given a sealed room with 4 humans breathing
  When 30 minutes elapse
  Then humidity rises from 30% baseline to 60%
  And humidity_threshold_crossed fires at 50%
  And HUD shows "HUMIDITY HIGH — 60%"
  And if no dehumidifier, condensation may form on cold surfaces

Scenario: Condensation forms on cold wall
  Given a room with humidity 65% + a metal wall at 5°C
  When the wall surface temp is below dew point
  Then condensation_formed fires
  And droplet decals render on the wall (visible)
  And droplets accumulate over time (puddles per M19 water material)
  And puddles can be wiped via M28B breach_repair_tool variant or evaporate when humidity drops

Scenario: Dehumidifier removes humidity + produces clean water
  Given a room with humidity 70% + dehumidifier_t2 active
  When 30 minutes elapse with 200W dehumidifier draw
  Then humidity drops to 40% baseline
  And ~5 L clean water byproduct accumulates in attached tank
  And HUD shows "WATER COLLECTED: 5 L"

Scenario: Frost on extreme cold surface
  Given a -30°C wall surface + humidity in adjacent room
  When humidity diffuses across boundary
  Then frost_formed fires on the wall
  And frost decals render visibly
  And frost can be melted by heating the surface OR scraped off manually

Scenario: Sweat drains hydration in hot rooms
  Given a human in a 40°C room
  When 5 minutes elapse
  Then actor_sweating fires per tick
  And M17 hydration drains at 3× baseline rate
  And humidity in the room rises (sweat evaporates → atmospheric humidity)
  And HUD warns "DEHYDRATING — find water"

Scenario: "Stuffy" affliction in crowded uncooled room
  Given 6 actors in a closed room with humidity 80% + O2 18 kPa + CO2 0.4%
  When 10 minutes elapse
  Then M16A stuffiness affliction triggers
  And actors take minor stamina penalty
  And HUD shows "STUFFY ROOM — ventilate or leave"

Scenario: Determinism across humidity + body heat
  Given two engines with identical crew loadout + identical scenario seed
  When 600 ticks of crew-heat + humidity simulation elapse
  Then identical event sequence
  And identical SaveBlob.checksum at tick 600
```

## Out of scope

- Per-actor metabolic rate modeling (different humans output different heat) — M48+ realism.
- Per-clothing humidity transfer (insulation vs sweat-wicking) — M48+ realism.
- Inter-room humidity diffusion via duct ports — abstracted via M28D damper config.
- Mold growth from prolonged high humidity — M48+ tropical biome scenarios.

## Notes for the implementer

- Per-origin body heat baseline: human 100W, robot 50W, android 75W, heavy biomech 150W. Configurable per actor archetype.
- Humidity tracked per-room as `f32` (0.0 = bone-dry; 1.0 = saturated); auto-computed dew point from temp + humidity.
- Condensation/frost only render when dew point or freezing point reached on physical wall material; not arbitrary.
- Dehumidifier byproduct routes via M19 pipe network to fresh-water storage (closed-loop atmospherics benefit).
- Sweat drain reads M17 hydration resource; integrates with M14A walk speed modifier (dehydrated actor walks slower).
