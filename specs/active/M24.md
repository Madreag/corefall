## Player-facing behavior

### Per-environmental-slice AI doctrine

**Atmospheric:**
- Hypoxic → seek breathable atmosphere OR retrieve oxygen tank from inventory OR retreat to airlock
- Combustible atm → avoid fire-spread tactics (no grenade throw in combustible room)
- Toxic atm → equip mask OR seek breathable area

**Gravitational:**
- Gravity anomaly → avoid affected zones; reroute path
- Low gravity → adapt aim (compensates for ballistic drift)
- High gravity → reduce sprint usage

**Thermal:**
- Hyperthermic → seek cooling shelter; equip heat-resistant gear
- Hypothermic → seek heat source; insulation
- Robot: downclock + retreat from heat source

**Radiation:**
- Move to shielded area
- Equip radiation suit
- Limit exposure time

**Photic:**
- Low visibility → use sensors + suppressors (less alarm)
- Glare → blocked by darkness; eye protection

**EM:**
- EM disruption → manual aim (sensors compromised)
- EMP recovery → wait for cool-down

**Acoustic (M9.5):**
- Vacuum no_voice → switch to radio comms
- Comms blackout → fall back to last-known orders

**Weather (M7.7):**
- Storm → seek shelter; reduce sprint
- Lightning → avoid open ground
- Dust storm → use IR sensors

### Reason labels for environmental decisions

Per `references/equipment-ai-behavior-contract`:
- `no_breathable_atmosphere` — bot seeks atmosphere
- `combustible_atm_no_grenade` — bot refuses grenade throw in volatile room
- `radiation_exposure_lethal` — bot retreats from radiation zone
- `gravity_anomaly_avoidance` — bot reroutes around anomaly
- `heat_critical_downclock` — robot bot retreats from heat
- `comms_blackout_fallback` — bot reverts to last-known orders

### Replay-visible decisions

Every environmental decision emits `ai.environmental_decision` event with reason_label.
