# M21 — BP5 Micro Pressure Hold Slice

## Status

`active`

## Intent

**M21 is the BP5 micro-fun-slice** — a 60-90s `micro_pressure_hold` scenario that proves M19 atmospherics + M20 environment aggregator work as a playable mission. Per BP5 fun-proof gate.

M21 promise: **"hold pressure in a breached habitat while the atmosphere fights you — every aperture, every gas, every breath matters."**

## Player-facing behavior

Scenario: micro_pressure_hold (60-90s):
- Habitat with 4 connected rooms; one wall has a slowly-growing breach
- Player must: seal breach (with foam/concrete tool) OR pump O2 from reserve tank
- Atmosphere drains via aperture per PV=nRT
- Toxic gas creeps in from adjacent compartment (combustible_atmosphere)
- Player can vent toxic gas via airlock (but loses pressure faster)
- Win: pressure > 80kPa at 60s mark. Lose: pressure < 30kPa OR oxygen < 15s.
