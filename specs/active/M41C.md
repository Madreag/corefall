# M41C — Skill-Based Matchmaking + Rank Tiers + Leaderboards + Seasons + Tournament Format

## Status

`active`

## Intent

M41C locks the **competitive ranking + tournament + leaderboards layer** that M41 + M41B declared but did not exhaust: per-mode MMR/Elo, 6 rank tiers (Bronze / Silver / Gold / Platinum / Diamond / Master), seasonal rank reset with soft-reset toward mid-tier, MMR decay for inactivity, smurf detection via win-loss-velocity heuristics, anti-throw protections for 5+ consecutive losses, four shippable tournament formats (single-elim 16, double-elim 16, round-robin 8, Swiss 16), per-mode top-100 global leaderboards, per-faction leaderboards, and DR-031-compliant ranking-only cosmetic decorations (rank-banner / rank-portrait-frame / seasonal-icon) that cannot be purchased and confer no gameplay power. M41 ships online co-op + endgame Tournament mode skeleton; M41B ships cross-region MMR balancing primitives; M41C is the milestone that turns those primitives into a full, season-driven competitive ladder.

## Player-facing behavior

- **Per-mode MMR is tracked separately.** A player who is Diamond in `pvp_arena` can still be Silver in `bunker_defence` — the two ratings are independent and shown side-by-side on the player's rank panel.
- **6 rank tiers, transparent and deterministic.** Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Master, each split into 3 sub-divisions (III/II/I). Promotion / demotion happens at fixed MMR thresholds documented in `content/matchmaking/rank_tiers.ron`; no hidden modifiers.
- **Soft seasonal reset preserves competitive identity.** At season rollover, every player's MMR is pulled 40% of the way toward 1200 (the population mean). A Master player at 2400 enters the new season at ~1920 (mid-Diamond); a Bronze player at 700 enters at ~900 (mid-Bronze). Nobody starts at zero; nobody keeps their full peak.
- **MMR decay punishes inactivity, not absence.** A ranked player who plays zero matches in a competitive mode loses 50 MMR per consecutive week of inactivity, up to a 200-MMR cap total per dormancy stretch. Returning + playing one match halts decay; decay never demotes below the floor of the player's *peak* tier for the season.
- **Smurf detection flags obvious cases, no false-positive bans.** A new account that wins 12 of its first 15 placement matches with a >70% kill-participation rate gets flagged as a suspected smurf; the matcher accelerates its MMR climb so it reaches a fair tier within 5 additional matches instead of grinding for weeks. The flag is non-punitive — no ban, no shadow-flag visible to opponents.
- **Anti-throw protects against tanking.** A player on a 5+ loss streak in a single ranked mode gets a "protection window": further losses lose at most 5 MMR each (versus the normal 12-25), and the matcher widens the search to surface easier opponents. Wins still deliver normal MMR gains.
- **Tournament formats shippable from a UI.** A server admin or party leader opens the tournament panel, picks one of four formats (Single-Elim 16, Double-Elim 16, Round-Robin 8, Swiss 16), invites players via the M40B party UI, and the bracket / schedule auto-generates. Brackets persist across server restart and survive an admin reschedule.
- **Per-mode top-100 leaderboards are visible in-game.** Each PvP / endgame mode has a top-100 list with name, rank tier, MMR, win-loss, and faction allegiance. The board updates within 60 seconds of a ranked match resolving.
- **Per-faction leaderboards rank within an allegiance.** Players who pledge to a faction get a per-faction top-100 in addition to the global list. A Diamond Faction-A player who is rank-43 globally might be rank-7 within Faction A.
- **Ranking-only cosmetics that cannot be bought.** Reaching Diamond unlocks a `diamond_rank_banner` + `diamond_portrait_frame`; finishing a season as Top-100 unlocks a `seasonal_top100_icon` permanently tagged with the season number. None of these items appear in any storefront, in any battle pass, or in any gacha pool — per DR-031, they are play-earned, non-purchasable, and confer zero gameplay power.

## Notes for the implementer

- MMR/Elo K-factor MUST be tier-graded, not flat: K=32 below Platinum, K=24 Platinum + Diamond, K=16 Master. This makes top-of-ladder movement reflect skill rather than match-count.
- The 6 tier thresholds are entry-MMR + 100 per division. Default table (unless `rank_tiers.ron` overrides): Bronze III 600 / Bronze II 700 / Bronze I 800 / Silver III 900 / Silver II 1000 / Silver I 1100 / Gold III 1200 / Gold II 1300 / Gold I 1400 / Platinum III 1500 / Platinum II 1600 / Platinum I 1700 / Diamond III 1800 / Diamond II 1900 / Diamond I 2000 / Master III 2100 / Master II 2200 / Master I 2300+. Demotion happens at threshold-50 to prevent yo-yoing on the boundary.
- Soft-reset MUST be `new_mmr = old_mmr + 0.4 * (1200 - old_mmr)`. Do NOT use a multiplicative shrink toward zero (that destroys top-ladder identity) and do NOT preserve full MMR (that prevents lower players from competing fairly).
- Decay MUST be wall-clock weekly, computed off the last_ranked_match_at timestamp, NOT off real-time playtime. A player who plays one ranked match in a 6-week window halts decay for that week regardless of how short the session was.
- Decay floor MUST be the bottom of peak-tier-of-current-season, computed at the start of decay, NOT the player's lifetime peak. A Master-season-7 player who drops to Diamond in season 8 has a season-8 floor that lets them decay below 2100; their season-7 Master record stays intact in the profile.
- Smurf detection MUST be non-punitive. Do NOT add chat-flag, do NOT add opponent-visible smurf-marker, do NOT ban. The only effect is an MMR-gain multiplier (default 1.8x for 5 matches) so the smurf reaches their fair tier fast and ranked play stays fair for everyone else.
- Anti-throw deactivates on the FIRST win, not after N wins. A player can intentionally throw, get protection, then immediately stop throwing without grinding back through the protection cooldown. The protection is a safety net, not a punishment.
- Tournament bracket determinism MUST be seeded off `(seed_u64, sorted_player_id_list)`. Same input → same bracket across OS. Use the M41B-style BLAKE3 hash for the determinism CI gate.
- Tournament bracket persistence MUST survive a server restart. Store the bracket state in `cf-save` per the M36C save schema; reload at startup. Admin reschedule events apply to the in-memory bracket and immediately flush to disk.
- Round-robin scheduling MUST use the circle method (rotate all-but-one players around a fixed first player). Otherwise back-to-back same-map scheduling produces unfair fatigue.
- Swiss-system pairing MUST avoid rematches across all 5 rounds. If a player pool of 16 is exhausted and a rematch is unavoidable, the pairing falls through to the next-score-bucket; document this fallback in the test fixture.
- Per-mode top-100 leaderboard refresh MUST be a 60-second batched flush, NOT a per-match query. Use the M36B telemetry pipeline pattern: collect deltas in memory, flush to the leaderboard store every 60s, surface via a single read query at the UI.
- Per-faction leaderboards share the per-mode store; the per-faction view is a filtered query on the same backing table. Do NOT duplicate the storage.
- Every ranking decoration MUST be marked `purchasable: false` AND `confers_power: false` in its `.ron`. The CI script `m41c_dr031_invariant.sh` greps the subtree for either token set to `true` and fails CI. There is no "soft" enforcement here — DR-031 is closed-direction.
- Ranking decorations MUST NOT appear in any `content/cosmetic_shop/`, `content/battle_pass/`, `content/gacha_pool/`, or `content/dlc/` subtree. The DR-031 invariant test asserts the negative.
- All new event schemas MUST be added to `dump_schemas --check` so schema drift fails CI.
- All new events have `cosmetic: false`. Ranked + tournament + leaderboard events are sim-relevant + non-droppable.
- Audit log MUST record player-id-hash (BLAKE3 of `player_id || daily_pepper`) for season-rollover + smurf-detection + anti-throw events, never raw player IDs. The pepper rotates daily, mirroring the M36B + M41B privacy posture.
- Cross-region MMR balancing (M41B) MUST consume the per-mode MMR from this milestone, not a session-level placeholder. The M41B `cross_region.rs` MODIFY entry is exactly this swap.
- Do NOT add `cf-server-ops::matchmaking::leaderboard_rewards` here — real-money rewards are forbidden by DR-031 and seasonal cosmetic unlocks are handled by the rank-decoration content, not a rewards crate.
