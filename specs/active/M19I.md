# M19I — Critter Ranching + Atmospheric Closed-Loop Cycle + Plant Ecosystem

## Status

`active`

## Intent

ONI-grade critter ranching + plant ecosystem: critters (Hatch / Slickster / Puft / Pip / Pacu / Drecko / Shine Bug / etc.) consume + produce gases as a CLOSED LOOP that the player exploits for life-support automation. Plant farms (Algae Terrarium / Mushroom Plant / Hydroponics) part of the same cycle. Together: a fully sustainable base where atmosphere balances via biology.

## Player-facing behavior

- **Hatch**: lives in dirt; eats coal/algae/sand; outputs coal-or-other based on diet morph (eat sulfur → outputs sulfur). Lays eggs; mass conservation.
- **Slickster**: lives in oil tile; eats CO2; outputs crude oil. The CO2 → oil conversion is the most coveted ONI loop.
- **Puft**: lives in polluted oxygen; eats polluted oxygen + outputs slime. Slime is mushroom plant food → atmosphere cleanup loop.
- **Pip**: lives on plants; eats wood; reproduces fast; can be deployed in forests.
- **Pacu**: lives in water; produces fish that can be harvested (food); breeds in clean water.
- **Drecko**: lives in hydrogen; outputs phosphorite (fertilizer); also gives shed material (textile / reed fiber).
- **Shine Bug**: lives anywhere; emits constant 1500 lux light + 100 W heat. Mobile light source.
- **Algae Terrarium**: per-tile algae structure; converts CO2 + water → O2 + clean water (rate per ONI).
- **Mushroom Plant**: per-tile structure; converts slime + dirt → mushroom (food).
- **Hydroponics**: per-tile irrigated soil; grows wheat/lettuce/etc. for food.
- **Ranching loop**: build pasture rooms → assign critters → critters auto-consume + produce → assign player worker to muck out + collect → output routes to base storage.
- **Per-critter happiness**: groomed critters reproduce faster + output more; ungroomed slowly die.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Player builds first pasture + assigns a hatch
  Given a pasture room (sealed; 4 hatch egg compatible) + a fresh hatch egg
  When the player places egg in pasture
  Then critter_egg_laid fires (placement event)
  And critter spawns when egg incubates (per ONI 6-tick incubation)
  And critter starts auto-eating coal from feed dispenser
  And critter_consumed_gas + critter_produced_output fire per tick

Scenario: Slickster converts CO2 to oil
  Given a pasture filled with Slicksters + CO2 supply (from base atmosphere)
  When 1 in-game day elapses
  Then per-Slickster: 60 kg CO2 consumed; 20 kg crude oil produced
  And the player harvests crude oil for fuel chain (M19B)
  And base CO2 levels drop visibly

Scenario: Puft cleans polluted atmosphere
  Given a sealed Puft pasture + connected to base scrubber
  When the base atmospheric scrubber is offline
  Then Pufts ingest polluted O2 + produce slime
  And atmosphere quality improves (M19E analyzer reads it)
  And slime accumulates for mushroom cultivation

Scenario: Algae terrarium produces O2 from CO2
  Given a algae terrarium + sufficient CO2 + water + 0 lux (no light required)
  When 60s elapse
  Then plant_harvested fires (algae)
  And M19 room atmosphere CO2 decreases + O2 increases (sustainable life-support)
  And the closed-loop benefit is visible to player

Scenario: Grooming station improves critter happiness
  Given a critter at 50% happiness
  When the player presses [E] at grooming station + holds 10s
  Then critter_groomed fires
  And critter happiness rises to 100%
  And critter morphing accelerates (Hatch → Sage Hatch happens faster)

Scenario: Critter morphs based on diet
  Given a basic Hatch eating algae for 5 cycles
  When critter morphing tick runs
  Then critter_morphed fires with from="Hatch", to="Sage_Hatch"
  And output changes accordingly (Sage Hatch produces algae waste)
  And player exploits morphs for resource targeting

Scenario: Plant dies without light + reseeds
  Given a hydroponics_wheat with light_level=0 (no lamp)
  When 60s elapse
  Then plant_died_no_light fires
  And the plant becomes a stump (can be replanted; seeds drop)
  And player must add lamp to restore the farm

Scenario: Plant dies without water + must rehydrate
  Given a hydroponics_lettuce + empty water tank
  When 60s elapse
  Then plant_died_no_water fires
  And player must refill water tank to restart
  And the closed-loop fragility is visible

Scenario: Per-critter happiness affects output
  Given a happy Slickster + an unhappy Slickster
  When 1 day elapses
  Then happy Slickster outputs 20 kg crude oil
  And unhappy Slickster outputs 5 kg (4× penalty)
  And player learns "groom or starve" axiom

Scenario: Per-pasture critter count limit
  Given a pasture rated for 6 critters
  When the player tries to assign 7
  Then assignment rejects with "PASTURE OVERCROWDED"
  And the player must expand pasture OR re-house critters

Scenario: Determinism across critter ecosystems
  Given two engines with same seed + identical pasture setup
  When 600 ticks (10 cycles) elapse
  Then identical critter morphing + output sequence
  And identical SaveBlob.checksum at tick 600
```

- Cross-breeding critters (Hatch × Slickster offspring) — M48+ creativity.
- Multiplayer co-pasturing across factions — M48+ MMO.

## Notes for the implementer

- Critter behavior is simpler than actor behavior: no full AI mind layer; reactive only. ~80 µs per critter per tick.
- Per-critter gas consumption is bounded by available atmosphere; if surrounded by zero target gas, critter slowly starves.
- Plant growth tick runs at 1/120 cadence (every 2 sec at 60 Hz); checksum-stable.
- Morphing is event-driven (X cycles of diet Y → morph); seeded RNG used for morph chance.
- ONI critter content is data-driven RON; modders add custom critters via schema.
