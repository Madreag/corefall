# M31B — Weather States Full Catalog + Per-World Variants + Storm Survival Loop

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Expand M31's prose-only "7 weather states + 12 worlds" promise into a deep, content-complete weather catalog with **per-world overrides**, **per-state effect contracts**, and a **storm survival gameplay loop** (RimWorld toxic-fallout / STALKER emission parity). M31 ships the kernel + state machine; M31B ships the body: every world's weather table, every state's effect on visibility / movement / equipment durability / temperature / atmosphere, plus the storyteller-integrated storm beat that turns weather from cosmetic into a mission objective ("survive the next 90 seconds in shelter or die").

## Canonical ownership

- `cf-weather` (kernel state machine + transition logic) — owned by M31.
- `cf-weather::catalog` (per-world weather tables + state effect rows) — **owned by M31B** (NEW).
- `cf-weather::storm_loop` (shelter-or-die gameplay beat + storyteller hook) — **owned by M31B** (NEW).
- Per-state actor effects (visibility / traction / durability / temperature) — read by `cf-actor`, `cf-perception`, `cf-equipment`, `cf-atmos`; produced by `cf-weather`.

## Player-facing behavior

- **17 weather states** across the catalog (M31's 7 base + 10 per-world specializations): `clear`, `clear_cold`, `rain`, `acid_rain`, `storm`, `electrical_storm`, `dust`, `global_dust_storm`, `fog`, `methane_fog`, `snow`, `blizzard`, `heat_shimmer`, `hot_wind`, `lava_spray`, `cinder_fall`, `ion_aurora`.
- **Per-world weather table** — each of the 12 launch worlds has a curated subset (3-6 states) plus authored transition probabilities; players learn each world's weather mood (Earth wet & variable, Mars dusty & dry, Vulcan hellish & static, Mimas cold & methanous, Venus acidic & lethal).
- **Per-state effect contract** — every state authors 5 numeric effects: `visibility_mul`, `traction_mul`, `equipment_wear_mul`, `temperature_offset_c`, `atmosphere_overlay` — so designers tune content data, not code.
- **Storm survival loop** — when a deadly state (`electrical_storm`, `global_dust_storm`, `blizzard`, `acid_rain`, `lava_spray`, `cinder_fall`) is forecast, a 30-90s `weather.storm_warning` event fires; HUD shows countdown + bearing; player runs to shelter (a sealed roofed tile or vehicle interior) before the wave hits; failure to shelter = sustained damage tick (RimWorld toxic-fallout parity, but in-mission).
- **Forecast affordance** — barometer / weather radar / sat-uplink items let the player extend warning window (T1 barometer = 30s; T2 radar = 60s; T3 sat-uplink = 90s + bearing arrow); no item = surprise (5s warning only).
- **Per-state visual + audio cue** — every state has a unique HUD caption, screen filter (color grade + particle overlay), and ambient audio bed; players read incoming weather visually 10-15s before mechanical effects start.
- **Equipment durability gating** — `acid_rain` corrodes exposed metal at 0.5%/min wear; `lava_spray` ignites cloth armor (M14 fire) and burns durability at 2%/min; `dust` clogs unsealed weapons (jam rate × 3); `electrical_storm` discharges through metal armor (random shock); players choose loadouts per forecast.
- **AI integration (M24 carry)** — bots read the same effect rows + storm warnings; AI seeks shelter when caught in deadly state OR equips weather-rated gear when issued; reason labels `weather_storm_seek_shelter`, `weather_acid_rain_no_metal_armor`.
- **Replay-visible** — every state transition, warning, shelter event, and damage tick emits a typed `weather.*` event; replays of "the dust storm raid" reproduce bit-exact.
- **Storyteller hook (M25 carry)** — `cf-storyteller` registers `WeatherStormEvent` per deadly state; Cassandra Classic schedules one every 8-12 mission minutes; Randy Random can roll a global dust storm during a boss fight.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Per-world weather table loads from content
  Given content/weather/worlds/mars.ron with states [dust, global_dust_storm, clear, clear_cold, electrical_storm]
  When the Mars scenario boots
  Then cf-weather::catalog reports 5 allowed states for world=Mars
  And weather sampler never picks `rain` or `snow` on Mars
  And replay event weather_state_changed only cycles among the authored 5

Scenario: Per-state effect contract is data-driven
  Given content/weather/states/dust.ron with visibility_mul=0.40, traction_mul=0.85, equipment_wear_mul=3.0, temperature_offset_c=-2.0
  When weather transitions to `dust`
  Then cf-perception multiplies effective sight range by 0.40
  And cf-actor multiplies traction by 0.85
  And cf-equipment::durability multiplies wear on exposed (held) weapons by 3.0
  And cf-atmos shifts outside ambient by -2.0°C
  And HUD weather widget shows caption "Dust rolling in" and dust particle filter

Scenario: Storm warning gives the player a forecast window
  Given player carrying weather_radar_t2 (window=60s)
  And the next deadly state is `electrical_storm` scheduled in 50 ticks
  When the storm scheduler picks the event
  Then weather_storm_warning fires with countdown=60s
  And HUD shows "ELECTRICAL STORM INCOMING — 60s — bearing 045°"
  When 60 ticks pass:
    Then the deadly state activates
    And the warning HUD switches to a damage-rate readout

Scenario: No forecast item gives only the surprise window
  Given player with no barometer / radar / sat-uplink
  When a deadly state is scheduled
  Then weather_storm_warning fires with countdown=5s only
  And HUD shows "STORM IMMINENT" with no bearing

Scenario: Shelter check during deadly state
  Given `electrical_storm` is active
  And player is on a roofless tile (no shelter)
  When 30 ticks pass:
    Then weather_storm_damage_tick fires every 6 ticks
    And player accumulates electric affliction (M16) at 1.0 HP / tick
  When player runs under a roofed sealed tile:
    Then weather_shelter_entered fires
    And damage ticks stop
    And HUD switches to "SHELTERED" state

Scenario: Acid rain corrodes exposed metal armor
  Given weather=`acid_rain` (Venus or Vulcan)
  And player wearing steel chestplate (durability=1.0)
  When 600 ticks (60s) elapse:
    Then weather_equipment_wear_tick fires every 60 ticks
    And steel chestplate durability decreases by 0.005 / tick (0.5%/min)
    And HUD shows armor corrosion indicator

Scenario: Dust storm jams unsealed weapons
  Given weather=`dust` + player wielding unsealed_rifle (jam_base=1%)
  When player fires under dust
  Then effective jam_rate = 1% × 3 = 3%
  And HUD shows "WEAPON DIRTY" warning after 20 shots
  And replay event weather_equipment_wear_tick records `cause=dust_clog`

Scenario: Lava spray ignites cloth armor on Vulcan
  Given weather=`lava_spray` + player wearing cloth_robe (flammable=true)
  When the lava_spray particle hits within 2 tiles of the player
  Then cf-fire (M14) ignites the cloth_robe
  And durability drops 2%/min while burning
  And player must equip a sealed Vulcan suit or burn

Scenario: AI seeks shelter on deadly forecast (M24 carry)
  Given M24 environmental competence + AI bot exposed
  When weather_storm_warning fires with countdown=30s and bearing=045°
  Then cf-ai::doctrine emits ai.environmental_decision with reason_label=`weather_storm_seek_shelter`
  And bot routes to nearest roofed sealed tile within reach
  And bot reaches shelter before damage tick begins (or dies trying)

Scenario: Storyteller schedules a storm during a mission
  Given storyteller=Cassandra Classic + 8 minutes into the mission
  When the storyteller picks an incident
  Then a registered WeatherStormEvent (for the current world) is eligible
  And director.incident_chosen fires with event_id=weather_storm_event
  And the storm beat sequences warning → state → shelter loop → resolution

Scenario: Global dust storm on Mars blocks long-range fire
  Given Mars + weather=`global_dust_storm` (visibility_mul=0.20)
  When sniper bot tries to acquire a target at 800u
  Then cf-perception caps effective sight to 800u × 0.20 = 160u
  And the sniper can no longer acquire the target
  And ai.target_lost fires with reason=`weather_visibility_loss`

Scenario: Replay determinism for a weather event
  Given a recorded mission containing weather_state_changed + weather_storm_warning + weather_shelter_entered
  When the replay is played back twice (same seed, same content version)
  Then every weather.* event fires at the same tick with the same payload
  And final HP / equipment durability deltas match bit-exact
```

## Out of scope

- Cosmetic-only weather variants (overcast / partly-cloudy) — flavor pass; deferred.
- Per-biome micro-climate (a forest patch on Earth being wetter than the open plain) — M31C territory.
- Hurricane / tornado mass-displacement weather — defer to M31C if requested.
- Weather affecting plant growth (RimWorld farm parity) — owned by M30 / M32 farming milestones.

## Dependencies

- M31 (kernel + state machine + day/night) — must close before M31B's catalog rows have a host.
- M16 / M14 / M14A (afflictions + fire + materials) — damage-tick + durability paths consume these.
- M20 + M24 (environmental aggregator + AI competence) — shelter-seek doctrine carry.
- M25 (storyteller registry) — `WeatherStormEvent` registration site.

## Notes for implementer

- Audit M31 first: if `cf-weather` already has a kernel state-machine + per-tick transition picker, do NOT rewrite it. M31B is **catalog rows + storm-loop FSM + forecast window** layered on top.
- The 17 state RON files are the bulk of the work; each is ~30 lines (id + 5 effect numbers + audio cue ids + particle id + caption string + transition probabilities). Author them as data first; let designers tune values without recompile.
- `WeatherStateRow.atmosphere_overlay` (Option<AtmosphereOverlay>) is the glue to M19's atmospheric layer — acid rain authors `corrosive=true`, methane fog authors `combustible=true`. Do NOT duplicate atmosphere logic in `cf-weather`; feed the overlay into cf-atmos and let M19 handle the rest.
- Storm-loop FSM is `Idle → WarningWindow(remaining_ticks) → ActiveDeadly(damage_tick_period) → Resolution`. Cancel a warning if the picker re-rolls during the window (rare; allow for storyteller branching).
- Shelter check is a single query: "is the actor on a tile with `roof.is_sealed=true` AND `walls.bound=true` for at least one adjacent face"; reuse M28's room-sealing classifier — do NOT reinvent.
- Forecast window scales with the **highest-tier** equipped item; do not stack barometer + radar.
- Per AGENTS.md: no `println!`, no `unwrap()` on user inputs, no `thread_rng()` in `cf-weather` (it's a sim crate) — use the scenario's seeded RNG handed in from `cf-sim-core`.
- M24 doctrine integration: emit the shelter-seek reason label only when forecast confidence is non-zero; bots without forecast (no radar) should still flee deadly state but with reason `weather_deadly_state_active` not `_seek_shelter`.
- Replay schemas must follow existing `cf-replay/schemas/event/*.json` conventions; reuse `Tick`, `ActorId`, and `WorldPos` shared types.
