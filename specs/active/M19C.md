# M19C — Suit Life-Support Deep + 5-Tier Tank Ladder + Filter Ladder

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Ship the full Stationeers-grade suit life-support deep simulation — 5-tier oxygen tank ladder + filter ladder + waste-tank management + helmet flush + per-suit pressure/temperature management — turning the M19 baseline EVA suit into the full Stationeers Hardsuit gameplay loop where breathing IS the gameplay.

## Player-facing behavior

- **5-tier oxygen tank ladder**: small backup canister (0.5 L) → standard (5 L) → extended (20 L) → tactical (50 L) → heavy mining (200 L). Tank size affects mass + bulk (M14A mass aggregation); player must decide weight vs duration.
- **Filter ladder**: T1 dust filter (clean Mars dust) → T2 chemical filter (toxic atmosphere) → T3 carbon scrubber (CO2) → T4 universal multi-stage (all-in-one premium). Filters wear out; replace at base.
- **Waste tank**: CO2 + Pollutant from breathing accumulates in helmet's waste tank → must dump at base scrubber → fill cycle ~30-60 min for a standard tank.
- **Helmet flush**: emergency one-shot vent (dump suit atmosphere to surrounding room) — clears a toxic-gas leak in 2s but exposes player to ambient until resealed.
- **Suit pressure tuning**: player can adjust suit pressure (11-300 kPa survivable; 50-100 kPa comfortable). Higher pressure → faster movement (Stationeers "low-pressure waddle"); too high → dangerous on planet surfaces.
- **Suit temperature**: heated/cooled suit modes; ambient temperature inside helmet vs outside (helmet condensation visual at fog point).
- **Per-origin variation**: humans need 16 kPa O2 minimum; androids tolerant to 12; robots immune; Methane Breather oxygen-toxic (breathes natural gas, dies in atmosphere); Crystalline (radiation-immune).
- **HUD readability**: per-tank gauge + filter wear + waste tank fill level + breathing rate + per-suit pressure dial + temperature delta.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Player swaps from T1 emergency to T3 extended tank
  Given a human soldier with T1 (0.5L) tank at 25% remaining + walking in vacuum
  When player swaps to a fresh T3 tank
  Then HUD time-to-empty jumps from "4 min" to "120 min"
  And mass aggregator updates: +5.5 kg added to inventory (T3 8.0 kg vs T1 2.5 kg)
  And M14A walk speed slightly reduces due to added mass

Scenario: Filter wears out + must be replaced
  Given a T2 chemical filter active for 30 minutes in Pollutant atmosphere
  When 30 min elapse
  Then filter_worn_out event fires
  And HUD shows "FILTER WORN — REPLACE AT BASE"
  And player's O2 starts accepting filter-rated contaminants (poisoned breathing risk)
  And the player can hot-swap (5s animation) if carrying spare

Scenario: Waste tank fills up + must dump
  Given continuous breathing for 60 min (waste tank accumulates CO2 + Pollutant)
  When the waste tank reaches 100%
  Then waste_tank_full event fires
  And HUD shows "WASTE TANK FULL — RETURN TO BASE SCRUBBER"
  And further breathing back-pressures into helmet (slower CO2 expulsion → hypercapnia)
  And player must reach base scrubber within ~5 min to avoid CO2 toxicity

Scenario: Helmet flush extinguishes toxic-gas leak
  Given a Pollutant gas leak inside the suit (filter failure)
  When player triggers act.player.helmet_flush
  Then helmet_flushed event fires
  And suit atmosphere dumps to surrounding room (2s vent animation)
  And during the vent, player is exposed to ambient (vacuum / hostile gas)
  And the player must reseal + refill quickly before suffocation

Scenario: Suit pressure adjustment affects movement
  Given a suit at 50 kPa (comfortable) → player adjusts to 100 kPa (high-pressure)
  When the adjustment commits
  Then suit_pressure_adjusted fires
  And player's M14A walk speed increases by ~10% (less low-pressure waddle)
  And suit oxygen consumption increases proportionally
  And HUD shows "SUIT 100 kPa — high-pressure mode"

Scenario: Per-origin breathing variance
  Given a Methane Breather actor in Earth atmosphere (mostly N2 + O2)
  When the actor breathes
  Then suit_o2_low fires (Methane Breather is O2-toxic)
  And HUD warns "OXYGEN TOXIC FOR THIS ORIGIN — SWITCH TO METHANE TANK"
  And the player's strategic choice (per-race promised land) becomes obvious

Scenario: Helmet seal breach + faster O2 leak
  Given a helmet with 90% seal_integrity (from M14 hit damage)
  When the actor breathes in vacuum
  Then O2 consumption is 3× normal (per M19 seal breach math)
  And HUD shows "HELMET BREACH — RAPID OXYGEN LOSS"
  And player must reseal via vacuum_patch (M28B breach_repair_tool variant or repair kit)

Scenario: Determinism across 24-hour endurance test
  Given two engines running m19c_vacuum_extended_endurance with identical seed
  When 24 in-game hours elapse with continuous suit life-support
  Then identical O2 + filter + waste tank state per tick
  And identical SaveBlob.checksum at tick 5,184,000
```

## Notes for the implementer

- Stationeers reference values used: `inhaled_mol_per_tick = 0.0048 · BreathingRate · BreathingEfficiency`; 16 kPa O2 minimum; 11-300 kPa suit survivable; -10 to 49°C suit survivable.
- Per-origin breathing matrix: human (16 kPa O2 min), android (12 kPa min), robot (none), Methane Breather (methane required; O2 toxic), Crystalline (radiation-immune; O2 ambient OK).
- Filter cells (activated_carbon) are M30 ore products + M32 fabricator recipe.
- Suit pressure dial uses 5 detent positions: 11 / 30 / 60 / 100 / 200 kPa for game-feel quantization.
- Helmet flush has a 30s cooldown to prevent abuse.
