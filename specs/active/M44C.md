# M44C — Vehicle Physics + Tire Grip × Surface + Suspension + Per-Component Damage + Engine Subsystems

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Per-tire-type × per-surface-material grip lookup + suspension dynamics + rollover physics + per-component vehicle damage states (engine / radiator / fuel tank / battery / tire × 4 / window / door) + engine fire/oil leak/fuel rupture cascade — the no-compromise vehicle physics layer that turns M44A's "Electric ATV" from a sprite into a real dynamic vehicle.

## Canonical ownership

Owns vehicle physics: tire grip, suspension, rollover, per-component damage, engine subsystems. M44A vehicles consume; M44B convoy AI consumes; M14 collision/impulse produces damage events.

### Suspension dynamics

- Per-vehicle `suspension_stiffness_n_per_m` + `suspension_damping_n_s_per_m`.
- Per-wheel independent: front_left, front_right, rear_left, rear_right.
- Per-wheel HP — wheel damage from impacts; flat tire = -50% grip; blown tire = 0% grip.
- Vehicle pitches/rolls based on per-wheel terrain height differential (per M14A walk_angle slope sampler reuse).

### Rollover physics

- Per-vehicle `center_of_mass_height` + `wheelbase_width`.
- Rollover threshold: lateral_g × cos(roll_angle) exceeds stability margin.
- Visible warning at 0.7 of threshold (HUD tilt indicator).
- Roll-over event: vehicle ragdolls + occupants take collision damage per M14.

### Per-component damage states

Per-component HP separate from vehicle hull HP:

| Component | HP | Damage state visible | Failure effect |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Engine | 400 | smoke + sparks at 50%; fire at 25% | -50% top speed; eventual stall |
| Radiator | 200 | green coolant leak at 50%; engine overheat at 0% | engine HP drains per tick |
| Fuel tank | 300 | dripping at 50%; gas pool at 0% | fuel loss; ignition risk |
| Battery | 250 | bulging at 60% (M29 thermal runaway); explosive failure at 0% | electric system offline; possible explosion |
| Tire × 4 | 100 each | visible deflation at 50%; rim only at 0% | per-tire grip reduces |
| Window × N | 80 each | crack decal at 50%; shatter at 0% | environmental exposure; loss of sealing |
| Door × N | 200 each | dent at 50%; jam at 0% | cannot open; loss of egress |
| Headlight × 2 | 50 each | flicker at 50%; dark at 0% | reduced night visibility |

### Engine subsystems

- **Engine types:** electric_motor / ICE_petrol / ICE_diesel / jet_engine / nuclear / hand-crank.
- **Per-engine torque curve** (kg·m at RPM).
- **Cooling requirement:** ICE + nuclear engines require radiator + coolant; overheat = damage.
- **Lubrication:** ICE engines need oil (M19B fuel chain); without oil → engine seizes.
- **Air intake:** ICE engines need atmospheric oxygen; in vacuum → stall (per M19 atmosphere).
- **Exhaust:** ICE produces CO + CO2 (M19 atmospheric gases); plays into combustion atmosphere ignition (PARITY-92 from M14A).

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Tire grip varies per surface
  Given an ATV with tire_street + concrete surface
  Then top speed = 12 m/s
  Given same ATV moves onto ice surface
  Then grip drops to 0.1×; top speed drops to ~2 m/s + heavy drift

Scenario: Off-road tire excels in mud
  Given ATV swaps to tire_off_road + traverses mud
  Then grip = 0.8× (vs street tire 0.3×)
  And player reaches destination 2× faster

Scenario: Tire puncture from sharp terrain
  Given ATV drives over sharp rocks
  When per-tile sharpness threshold exceeded:
    Then tire_punctured fires
    And tire_hp drops to 50/100
    And grip × 0.5

Scenario: Engine fire cascade
  Given engine HP = 25%
  When the per-tick check runs:
    Then engine_fire fires
    And fuel_tank ignition_risk activates
    When sustained 30s without extinguish:
      Then fuel_tank explodes (per M14 explosion)

Scenario: Radiator leak triggers engine overheat
  Given radiator HP = 0
  When 60 ticks elapse with engine running:
    Then engine HP drains at 0.5/tick
    And engine smoking fires at 50% HP
    And engine fire fires at 25% HP

Scenario: Vehicle rollover from sharp turn
  Given ATV at top speed turning sharp
  When lateral_g exceeds stability margin:
    Then rollover.warning fires (visible HUD tilt indicator)
  When lateral_g exceeds full threshold:
    Then vehicle_rollover fires
    And vehicle ragdolls per M14 ragdoll
    And occupants take crash damage

Scenario: Vacuum stalls ICE engine
  Given ICE engine vehicle in vacuum (M19 atmospheric pressure < 5 kPa)
  When engine attempts to run:
    Then engine_stalled fires with reason="no_atmospheric_oxygen"
    And vehicle becomes immobile until atmospheric environment restored

Scenario: Battery thermal runaway
  Given battery HP = 50% + ambient temperature > 60°C
  When the per-tick thermal check runs:
    Then battery_thermal_runaway fires
    And M29 cascade event triggers
    And nearby actors take burn risk

Scenario: Determinism — same seed reproduces vehicle trajectory
  Given identical seed + identical terrain + identical inputs
  When 600 ticks elapse with mixed surface driving
  Then identical vehicle positions per tick
  And identical tire/component damage events
```
