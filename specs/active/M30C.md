# M30C — Cave-In Physics + Reinforcement Gameplay

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Surface the Cortex-Command-defining "hand-dug tunnels collapse if unsupported" gameplay loop on top of M14E's structural integrity engine — explicit player-facing mechanics for placing support_beam, recognizing structural warning signs, managing cave-in risk while deep-digging, and the AI miner doctrine that respects tunnel safety.

## Player-facing behavior

- **Player verbs**:
  - Carry a stack of support beams in inventory (~5 kg per beam; light wood + 2 iron each).
  - Place a beam at a tunnel center span; visible 3D-look beam sprite straddles tunnel ceiling-to-floor.
  - Place reinforced beam (T2; 4 iron + 1 steel) for deeper tunnels; supports 32-px unsupported span instead of 16.
  - Repair a damaged beam (post-impact / post-explosion) before it fails.
  - Demolish a beam intentionally (e.g., trap a pursuing enemy under collapse).
- **Warning chain**:
  - L1 crack visual on ceiling (cosmetic).
  - L2: HUD banner "STRUCTURAL WARNING" + audio creak loop.
  - L3: "CEILING UNSTABLE — EVACUATE" sticky warning + camera shake.
  - Collapse: terrain falls + actor damage routes through M14 fall_impulse_chain.
- **Cascade catastrophe**: pulling out a load-bearing beam mid-base triggers a sequential cave-in across multiple chunks — visible wave-front; AI miners flee toward the surface; squad members trapped under = dynamic rescue scenario.
- **AI miner doctrine** (AI-MINE-A-09): AI miners auto-place a beam when unsupported span exceeds 12 px; never deep-dig without reinforcement; player can override but AI complains via reason_label.
- **Pre-placed beams in procgen**: ancient ruined bunkers (M25 / M43) ship with pre-placed beams that may be damaged or rotted — adds exploration flavor.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Player places a T1 support beam under unsupported span
  Given a 24-px unsupported tunnel + player carrying 3 T1 support beams
  When the player presses [E] at the tunnel center
  Then support_beam_placed event fires
  And M14E structural_integrity_field locks ±8 px around beam to integrity 500
  And L1 crack decal removes from ceiling
  And HUD warning clears

Scenario: AI miner auto-places beam when span exceeds 12 px
  Given an AI miner deep-digging a fresh tunnel + carrying spare beams
  When the unsupported span reaches 13 px
  Then AI-MINE-A-09 doctrine triggers
  And miner places a beam at the unsupported center
  And cf-ai emits reason_label="placing_beam_for_safety"
  And ai.auto_action_taken event fires

Scenario: Demolishing a beam mid-base triggers cascade
  Given a 48-px-wide bunker tunnel supported by 2 T2 beams
  When the player demolishes one beam
  Then support_beam_demolished fires
  And M14E re-computes integrity field
  And within 60 ticks, structural_warning_l1 → l2 → l3 cascade
  And cave-in cascades to neighbor chunks
  And the player has ~6 seconds to evacuate before collapse

Scenario: Cave-in rescue scenario (squad-rescue gameplay)
  Given m30c_cave_in_rescue scenario: 2 squad members trapped under a collapsed tunnel
  When the player approaches with iron pickaxe + 2 spare T1 beams
  Then the player must dig carefully (avoid triggering further cave-in)
  And place beams as they dig to stabilize the rescue path
  And reach the trapped allies + revive (per M14 ragdoll wake-up)
  And mission completes when both squad members reach the surface

Scenario: Beam takes impact damage from combat
  Given a T1 support beam (integrity 200)
  When an explosion (HE grenade, 150 dmg) hits within 2 m
  Then beam integrity drops to 50/200
  And structural_warning_l1 fires (ceiling cracking nearby)
  When the player repairs with the welder (welds 50 integrity per second)
  Then beam returns to 100/200 (partial repair)

Scenario: Reinforced (T2) beam holds longer spans
  Given a 32-px tunnel section with a T2 beam at center
  When 1000 ticks elapse (long-time test)
  Then no cave-in triggers
  And M14E integrity field reads beam strength 800 vs T1 500
  And ai.miner doctrine notes "T2 beam — extended deep-dig possible"

Scenario: Pre-placed rotten beams in procgen ruins
  Given an M43 procgen ruined bunker
  When the player explores
  Then they encounter pre-existing T1 beams with 20% integrity (rotted)
  And the beam visual shows the rotted decal
  And the player decides: repair (use weld + 1 iron) OR replace (use new beam)
  And the gameplay rewards both choices

Scenario: Determinism across cave-in cascade
  Given two engines running m30c_cave_in_rescue with same seed
  When the cascade triggers + propagates
  Then identical event sequence (cave_in_triggered + per-chunk warnings)
  And identical SaveBlob.checksum at tick 600
```

## Out of scope

- Earthquake-triggered base-wide collapses — M16 hazard event + M31 weather kernel.
- Earthquake-resistant building codes — M48+ realism.
- Underwater tunnel cave-ins (water tile + flooding) — M16 + M14 already cover; M30C is dry-ground only.
- Per-pixel rebar/mesh that increases unsupported-span budget mid-pixel — M48+.

- M22 AI pathfinding (active): AI miner avoids unsafe paths

- M9 reactor defense (done): demolish-load-bearing-beam-as-trap scenario callback

## Notes for the implementer

- Player-facing crack decals are the **most important readability layer**: the player must SEE the warning before the collapse. Three escalating visuals (L1 hairline / L2 visible cracks / L3 rubble breaking off) + audio creak progression.
- Beam recipe lives in M32 crafting; placement tool lives in M30C; demolisher is the same M28A demolish mode pointed at the beam item.
- AI-MINE-A-09 doctrine: `if unsupported_span > 12 && has_beam_in_inventory: place_beam`. If no beam, retreat to surface.
- The cave-in cascade visual reuses M18 terrain.terrain_cascade event (already exists) — same animation pattern.
- Repair-beam UI is hold-to-confirm; ~3 seconds per 50 integrity restored at T1 welder.
