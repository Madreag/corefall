# M44E — Mech Walking + Tracked Vehicles + Bipedal Combat Mechs

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Ship **CC-grade walking-mech sim** + **tracked-vehicle physics distinct from M44C wheeled** + **4 chassis-grade mech archetypes** (Light Recon / Medium Combat / Heavy Assault / Mining). Mechs walk on M14A `LimbPath` waypoints (no slide, no wheels) so a CC veteran sees the alternating-foot gait the moment a mech steps. Tracked vehicles (used by Heavy Assault's tracked-mode crawl + future M44D variants) simulate per-tread contact patches, neutral-steer pivot, track-shed-on-damage, ditched-track immobility — none of which M44C captures. Each mech is **chassis-grade** (M13): each leg is hip / thigh / shin / foot with per-zone armor; chassis-mounted weapons socket into shoulder + hip + back hardpoints; pilot sits in cockpit anchor with M13 eject + cockpit camera. IK leg planting via `LimbPath` lands feet on the actual terrain-pixel height instead of clipping.

## Canonical ownership

Owns the **mech-walking-as-vehicle bridge**: how M14A `LimbPath` strides drive a chassis-grade mech (vs an infantry actor), IK leg planting against M14A walk_angle, chassis-mounted weapons distinct from infantry equipment, and **tracked-vehicle physics** (per-tread contact + skid steer + track-shed). Does NOT redefine: infantry walking (M14A), chassis grammar (M13), drivetrain (M44A), wheeled tire grip (M44C), turret-on-hull combat (M44D).

## Player-facing behavior

- **Light Recon Mech (T2)** — 600 steel + 100 motor + autocannon_20mm + 1× T3 battery; 4 hr. Pilot (torso cockpit); 2 legs; 2 arms (left can grip M14A infantry weapons OR mount chassis weapon). 4 m. Walk 100 px/s, sprint 180. Armor: 20 / 12 / 8 / legs 15 mm.
- **Medium Combat Mech (T3)** — 1500 steel + 150 motor + 2× hardpoint + 1× T4 battery + optional jump-jet; 10 hr. Pilot + optional gunner (independent turret-head). 2 shoulder + 1 back hardpoint. 6 m. Walk 80 px/s. Jump-jets = 3s arc per M14A jetpack. Armor: 40 / 25 / 15 / legs 30 mm.
- **Heavy Assault Mech (T4)** — 3500 steel + 400 motor + 4× hardpoint + nuclear engine; 24 hr. Pilot + commander + gunner. 2 shoulder + 2 hip + 1 back hardpoints. 9 m. Walk 50 px/s. **Tracked-mode crawl**: legs fold, belly tracks deploy, top 90 px/s, loses leg-pivot fine aim. Armor: 80 / 60 / 40 / legs 50 mm. 6 ERA slots.
- **Mining Mech (T3)** — 1200 steel + 100 motor + heavy_drill_arm + 2× cargo_bay; 8 hr. Pilot. Left arm = heavy_drill_arm, right = ore-grapple claw, back = 2× cargo bay (1 ton each). 5 m. Walk 60 px/s. **Deep-mining stance** anchors feet, drill extends, immobile.
- **Walking sim parity with M14A.** Each leg uses M14A `LimbPath` waypoints; stride alternation is the same algorithm; foot atom-group collides with terrain pixels; chassis-rotation spring targets `MECH_WALK_ROT_TARGET = 0.05 rad` (vs infantry 0.15). IK leg planting: foot lifts to clear slope and lands at the actual terrain pixel.
- **Tracked-vehicle physics is its own model.** Per-tread contact patches (left + right) replace per-tire contact. Differential track speed = turn rate. Both-reverse = neutral pivot. One-stopped = pivot around stopped track. Track-HP 0 = track shed (segment becomes debris); ditched track = immobile.
- **Chassis-mounted weapons** ≠ infantry: socket into named hardpoints (`shoulder_left/right`, `hip_left/right`, `back_center`); draw power from chassis reactor; ammo lives in chassis ammo bay; reload 4-8s autocannon, 12s missile pod (sub-mechanism, not hand-held mag swap).
- **Pilot eject is real** per M13. Otherwise pilot pops out as small M14A actor with optional parachute; cockpit decal open+smoking; mech becomes salvageable wreck.
- **Deep-mining stance** sets `LimbPath.SetFootCollisionDisabledSegment=false`, extends 32-pixel-radius dig cone per M30B `try_carve`; player cannot move.
- **Mech-on-mech brawls** use M14C HEAT + APFSDS; ERA pre-detonates per M14C; chassis weapons fire through M14 penetration ray; armor cascade per M13.
- **Reactor draw is visible.** HUD shows walk 5 kW / sprint 15 / autocannon burst 20 / jump-jet peak 40 kW. Overdraw → emergency throttle + heat-sink venting (M19).
- **Tracked-mode toggle on Heavy** is a stance transition: legs fold (joints disengage), tracks deploy, aim restricted to chassis-yaw, +1.8× top speed.

## Notes for implementer

- M14A `LimbPath` is chassis-agnostic. The work = **expose it as pub API** on `cf-actor::limb_path` + bind cf-vehicle::mech as a consumer. Don't fork — share. CCCP uses one `LimbPath` for AHuman + ACrab + ACDropShip; follow that.
- Mech ≠ infantry walking only in calibration constants: `MECH_WALK_ROT_TARGET = 0.05` (vs 0.15), `MECH_STRIDE_PERIOD_MS = 600` (vs 400), `MECH_FOOT_PUSH_FORCE_N = 8000` (vs 1200). Live in per-mech .ron, not hardcoded.
- Tracked physics is NOT M44C wheeled. Each tread = 8-12 contact patches along its length; per-patch normal + friction; differential L/R speed → yaw rate; no Ackermann. Skid-steer kinematic.
- IK leg planting reuses M14A walk_angle sampler verbatim. Novelty: target_foot_y feeds LimbPath waypoint Y per stride, not a stride-relative offset.
- Chassis hardpoints = **named slots** in mech .ron (`shoulder_left { pos, arc, accepts: [autocannon|missile_pod|energy_beam] }`). `fire_hardpoint { hardpoint_id }` resolves through this map. Do not share registry with infantry weapons.
- Heavy mech tracked-mode is an M13 stance transition with chassis-module-disengage cascade (legs inactive, tracks active). Toggling back reactivates legs via the same M13 module path. Do NOT model as two separate vehicles.
- Mining mech deep-mining stance reuses M30B `try_carve` with the chassis-mounted heavy_drill_arm tool. The 32-pixel-radius cone is a parameter on the heavy_drill_arm .ron, not hardcoded.
- Mech-pilot doctrine runs at stride scale, not tick scale: target acquisition every 0.5s, hardpoint volley every stride, retreat decision every 2 strides. Do NOT run at 60 Hz — it overwhelms M22.
- Pilot eject reuses M13's eject cascade. Only mech delta: animation plays through the cockpit-hatch decal position, not the infantry helmet-pop.
- Per-mech .ron mirrors M13 + M44D schemas — do NOT fork. Declares chassis body-graph ref, per-leg LimbPath waypoints (mirrored L/R), hardpoint slots, reactor capacity, armor zones, optional tracked-mode flag with TrackUnit refs.
