# M19N — Cooking + Food Chemistry + Per-Meal Nutrition + Food Storage + Per-Actor Preference + Brewing/Distilling

## Status

`active`

## Intent

Cooking + food layer between M17's caloric drain and M43's "food: plants + cooking + hunting." Raw ingredients can be cooked into meals at a campfire / electric stove / induction cooker; meals have per-recipe nutrition + quality + spoilage rate + per-actor preference; food storage actually preserves food; brewing / distilling produces beverages with morale + side-effects.

## Canonical ownership

Owns cooking recipes, food storage, brewing, distilling, smoking, curing, per-actor food preferences, hydration. M17 caloric resource consumer; M19F humidity producer for cooking steam; M19H morale consumer.

## Player-facing behavior

### Raw ingredients (20+ launch)

- **Plant-based:** wheat / rice / lettuce / tomato / mushroom / potato / corn / soy / spices (grown via M28D farming).
- **Meat-based:** hatch meat (M19I) / fish (M28E aquaculture) / chicken / cow / pig.
- **Liquid-based:** clean water (M19F dehumidifier) / polluted water / milk / oil.
- **Specialty:** slime (M19I) / polluted food (food-poisoning risk) / honey (beehive).

### Cooking stations (4 tiers)

- **Campfire (T0):** 1 burner; wood fuel; outdoor only; meal quality cap = Standard.
- **Stone hearth (T0+):** 2 burners; better fuel efficiency; meal quality cap = Good.
- **Electric stove (T1):** 4 burners; 2 kW; meal quality cap = Excellent.
- **Induction cooker (T2):** 6 burners + smart temperature control; 5 kW; meal quality cap = Masterwork.

### Per-actor preference

- Per-actor `food_preferences` table: "loves potatoes" / "hates fish" / "vegetarian" / "carnivore" / "ascetic".
- Preferences set at actor creation (per origin / per trait) + can shift via events.
- Eating preferred food: +morale; eating disliked food: -morale.

### Cooking skill (M32C integration)

Per M32C `Cookery` skill: higher skill → higher quality meals + lower spoilage chance + recipe permutation chance.

### Brewing + distilling

- **Brewery (T1 station):** 2-day brew time; produces beer / wine / mead.
- **Distillery (T2 station):** distill brewed alcohol → moonshine / whiskey / gin.
- Brewing skill affects yield + quality.

### Smoking + curing (preservation)

- **Smoke house (T1):** smoke meat over wood fire; 4hr; produces smoked meat with × 0.2 spoilage rate.
- **Curing rack:** salt-cure meat over 7 in-game days; × 0.1 spoilage.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Simple meal cooked at campfire
  Given campfire + 1 hatch meat + 1 lettuce
  When player triggers act.player.cook_meal with recipe=simple_meal
  Then meal_cooked fires after 5min sim time
  And output = SimpleMeal (300 cal / 50 hydration / 0 morale)

Scenario: Food spoilage in pantry
  Given simple_meal stored in pantry (room temp 22°C)
  When 8 in-game hours elapse:
    Then food_spoiled fires
    And meal moved to Spoiled state
    And eating spoiled meal triggers food-poisoning roll

Scenario: Freezer halts spoilage
  Given fine_meal stored in freezer_t2 (-20°C)
  When 48 hours elapse:
    Then spoilage = base × 0.05 (effectively halted)

Scenario: Per-actor preference
  Given actor with preference "loves potatoes"
  When actor eats potato_stew:
    Then preference_consulted fires
    And morale += +8 (preference boost)

Scenario: Polluted water sickness
  Given actor drinks polluted_water (40% sickness)
  Then 40% roll for disease M19H slimelung-equivalent

Scenario: Brewery produces beer
  Given brewery + 5 wheat + 2 hops + 5 water
  When 2 in-game days elapse:
    Then beverage_brewed fires
    And 5 beer bottles produced

Scenario: Moonshine addiction
  Given actor drinks moonshine 3 days in a row:
    Then addiction trait acquired
    And actor seeks moonshine when stressed

Scenario: Cookery skill boosts quality
  Given actor cookery_skill=15
  When fine_meal cooked:
    Then meal quality rolled per M32C skill 10-14 table
    And 20% Excellent / 5% Masterwork chance

Scenario: Salt-curing preserves meat
  Given raw meat in curing_box for 7 days
  When time elapses:
    Then preservation_applied fires
    And meat transformed → cured_meat (× 0.1 spoilage)
```
