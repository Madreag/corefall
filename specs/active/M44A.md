# M44A — Electric Ground Vehicles

## Status

`active`

## Intent

4 battery-powered ground vehicle SKUs (Electric ATV / Mining Hauler / Cargo Skid / Forklift) — closes the M44 "vehicles are fuel-only" gap with the Stationeers-style "drive electric ATV to deposit, hot-swap battery at base" loop.

## Player-facing behavior

- Build an **Electric ATV** at T2 fabricator (200 steel + 50 motor + 1× T3 battery; 4 hr); drive from base → field op → return; range ≈ 4 hours of mixed driving on full T3 battery.
- Build a **Mining Hauler** at T3 fabricator (500 steel + 80 motor + 2× T3 battery + 1× T4 battery; 8 hr); carries 2 tons ore + 5 spare battery slots; range ≈ 6 hours.
- Build a **Cargo Skid / Forklift** at T1 fabricator (50 steel + 10 motor + 1× T2 battery; 1 hr); pure base-loading vehicle; range ≈ 2 hours (battery economy not the bottleneck — it just moves cargo).
- Hot-swap vehicle batteries at the M28C vehicle bay (4-8 s per battery; uses base hot-swap station inventory).
- Cold-weather penalty (per M29 cold curve) reduces vehicle battery capacity ~40% at -20°C; player learns to budget for Mimas/Europa.
- Vehicle HUD shows per-circuit battery state (drive battery / sensor battery / cargo-sub-circuit), time-to-empty, charge rate (when at base).
- Heavy cargo = slower acceleration; same M14A mass-aggregation curve scaled to vehicles.

## Vehicle locked datasheet (canonical launch)

| Vehicle | Mass (kg) | Cargo (kg) | Drive battery | Range (full) | Top speed (px/s) | Cold penalty | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Electric ATV (T1) | 800 | 200 | 1× T3 (20 kWh) | 4 hours mixed | 240 | -40% at -20°C | Driver + 1 passenger; light suspension; vac-rated |
| Mining Hauler (T2) | 2400 | 2000 (ore) | 2× T3 + 1× T4 (140 kWh) | 6 hours mixed | 160 | -40% at -20°C | Driver + 0 passenger; 5 spare battery slots; armored cabin |
| Cargo Skid (T0) | 400 | 500 (crates) | 1× T2 (5 kWh) | 2 hours | 120 | -25% at -20°C | Slow base-loader; manual driver OR forklift_doctrine |
| Forklift (T1) | 600 | 300 (1 crate) | 1× T2 (5 kWh) | 2 hours | 100 | -25% at -20°C | AI auto-load capable; M22 path-aware |

**Per-circuit sub-batteries** (vehicle electrical layer):
- **drive_battery_slot**: main motor; the bulk of capacity
- **sensor_battery_slot**: headlights / radio / IFF / autopilot; ~5% of capacity; isolated to prevent main drain
- **cargo_circuit**: powers refrigerated / sealed / armed cargo crates; pulls from drive_battery when active

## Out of scope

- Hovercraft / antigrav vehicles (M48+ exotic).
- Multi-vehicle convoy AI doctrines (M22 base; M48+ for full convoy logistics).
- Vehicle-mounted weapons (T-mech chassis grammar from M13 owns mech weapons; ground-vehicle weapons = M44B+).
- Underwater vehicles (M44 owns submarine; M44A is ground only).
- Aerial drones (M13 drone allies owns small drones; large UAVs = M48+).

## Notes for the implementer

- Vehicle is NOT a chassis (M13 chassis = mech); it's a separate `cf-vehicle::Vehicle` struct that wraps actor occupancy + drive + cargo. Reuse M13 module grammar selectively (armor zones, sensors).
- Per-circuit battery isolation prevents pathological drains (turning on headlights doesn't drain the drive battery). Sensor circuit pulls from sensor_battery; cargo circuit pulls from drive_battery when active.
- Vehicle hot-swap is 4-8 seconds (longer than actor hot-swap because vehicle batteries are physically larger). T3 = 6s, T4 = 8s.
- Forklift auto-load doctrine uses standard M22 pathfinding with `prefer_loading_dock_path` cost modifier; identical pattern to M28C forklift_doctrine.
- Cold-weather penalty reuses M29's `effective_capacity_pct` curve from M29 lerp(60%, 100%, ...).
- Determinism: every per-tick vehicle state (position / velocity / battery) is recorded in run-bundle; replay byte-identical across same-seed runs.

### cf-vehicle write-merge convention (cross-M44 family)

`cf-vehicle/src/lib.rs` is the canonical entry crate for **every** M44 sub-spec (M44A-H). To avoid 8 specs colliding on a single 1000-line `lib.rs`, each downstream M44 spec MUST create its own per-spec submodule rather than appending into `lib.rs` directly:

- M44A → `cf-vehicle/src/m44a.rs` (this spec — electric ground, `Vehicle` struct + `electric` + `cargo_routing`)
- M44B → `cf-vehicle/src/m44b.rs` (cargo drones — but most M44B logic lives in `cf-drone` per its spec)
- M44C → `cf-vehicle/src/m44c.rs` (engine / wheeled physics extensions)
- M44D → `cf-vehicle/src/m44d.rs` (combat ground: combat / turret / crew_slot / ammo_rack submodules)
- M44E → `cf-vehicle/src/m44e.rs` (mech / tracked / ik_leg_planting / chassis_hardpoint submodules)
- M44F → `cf-vehicle/src/m44f.rs` (watercraft / submarine / buoyancy submodules)
- M44G → `cf-vehicle/src/m44g.rs` (helicopter / rotor / ground_effect / cargo_sling / pilot_skill submodules)
- M44H → `cf-vehicle/src/m44h.rs` (aircraft / vtol / recon_drone / ejection_seat / autopilot / air_refuel / carrier_landing submodules)

`cf-vehicle/src/lib.rs` is **append-only** across this family. Each downstream spec adds exactly **one** new line:

```rust
pub mod m44b;
pub mod m44c;
// ... etc
```

Per-spec submodule then re-exports its leaf modules (`pub mod helicopter;` etc.) so external crates can `use cf_vehicle::m44g::helicopter::*;`. This prevents merge conflicts when M44A-H ship in any order and keeps blame clean. Common shared traits (`Vehicle`, `VehicleId`, `Driving` stance plumbing) stay at the crate root in `lib.rs` and are NOT touched after M44A authors them.
